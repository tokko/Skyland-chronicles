# Voice Profile

**Status: placeholder.** Populated by the orchestrator's voice-extraction pass over `source/manuscript.*` before any scene is written.

The narrative voice the scene writers inhabit. Distilled from the source manuscript. Polished to suppress the bad habits in `polish-targets.md` but otherwise preserved.

## Narrative distance

How close to the POV character does the camera sit. Stable across the book or shifts. Free indirect vs. tight third vs. distant.

## Sentence rhythm

Distribution. Where do long sentences land. What earns a fragment. How do sentences open (subject-first, prepositional, participial, conjunction).

## Cadence

How paragraphs build and resolve. Where rhythm carries the reader and where it brakes for emphasis.

## Lexicon

Words and metaphor domains the author reaches for. Words to keep. Words the source overuses (move to polish-targets.md).

## Punctuation habits

Em dash, semicolon, comma splice, italics, ellipsis. What's earned, what's filler.

## Recurring imagery

Motifs that travel across scenes (wind, fall, scaffolding, breath, ...). Useful as connective tissue.

## Voice anchors

Three to five paragraphs from the source manuscript that exemplify the voice at its best. Each annotated with what makes it work.

## Voice anti-anchors

Two to three paragraphs from the source manuscript that show the bad habits (repetition, staccato, minutiae fixation) the rewrite must suppress. Annotated with what's wrong and how to recast.

## Sample reframings

For each polish target, a before/after pair showing the source voice cleaned up without losing identity.
