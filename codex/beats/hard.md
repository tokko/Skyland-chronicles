# Hard Beats

The spine. Every beat listed here **must** be reached, on-page, in logical and naturally-motivated fashion. Hard beats may arrive earlier or later than originally outlined. They may not be skipped, off-screened, summarized, or downgraded.

This file is auto-included in every turn so the orchestrator always knows what's still owed to the story.

**Status: empty until manuscript is ingested and beats extracted.** After extraction, the user approves the hard/soft split before any scene is written.

## Format

Each beat:

```
### <BEAT-ID> — <Short title>
- **Book / planned location**: book-N / around chapter MM
- **Why hard**: the structural reason it cannot be cut
- **Preconditions**: what must already have happened
- **Description**: what occurs, including the felt shift in the POV character
- **Status**: pending | landed (scene: <book-N/ch-MM/scene-PP>)
```
