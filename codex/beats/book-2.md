## What's leapt at the open

Weeks-to-months between the Outcast raid (Book 1 close) and the start of Book 2's first scene. Kyra is now riding with the Bizhjerai by choice rather than under guard. The killing of Raynard (source ch.17) may sit at the head of Book 2 as the closing-the-door-on-the-Skylands beat, or it may be retained as Book 1's terminal beat — the orchestrator decides during chapter outlining. Either way, by Book 2 ch.1 Raynard is dead and his Outcast alliance is done.

## What's leapt at the close

A short interval (a season or so) between the establishment of the Utopeia colony on the coast and the first summer of raids that opens Book 3. Trade has happened; the perimeter has hardened; the family has resettled at Dun Traelak. Book 3 opens on the marketplace reunion (source ch.41) as the new equilibrium — Marissa is already there, Kendall has already defected, the colony has already arrived and is already pressing against Clan territory.

## Notes for the writer

- **Chapter-range shift acknowledged.** H-the-first-skylander-incursion sources to ch.40–42 per `codex/beats/hard.md`. We are landing it in Book 2 (which ends at ch.40) rather than Book 3 (which begins at ch.41) because the colony's *founding* — the arrival, the lumber-cutting, the first trade negotiations — falls almost entirely in ch.36–40. The marketplace reunion at ch.41 belongs as Book 3's opening equilibrium image, not as Book 2's closing beat. This is a clean ±1 shift; the colony is "founded" in Book 2; the colony is "consequential" in Book 3.
- Kyra's voice has shifted by this book. She is no longer the wary captive; she is integrating, then partnered, then mother to a dragon, then reunited with the child from the Skylands. The voice's diagnostic register softens around Anjali and Malkareth and re-hardens around the colonial threat. The cadence-shift is character work, not author drift.
- H-kyra-and-red is the trilogy's first major sex scene that the reader is meant to root for. Polish-targets §15 (post-battle sex as default) applies here in reverse: this is the one Kyra-sex scene that is allowed to land with full weight because the relationship is the point. Suppress the rest of the source's post-fight sex pattern across this book; reserve it for this scene and one or two callbacks.
- H-grasax-the-liche spans multiple source chapters and includes Kyra's first half-Archoning, the loss of her left arm, and Anjali's near-fatal gut wound on Grasax's staff. The body-trauma of Anjali surviving this beat is the load-bearing setup for her death by stray bullet later — the reader must know that wounds like this can be survived, so that the casual mortality of the bullet in Book 3 lands without warning.
- H-malkareth-hatches is the source's most-loved-by-the-narrator beat. Resist sentimentalising the dragon. He is a child, a weapon, a brother to Marissa, and a finite resource of power; he is not a pet. The voice carries this if the dragon is given dialogue rather than coos.
- The Skylander reunion (S-bringing-marissa-home) happens off-page in the locked split: Book 3 ch.41 is when Marissa arrives. Book 2 closes on Kyra knowing they are coming, not on them having arrived. This preserves Book 3's opening image (the marketplace reunion) as a hook rather than a callback. Document carefully which reunion scenes are deferred.
- Apply polish-targets §1 (suddenly / of course / smirk / chuckled): Kyra's domestic chapters are where the source's reflexive tics cluster densest. Trim aggressively.
- AI-ism watch: H-the-first-skylander-incursion is the book's longest exposition opportunity. Suppress info-dump (§5 / §8). Show the colony through what Kyra *sees* on her dragon-fly patrols, not through what she summarises about Skylander politics.
- Working title: *Partnership* is plain on purpose. The book is about Kyra learning that a marriage and a child and a dragon-bond and a sworn alliance with a foreign Clan are all the same thing under different terms. A more lyrical title would over-promise.
