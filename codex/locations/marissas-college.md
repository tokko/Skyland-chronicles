# Marissa's College of Magic

## Description

The premier Mage academy in the known world. Built by Marissa over ~30 years on the site of the former Groundlands, in the valley she had cleared of Wild Magic and savage infestation. About ten thousand residents in book 3 — 2,000-odd students, a couple of hundred teachers, plus support staff.

Architecture: deep-purple materialised-magic crystal in the Skylander style, with multi-storey housing buildings, a manufactorium district in the west, and the keep (where Marissa's workshop and the senior teachers live) in the east. Surrounded by a high outer wall guarded by trained students and staff; outer "northern walls" where deliveries and visitors arrive.

Admissions: selective. Fewer than 1 in 100 applicants are admitted; fewer than half last a single season. Every student takes an oath of magical binding to serve the College primarily and never to use power to harm or restrict others. The College has a manufactorium for its own gear, a power-distribution network from artificial ley lines, and a hangar for Archangel armour by book 3 ch.7.

## Geography

In the same valley that once held the Groundlands. Mountains north (where the savages came from) and east; cliffs along both flanks of the valley; the valley mouth (formerly the Groundlands gatehouse approach) defended by switchback paths. The College itself occupies most of the valley floor.

## Inhabitants

Headmistress Alana (running daily ops); senior teachers (none named on-page in source); ~2,000 students at any time; senior students and graduate staff; Marissa herself, when on-site. Barengar joins as Marissa's apprentice in book-3 ch.2.

## Function in the story

Hub of book 3. Source of Marissa's continuing influence on the world. Both the political-pressure target (Council of the Cities, Qoch) and the launching point for the Epitome project (which Marissa primarily develops at the off-site workshop, but tests and teaches at the College).

## Notes

- The College's true mission, in Marissa's view, is to produce Mages who will gradually replace her — she expects "many of my students would in time surpass me in both skill and power" (book 3 ch.3).
- A separate **primary workshop** sits above the former Skylands site (in the airspace where the Skylands used to float) — Marissa does her most sensitive work there, not at the College. See `codex/locations/marissas-primary-workshop.md`.
