# Marissa's Primary Workshop

## Description

A floating crystal structure built by Marissa in the same airspace where the Skylands monolith once stood. The Great Geyser of magic still flows from the ocean floor at this site — the most powerful natural fount in the world — and Marissa's workshop hovers in its updraft, drawing power directly from it. Protected by a permanent magical shield that keeps caustic Wild-Magic currents at bay; the shield has a controllable hole that allows Marissa's Archangels to enter and exit.

The structure is roughly modelled on Skylander architecture (deep-purple materialised crystal) but on a much smaller scale than the original Skylands — it is one building, not a city. Inside it has:

- A landing platform / hangar for Archangels (with several spare units pre-staged).
- A spell-finder chamber lined floor to ceiling with thousands of meta-magic spells cycling through hypothetical rune-configurations; each spell discovers a new combination and prints its schematic on crystal paper.
- A printing room where the schematics are output and triaged.
- A massive control nexus for the meta-magic spell-finder array — a self-modifying spell-framework occupying the entire lower level.
- A bunker (separate, below the workshop) containing pre-Apocalypse electronic machines (computers and possibly a server farm) — the source of Marissa's knowledge of GPS, satellites, geosynchronous orbits, ancient histories of communism/capitalism, and the rocket-engine principles she used for the orbital project. Marissa got these working by feeding them lightning.
- An elevator down to the lower levels and the Epitome substrate / Reliquary of Souls.
- Living quarters for Marissa and (from book 3 ch.5) Barengar.

## Geography

Floats above the Great Geyser, the column of magic that once held the Skylands aloft. In open ocean, far from any populated land — Marissa chose the location deliberately for both magic abundance and isolation.

## Inhabitants

Marissa; Barengar (from book 3 ch.5); Epitome's main substrate and various avatars (one per support function). No other humans.

## Function in the story

Where the Archangel armour was developed; where Epitome was built and where her primary substrate resides; where the orbital stations were launched from; where Marissa dies; where Epitome transmits her backup to orbit; where the workshop's power core overloads and destroys both the workshop and Epitome's primary form.

## Notes

- The workshop is the trilogy's most magic-dense location after the Great Geyser itself.
- Its location is one of Marissa's deepest secrets — only Epitome and (from book 3 ch.5) Barengar know it exists.
- The bunker with pre-Apocalypse machines is technically a separate sub-location; it predates Marissa and was found by her during book-2 → book-3 gap years. It is the source of every "modern" innovation Marissa makes in book 3.
