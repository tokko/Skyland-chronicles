# Lahar

## Description

The largest of the Cities — the political and cultural capital of the Union of Cities. A walled city of white stone with shimmering towers and buttresses; smaller than Dun Traelak by book 3 but more grandly built. Houses the Council of the Cities, the palace, and Marissa's principal political opposition.

In book-1 the Cities were a vague reference — "weeks away by horse" from Dun Traelak and from the Bizhjerai plains. Book-1 ch.36-area implies the Cities had developed industrial steam tech, contact with the Fists of Heaven (mercenary steamship fleet), and pre-existing trade networks. By book 3 the Union of Cities is a treaty-bound political bloc that Marissa has refused to join — she trades with them but does not sign the treaty.

The palace at Lahar has marble courtyards, a Council chamber, and a guard corps of plate-armoured soldiers carrying bolt-action rifles. The Council is "Council of the Cities" with multiple member-cities represented.

## Geography

In the hills south-west of the Bizhjerai plains. Several days' ride from Dun Traelak by horse; under an hour by Archangel (book 3 ch.3 — Marissa makes the trip in roughly 15 minutes after a Dun Traelak detour).

## Inhabitants

Council of the Cities (book 3, named "Council"); Commander Ola (palace guard commander, named book 3 ch.3); the unnamed Qoch of Mukhtar (visiting southern noble, killed by Marissa in ch.3); various nobles, traders, scholars. A young man with "piercing blue eyes, shoulder length brown hair and white robes... almost entirely covered in silver thread embroidery" serves as Council aide (book 3 ch.3-4).

## Function in the story

A book-3-specific political pressure point. Marissa's standing outside the Union is becoming costly; her killing of the Qoch of Mukhtar is the diplomatic incident that opens the Ca'el war thread.

## Notes

- The Union of Cities and the Bizhjerai are politically separate; the Bizhjerai do not appear in the Council.
- The Mukhtar (the Qoch's southern people) are presumably tributary to or aligned with the Ca'el — which is one of the reasons the Qoch presumes he can command Marissa.
