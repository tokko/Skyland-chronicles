# The Elweyr Forest

## Description

A vast temperate forest on the mainland — the first land the Pioneer expedition lands at after leaving the Skylands. Buffers between the coast and the Bizhjerai plains. Home to wildlife, the occasional Outcast camp, and Grasax's lesser spell-beasts on its eastern fringes.

The Skylanders cut down its thinnest stretch for Utopeia's lumber and infrastructure (book 1 ch.36). After Utopeia's fall, Skylander survivors fled into the forest depths — book 1 ch.51 references "thousands had survived the battle for Utopeia. Scattered into the Elweyr forest." Some of those survivors eventually consolidated under Micah and founded the Groundlands.

The Prowler (Skylander steam-Skyship) crashed into the Elweyr forest in book 2 ch.26 after its lift gas and engines were destroyed. The burning wreckage may have started a regional forest fire.

## Geography

Lies between the Bizhjerai plains (east) and the coast (west). Thickest along the coastline; thinnest where it meets the plains; in the southern reaches it borders the Cities. Crossable on horseback in days.

## Inhabitants

Wildlife (bears, elk, smaller predators). Sparse Bizhjerai hunters at its eastern edge. Skylander loggers and military outposts at its western coast in book 1 and book 2. Surviving Skylander refugees from Utopeia, then Groundlands precursors. Outcast bands occasionally pass through.

## Notes

- "Elweyr" is consistently spelled with one Y in the source; the codex preserves that.
- The forest is the trilogy's main neutral wilderness — it is where things start (Pioneer crash, ch.13) and where most things end up disposing themselves (Utopeia survivors, Prowler wreck).
