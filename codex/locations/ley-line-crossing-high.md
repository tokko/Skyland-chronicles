# High-Altitude Ley-Line Crossing

## Description

A location above the maximum Skymmer altitude — inaccessible by any aircraft, reachable only through a ley-line crossing spell. Marissa uses it to manifest her meetings with Kyra's spirit in a richer magic field. No physical description provided; characterised entirely by the dense magic available there.

## Geography

Above the Skymmer's absolute maximum altitude (which is calculated relative to ocean level, not ground level). The focusing tower erected north of the Groundlands was too high in altitude for the Skymmer to reach the crossing near it.

## Function in the story

The space where Marissa has private conversations with Kyra. Provides Kyra enough power to manifest at length. Also used as a ley-line nexus for the artificial ley lines.
