# The Undercity (Skylands)

## Description

The lower levels of the Skylands monolith — caves and tunnels carved or grown beneath the upper-surface city. Dimly lit by sparsely placed glow globes with long dark sections between them. Most of the fungus farms that fed the city were down here; most of the poor lived in residential tunnels above the farm levels.

Distinct sub-levels:

- **Upper Undercity** — residential; tunnel apartments, fungus markets, tram stations.
- **Lower Undercity** — fungus farms, illicit markets, brothels (e.g. Mama June's), Stardust manufacturing sites, gang territories.
- **The Bowels** — deepest tunnels; worst territory; where bodies and the most desperate disappear.

The Skyway tram station connected the Undercity to the Surface and to the wider city; the trams ran underneath and out along the magic stream that supported the city.

The Undercity flooded immediately when the Skylands fell into the ocean (book-1 ch.51): "Water flooded the Undercity and air rushed out." Most Undercity residents had no warning.

## Geography

Tunnels and caves carved beneath the Skylands surface. Multi-level. Connected to the Surface by the Skyway tram and a few stairs.

## Inhabitants (book 1)

The poor, the criminal, the desperate, the fungus farmers, and most of the city's mercenaries. Greg and Marissa lived in a "fairly safe area" of the Upper Undercity. Mama June's brothel (where Kyra worked briefly in book-1 ch.3 for a cover-identity assignment) is in the Lower Undercity. Stardust manufacturing sites and Robin Hood gang operations are throughout. Detective Ford's unsanctioned anti-Stardust raids targeted these sites.

## Function in the story

The base layer of book-1's worldbuilding. Where Kyra is from; where Greg lives; where the Stardust drug trade operates; where Marissa is born. Drowned in book-1 ch.51.
