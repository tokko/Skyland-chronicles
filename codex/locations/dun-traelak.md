# Dun Traelak

## Description

Originally an ancient fortress on the plains east of the Elweyr forest, held for centuries by the liche Grasax. Sits atop a natural ley-line fount — one of the most powerful in the region (only the Great Geyser of the Skylands was greater). A central shield tower at the fortress's heart housed the generator that maintained Grasax's permanent magical barrier around the outer walls. The fortress had thick stone-and-crystal walls, narrow inter-housing alleys, a powder armoury, and storage of cannonballs and muskets — Grasax's library was rich in pre-Apocalypse magical knowledge.

Captured by Clan Bizhjerai + Clan Ithlak in book-1 ch.21-30 with Kyra's lead. Adopted as the Bizhjerai-Ithlaki main hold after Grasax's defeat. Adapted by Kyra and Kendall for ley-line transmission (the fount above the shield tower was rebuilt to feed and receive long-distance ley lines from the Bizhjerai plains). The artificial-ley-line network that supplies the Bizhjerai war effort in book 1 and the Bizhjerai industrial base in books 2-3 is anchored at Dun Traelak.

In book 1 the original fortress was small — about the size of a fortified keep. By book 3 ch.3 it has become a massive city: bigger than Lahar (the capital of the Cities), "an amalgamation of stone, wood and crystal" that "stretched far beyond what had been the Skylander encampment during the siege" — combining Bizhjerai stone-and-wood construction with Skylander crystal architecture.

## Geography

On the plains, east of the Elweyr forest, in Clan Bizhjerai territory. North of the Cities; south of the Bizhjerai winter holds. Surrounded by open ground that historically held Grasax's spell-beast preserve.

## Inhabitants

In book 1 (post-capture): Clan Bizhjerai garrison and citizens; the new Bizhjerai industry around the fount; Kyra's family (Kyra's mother, Marissa, Mal); Kendall and his crew after the Second Expedition reunion. Through book 2 it has become the political and industrial centre of the Unified Clans. By book 3 it is a major city — population in the tens of thousands at least — with Marie as Chief of Chiefs running it from elsewhere (Lahar by book 3 ch.3, where she resides at the palace).

## Function in the story

Marissa's home base and emotional reference point. Founded in fire (book 1), raised her (post-book 1), launched the Groundlands invasion (book 2), and remains the cradle of the trilogy. She returns briefly in book 2 ch.28 only to exile herself again. The shield tower fount is where she stood when she committed to the epilogue decision.

In book 3 it is referenced more than visited — Marissa flies past it on her way to Lahar in ch.3 and considers landing to visit Kendall but cannot spare the time.

## Notes

- The Skylands laid siege to Dun Traelak in book 1 ch.48 — that siege was broken by Bizhjerai resistance plus the arrival of the Red Flight dragons. Five Greater Dragons and ~5000 Drakonids supported the defence; three Drakonids in five died.
- Marissa refers to the fount as "the key to winning the war with the Skylands."
- The city's guard post at the inner (formerly outer) wall gate gave Marissa a "curious look" but did not challenge her when she left in book 2 ch.28.
- Kendall lives here permanently from book 1 ch.37 onward, including in book 3 with his husband Aurean and adopted children.
- The "ancient fortress" character is preserved — Kyra notes in book-1 ch.20 that the fortress is "not large but it is very well fortified." By book 3 the city has grown around the old fortress in concentric rings.
