# Dun Traelak

## Description

A major Bizhjerai fortified city — once a fortress that grew into a city after the war. Contains a shield tower with an active ley-line fount at its core — the same fount where Malkareth (Marissa's dragon-brother) hatched. The shield tower is large enough to stand inside and stare into the fount. The outer wall was formerly the inner wall before the city expanded.

## Geography

On the plains — Marissa rides her bike from the inner wall gate out to open plains and then "screams down" toward the Groundlands. The journey from Dun Traelak to the Groundlands is a "long ride."

## Inhabitants

The Bizhjerai Clan. Kendall and Aurean live here (or use it as base). Marissa spent approximately a decade here being raised after Utopeia.

## Function in the story

Marissa's home base and emotional reference point. She leaves it in chapter 1 for the mission and returns briefly in chapter 28 only to exile herself again. The shield tower fount is where she stood when she committed to the epilogue decision.
