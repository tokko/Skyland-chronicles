# The Groundlands

## Description

A valley settlement built from materialised magic — crystal brick houses, walls, towers, and workshops grown out of the earth rather than quarried or cut. Surrounded by cliffs on east and west sides with a northern wall fortified against savage incursions. At peak population: ~11,000 Skylanders. Smells of construction dust, magic, cook fires, and by chapter 24-25 the stench of 30,000 decomposing savage corpses.

The central shield tower was both the nexus for the main dome shield and the receiver for all artificial ley lines — the single-point vulnerability Marissa exploited. At the end: a crater where the tower stood, Wild Magic spilling out, buildings levelled by broadside cannon fire, rubble and corpses throughout.

## Geography

Located in a deep valley between tall cliffs (eastern and western cliff faces each carry defensive gun nests and anti-air positions). The north is accessible only through a gap that leads toward the mountains and the savage territories. The south faces open ground where the Bizhjerai fleet assembled. A dam and water filtration plant sit inside the wall. The valley mouth to the south was the approach Marissa used in chapter 1.

Travel: approximately two days on foot to the first northern focusing tower; faster on bikes. The high-altitude ley-line crossing was inaccessible by Skymmer.

## Inhabitants

Skylander survivors of Utopeia and subsequent recruits — approximately 11,000 at peak. Key figures: Micah (commander), Mathias Varl (senior Mage), Archibald (lead architect), Ms Vorka (housing coordinator), Arthur Swanson (lumber foreman).

## Function in the story

The central stage for most of the manuscript — the place Marissa infiltrates, works within, grows to care for (partially), and then destroys. It represents the Skylanders' second attempt at civilisation; its destruction is the fulfillment of Marissa's mission and the source of her moral crisis.
