# The Bizhjerai Plains

## Description

Vast grasslands east of the Elweyr forest and west of the mountain ranges. Home to the nomadic Bizhjerai, Ithlaki, Kriath, Azwail and other Clans. Rich grazing for the buffalo herds that are the Clans' primary subsistence economy. Open enough that horses are the favoured transport; sparse trees and shrubs.

Geographically the Bizhjerai Plains are where book 1's middle act takes place — Kyra's wilderness trek with Alissa, her capture, her time as a prisoner-guest at the Bizhjerai camp, the war with Clan Kriath, and the journey to and from Dun Traelak all happen on the Plains.

By books 2-3 the Plains are dotted with Bizhjerai-Skylander hybrid settlements, supplementary ley-line fount sites, manufactories, and travel routes between Dun Traelak and the Cities, between Dun Traelak and Lahar, and between Dun Traelak and the Ca'el lands.

## Geography

A wide swath of grass and rolling terrain. Eastward terrain transitions into hills and then mountains; westward into the Elweyr forest. The Cities are south.

## Inhabitants

Clan Bizhjerai (the protagonist Clan in books 1-3), Clan Ithlak (allied), Clan Kriath (defeated in book 1), Clan Azwail (mentioned book 1 ch.47 as adjacent territory), Outcasts and Clanless (book 1; mostly absorbed by Grasax's fall). Buffalo and other livestock herd through the seasons.
