# Utopeia

## Description

The first Skylander settlement on the mainland — established in the Elweyr forest after the Skylands launched the Second Expedition. Powered by an artificial ley line stretched all the way from the Great Geyser of the Skylands. Grew rapidly: in book-1 ch.49 Marissa describes "a hundred thousand Skylanders" crossing over in the months leading up to the Bizhjerai assault. The city had spires, walls of crystal, manufactories, a shield. Destroyed by the Bizhjerai (and the Red Flight dragons) in book-1 ch.49 with massive loss of life; Kyra's soul-rage killed thousands more single-handedly after Anjali's death.

## Geography

Coastal site at the thinnest stretch of the Elweyr forest. The artificial ley line ran from there along a coastline arc back to the Skylands, supported by floating relay stations. The Bizhjerai mustered fifty thousand Clansmen for the siege; the city held about a hundred thousand Skylanders, the bulk of them unarmed civilians.

## Inhabitants

Skylander colonists and military. About 30,000 trained warriors among the 100,000 population. Most fighters were militia or mercenaries with single-shot muskets — only a few special-forces troops had bolt-action rifles. After the destruction, fewer than a thousand survivors fled into the Elweyr forest; Micah eventually gathered some of them into the group that would become the Groundlands.

## Function in the story

The structural inflection point of book 1 (Anjali dies here) and the originating wound of book 2 (Marissa's vengeance mission). The massacre at Utopeia is why Kendall orders no survivors at the Groundlands in book 2 — he wants no third Skylander resurgence.

## Notes

- **Resolved ambiguity (a)**: Anjali died at Utopeia (the breach), NOT at Dun Traelak. Book 1 ch.49 makes this unambiguous. Earlier codex-staging flagged this as a probable drafting error in book 2 ch.27 — but book 2 ch.27 was correct; the error was in the codex's identification of "Anjali's death location" as Dun Traelak.
- **Resolved ambiguity (f)**: Aurean's reference to "Marie wounded at the breach at Utopeia" is the same Marie who is Chief of Chiefs in books 2-3 — she was wounded in the hip at Utopeia just before Anjali was killed; she survived. Book 1 ch.49 ("I felt Marie go down, wounded in the hip. She would live.") + book 3 ch.18 confirmation.
- The Prowler (Skyship) escaped Utopeia; Kendall wanted to prevent a repeat.
- Marissa's adoptive mother Anjali died here. Marissa's biological mother died years earlier on the Skylands (off-page; not at Utopeia).
