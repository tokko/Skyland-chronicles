# Utopeia

## Description

The first Skylander settlement after the destruction of the Skylands — the attempt by Skylander survivors to rebuild on the ground. It was attacked and destroyed by the Bizhjerai. Less than one in a hundred of its inhabitants survived. Marissa's birth mother died here; Alissa took in Marissa after the destruction.

## Geography

Unspecified location. Pre-dates the Groundlands by several years. The Prowler escaped the destruction of Utopeia — hence Kendall's remark that this time they would reduce it to wreckage.

## Inhabitants

Skylander survivors. Micah organised the survivors of Utopeia into the group that eventually formed the Groundlands.

## Function in the story

Historical wound and moral reference point. The Bizhjerai destroyed it; the survivors rebuilt as the Groundlands. The massacre at Utopeia is why Kendall orders no survivors at the Groundlands.
