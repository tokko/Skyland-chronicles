# The Ca'el Empire

## Description

A southern Mage-aristocratic empire that emerged over the ~30 years between the destruction of the Groundlands and book 3. Effectively led from the shadows by Zandor (Marissa's former lover, who survived the Groundlands and fled south); ruled in name by a hereditary Mage aristocracy that recruits and trains Mages aggressively. Tributary peoples include the Mukhtar (whose Qoch tried to demand training from Marissa in book-3 ch.3).

The empire's capital is a fortified Mage-city — never named in the source, only "the Ca'el capital." The Ca'el field a professional army with bolt-action rifles and trained Mages, organised in centuries; their officer corps is mostly Mage-trained. They fight a war of attrition against the Unified Clans across the southern frontier through book 3.

## Geography

South of the Bizhjerai plains, south of the Cities, in lands the trilogy does not map in detail. The capital and the front-line trenches are the only on-page Ca'el locations.

## Inhabitants

Mage aristocracy at the top; tributary peoples below; a professional army. Zandor and his surviving Skylander cohort form the magical core of the leadership. The empire is described in book-3 ch.14 as Skylander-derived in its magical technology — a sign that Zandor's surviving Mages founded its institutional knowledge.

## Notes

- The Ca'el is the principal book-3 antagonist nation.
- Its leadership-shadow (Zandor) is one of book 3's late-act reveals.
- The Mukhtar are likely-tributary: their Qoch's overconfidence in book-3 ch.3 suggests he believed he could lean on Ca'el backing.
