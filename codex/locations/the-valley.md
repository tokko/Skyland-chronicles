# The Valley

## Description

A deep valley between tall eastern and western cliff faces — the geographic container for the Groundlands. The cliffs on both sides were used for defensive gun nests and anti-air positions. The northern end narrows toward the mountains where the savages lived. The southern end opens to plains.

Covered in forest and rough terrain outside the cleared settlement area. North of the Groundlands: forested slopes, rocky mountain approaches, cave systems used by the savages. The focusing towers were placed progressively north along the valley floor.

## Geography

Travel times: approximately two days on foot from the Groundlands to the first focusing tower north; faster on the magic-powered bikes. The mountain caves were accessible from the northern end; a cliff overlook there had a view "breathtaking" at sunrise with the sun painting the valley red.

## Inhabitants

Skylanders (in the Groundlands, destroyed ch27). Savages in the mountain cave system to the north. Wild Magic spreading from the tower crater.

## Function in the story

The primary physical stage. Its cliffs provide the defensive structure of the Groundlands; the north provides danger and mystery (savages, egg chambers). After the battle it becomes a Wild Magic zone.
