# The Skylands

## Description

The original Skylander homeland — floating islands or elevated terrain, existing far above the ground ("Outside"). The Skylanders were a colonial civilisation on these elevated territories who expanded their reach downward and outward. Destroyed by Kyra Zorantis (The Red Terror) in her final act, which also killed her.

## Geography

Above the "Groundlands" level — possibly literally floating. The Skymmers' altitude cap is described as relative to the ocean level, which explains why the high-altitude ley-line crossings were inaccessible by Skymmer.

## Inhabitants

Former: Skylander society. Hierarchical, militaristic, colonial. Marissa was born here as the daughter of a Skylander man and woman.

## Function in the story

The origin point for the conflict. Everything in the manuscript is aftermath — the Skylands are gone before chapter 1. The Prowler (chapter 27) is described as "the last artifact of the Skylands" when it is destroyed.
