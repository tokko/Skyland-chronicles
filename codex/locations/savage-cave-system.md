# Savage Cave System

## Description

An extensive cave network in the mountains north of the Groundlands. The main feature: a large atrium chamber with wooden walkways lining the walls, hundreds of head-sized rocks embedded in carved nests packed with wood shavings — later revealed to be savage eggs. The atrium floor is open; the walkways form a ramp structure. The cave tunnels are narrow (two people shoulder-to-shoulder) with sharp inclines and forks. The atrium was lit only by Marissa's light spell.

By the time the group departed: the ramp structure was burning from Marissa's fire attacks; multiple egg chambers had been smashed; savages were keening over broken shells; smoke was thick throughout.

## Geography

North of the Groundlands, in the mountains. Approach: through forested slopes. The main tunnel from the cave entrance to the atrium: accessible. Beyond the atrium: branching tunnels with steep inclines leading to an overlook on a cliff face, where the savages herded the group.

## Inhabitants

Savages — thousands, with hundreds of eggs. The savages use the caves as a breeding ground and stronghold.

## Function in the story

The site of the group's most costly battle. Where Valia and Felaan died. Reveals the scale of the savage threat: not hundreds but potentially tens of thousands once eggs hatch.

## Notes

- The wooden walkways were a clue to savage intelligence and culture — they maintain and tend the egg chambers carefully.
- The savages showed they could distinguish between the eggs and attackers and reacted with horror to egg damage.
- Kendall in chapter 28 believes the savages are a pre-Apocalypse magical experiment — humans modified to subsist on magic instead of food.
