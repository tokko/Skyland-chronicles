# Skymmer

## Rules

A flying vehicle designed by Kendall — it uses telekinesis spells to remain airborne and provide thrust. Not personal armour (like Angel armour) but a vehicle. Carries multiple passengers.

A key quirk: speed decreases proportionally with altitude. At a certain altitude the Skymmer is completely stationary. This absolute maximum altitude is measured relative to the ocean level, not the surface below — so a high valley (like the northern valley) has less Skymmer range than a lower plain.

## Cost

Magical power to run the telekinesis spells. Continuous drain during flight.

## Limits

Cannot reach high-altitude ley-line crossings (above ocean-relative maximum altitude). Cannot hover at maximum altitude — stationary.

## Practitioners

Kendall invented it. The Bizhjerai had a prototype that Varl spied on and reverse-engineered for the Groundlands. Marissa helped Varl's team perfect their reverse-engineered version reluctantly.

## Visible signature

A flying vehicle with no visible propulsion mechanism (telekinesis is invisible). Presumably has a physical hull.
