# Blood Magic

## Rules

Blood Magic converts the practitioner's own body mass — muscle, fat, physical substance — into magical power. It can be used to enhance physical strength, speed, and endurance beyond normal limits. Marissa uses it to lift heavy objects, sprint faster, and sustain exertion she would otherwise be unable to perform.

## Cost

Visible physical degradation. Marissa notes she can count her own ribs by touch; her breast and hip volume have reduced to half; her hourglass figure is gone. Extended use results in a body that is visibly smaller and more fragile. It also delivers pain from the conversion process, which Marissa suppresses to continue using it.

## Limits

Cannot replace rest. Cannot be used indefinitely. The practitioner will die if they convert too much body mass. Does not provide any offensive magical capability on its own — it fuels physical action, not spells.

## Practitioners

Marissa Zorantis (primary practitioner shown). Probably known to other Construction Mages but not shown used by others.

## Visible signature

No distinctive visible signature — its effects are visible on the practitioner's body over time, not as an aura during use.
