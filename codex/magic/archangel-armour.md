# Archangel Armour

## What it is

A book-3 successor to the Angel armour: more vehicle than armour. Twice Marissa's height; several times her width. A walking, flying machine with a humanoid skeleton, hollow belly into which the pilot is enclosed via stirrups-and-clamps, sensor-spells generating an illusion of the outside on the helmet interior, intent-control through a magical interface, internal Angel-armour bail-out kit if the Archangel is destroyed.

Marissa took inspiration from a former student's propeller-driven flying machine (which used forward momentum to deform air into lift) but threw away the propeller for magical-jet propulsion. The Archangel reaches velocities a Skyship cannot match — Marissa makes the Dun Traelak-to-Lahar run in a few minutes in book 3 ch.3.

## How it works

- **Magical-jet propulsion** at the rear, with secondary jets for hover and manoeuvring. Multiple jets per limb.
- **Intent-control interface** — the pilot's thoughts translate into machine movement via a magical control spell.
- **Master-slave coupling** — two Archangels can be coupled, with one controlling both (Marissa flies Barengar's slaved to hers when he is new to the system).
- **Bail-out Angel** — internal Angel armour can be deployed if the Archangel fails.
- **Sensor-illusion helmet** — interior of the helmet shows a perfect illusion of the outside.

## Constraints

- Power-hungry. Requires a substantial internal core; even more if hovering for extended periods. Marissa is wary about deploying these without first solving "the power distribution issue."
- Intent-control communications travel via artificial ley line — and anyone who can splice that ley line could in theory hijack the Archangel. Marissa has not solved this in book 3 and limits production to prototypes.
- The "noise" of the magical jets is significantly louder than the Angel's.

## Who uses it

Marissa and Barengar in book 3 are the only known operators. The Bizhjerai military and the Cities have not been given the design.
