# Meta-Magic

## Rules

A spell that Marissa is developing throughout the manuscript — described as "cycling through the endless array of potential spells." It is a higher-order magical discipline: magic that works on other magic, or possibly magic that can generate or modify spells dynamically. Details are deliberately kept vague in the manuscript (Marissa avoids explaining it to anyone).

The meta-magic spell is powered by the artificial ley line in her workshop. By chapter 23 she has made significant progress on it; she alternates between working on it and grieving.

## Cost

Heavy power requirement — draws from the artificial ley line. The effort of maintaining it is significant.

## Limits

Not fully described. Marissa has not yet deployed it as a weapon or tool by end of manuscript. It remains a work-in-progress.

## Practitioners

Marissa Zorantis (only practitioner seen).

## Visible signature

Not described.

- It was Valia's idea (implied or encouraged by her), which is why working on it makes Marissa feel connected to Valia after her death.
- Its eventual application is left open — possibly relevant to future books.
