# Meta-Magic

## Rules

A higher-order magical discipline: spells that operate on other spells. Built by Marissa over decades from the seed Valia gave her in book 2 ch.21-23. Three main applications across the trilogy:

- **Spell-finder**: a meta-magic spell that generates other spells iteratively, testing each in simulation. Marissa's primary workshop holds thousands of these running in parallel; over decades they discover a vast library of new spells (or new methods to known spells). The book 3 ch.5 reveal: "Every so often she delivered new spells to the college to be taught to the students" — these are the meta-magic spell-finder's outputs.

- **Stacked-meta-magic for fine work**: a meta-magic spell that casts another meta-magic spell at smaller scale, which casts another at smaller scale, until one is fine enough to inscribe runes onto materials too fine to enchant by hand (gold and silver wires too thin to see). Book 3 ch.3 — the Archangel's bulb-mounted enchantments use this technique; Marissa cannot see the runes she inscribed.

- **Compressed-template combat array**: fifteen crystals embedded in Marissa's skull, each containing a highly complex meta-magical spell loaded with hundreds-to-thousands of overlapping spell templates. In combat she designates targets with marker-spells; the crystals select optimal kill-spells per target and execute them in parallel, drawing power from her artificial ley line rather than her own reserves. Book 3 ch.3 demonstrates this — she kills thirty mounted soldiers in seconds.

## Cost

- The spell-finder runs continuously and consumes the bulk of the workshop's power supply (the Great Geyser).
- Stacked-meta-magic for fine work requires careful staging; a single high-level invocation is feasible if the underlying ley-line supply is adequate.
- The combat array bypasses Marissa's own personal capacity — she draws from the artificial ley line, allowing her to cast spells far beyond what her body alone could channel.

## Limits

- The combat-array crystals must be physically embedded — they cannot be moved. Marissa cannot remove them without surgery.
- The spell-finder needs both raw power and time; it has been running for decades and is still finding new combinations.
- Meta-magic spells are inherently complex; "no human can cast them quickly" — but spells, including meta-magic spells, can cast more meta-magic spells, which is how the recursive depth is achieved.

## Practitioners

- Marissa Zorantis (primary practitioner; the field is essentially her invention).
- Epitome (uses meta-magic at vast scale; she is herself a meta-magic construct).
- Some advanced College students learn meta-magic basics; none reach Marissa's level.

## Visible signature

Crystals embedded in Marissa's skull glow when active. Spell-finder spells fill her workshop chamber with thousands of simultaneously glowing rune-cycles, "from roof to ceiling".

## Notes

- Valia's idea was the seed — Marissa makes this explicit in book 3 ("a variant of the spell-inventing spell that I had created in my youth after Valia had given me key insights").
- The orbital stations are Marissa's plan to give meta-magic global power distribution; Epitome is her plan for the moral arbiter.
- After Marissa's death, meta-magic essentially resides with Epitome alone — though some of its outputs (spells already delivered to the College) survive in the curriculum.
