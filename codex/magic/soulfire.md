# Soulfire

## Rules

A fire-based magical attack — crimson in Kyra's case ("an angry crimson spell dripped embers between her fingers" in chapter 26). Generates actual fire that can burn material. Unlike purely physical fire, soulfire is powered by magical energy and can be extinguished by the savages' magic-drain aura when the source power is drained — but any fire it has already ignited continues to burn normally.

## Cost

Magical power proportional to intensity and duration.

## Limits

Savages can drain the magical component. However, once physical combustion begins, the savages cannot reverse it — only the magical ignition source is consumed, not the resulting physical fire.

## Practitioners

Kyra Zorantis (crimson signature). Marissa (used fire attacks throughout). Kendall (lightning preferred, but fire possible for any Construction Mage).

## Visible signature

Crimson for Kyra. Orange/yellow for Marissa's fire attacks (not distinctively coloured in the manuscript). The colour distinction may indicate personal magical signature.
