# Bizhjerai Rune

## Rules

A tattoo-form magical marking carried by Bizhjerai Clan members. When activated by channelling power into it, it provides a significant boost to physical strength and endurance — filling the user with "strength and vigour." It also serves as a visible identity marker for Bizhjerai recognition.

## Cost

Magical power to activate. Duration and intensity scale with available power.

## Limits

Requires power to activate. Not a substitute for skill — it augments what the practitioner can already do. It is visible when blazing — in chapter 26 Marissa deliberately makes it blaze to identify herself to the Bizhjerai fleet during the aerial melee.

## Practitioners

All Bizhjerai Clan members carry it. Marissa carries it (born Skylander but raised Bizhjerai). Alissa created a disguise tattoo overlay to hide it while Marissa was undercover in the Groundlands.

## Visible signature

A tattoo on the left shoulder. When activated it blazes with visible light. The disguise tattoos Alissa applied faded over time after Marissa's cover was blown, requiring no maintenance by end of manuscript.
