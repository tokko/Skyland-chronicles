# Artificial Ley Lines

## Rules

Channels of magical power constructed between two points by a Construction Mage. They redirect and supply magic from a fount or one ley line node to another location. The Groundlands' entire power infrastructure depended on Marissa's artificial ley line network, with focusing towers as the physical receivers and distributors.

The central shield tower served as both the nexus for all artificial ley lines AND the primary shield generator — a single-point-of-failure design (Kyra and Marissa's birth mother made the same error in the original Skylands design).

After the nexus tower is destroyed, the artificial ley lines continue feeding power into the resulting crater, generating Wild Magic rather than controlled power.

## Cost

Significant initial construction cost (Construction Mage power and materials). Once established, they draw from the source and deliver continuously without further practitioner involvement.

## Limits

If the receiving end is destroyed, power spills freely. Knowledge of how to open ley-line crossings was Marissa's key operational security — Varl's Mages could not replicate it.

## Practitioners

Marissa Zorantis (designer and builder). Varl's Mages could maintain and partially operate them once built.

## Visible signature

Described as overhead streams of light visible above the settlement.
