# Liche Enchantment

## Rules

A ritual that binds a person's spirit to a physical object (in this case, the gem in Kyra's dagger) after death. The spirit can manifest in the physical world when sufficient magical energy is available in the local field. The spirit appears fully physical and can interact with the world — hold objects, fight, embrace — but is immaterial to mundane weapons. The spirit can expend stored power to maintain presence; it draws power from dense magic fields and can recharge at founts.

The spirit retains personality, memory, and emotional capacity fully intact. The spirit cannot travel beyond a limited distance from the binding object without the object being present.

## Cost

Performed by a skilled practitioner (Alissa crafted Kyra's binding). To cast it requires significant expertise in spirit-binding — it is not common magic. The bound spirit consumes magical energy to manifest; without a power source it cannot appear.

## Limits

Range from the physical object limits. Magic field density determines duration and energy of manifestation. The spirit can be released voluntarily by the holder of the object — Marissa could release Kyra to "join mom in the void" but declines in chapter 28.

Cannot be cast retroactively — must be planned before death.

## Practitioners

Alissa (created Kyra's binding). Grasax (the dragon) was previously bound or imprisoned in crystal and released — possibly a related or precursor form of binding.

## Visible signature

The gem in the dagger glows faintly. When Kyra manifests, her crystal arm, violet eyes, and scars are visible — she appears as she was in life. In very dense magic fields she maintains presence longer and can appear further from the dagger.
