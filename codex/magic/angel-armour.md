# Angel Armour

## Rules

A Construction Mage creation: a suit of materialised magic that forms around the wearer. It includes "ethereal wings" — magical engine systems that provide thrust, lift, and attitude control. The wings can also generate shields. The armour provides impact protection (cracked corset absorbed a spear thrust in ch22). The main engine provides primary thrust; individual jets on each side provide manoeuvring.

The armour is controlled through the wearer's choker — a physical interface piece at the throat. The connection is mental; losing it causes physical pain and loss of limb control (when Valia wrested the armour's control in ch22, Marissa's left arm went slack).

## Cost

Magical power drawn from the wearer's choker/crystals and from the environment. Under sustained high-thrust use (prolonged flight in combat), power depletes rapidly. The armour can be "cycled up" from dormant to active quickly — Marissa manifests it around her naked body in chapter 25.

## Limits

Cannot carry significantly more than the wearer's weight without severe performance degradation. Cannot function if the choker's connection is severed. Individual wing jets can be fired independently for manoeuvring — this is tactically useful but risky in tight spaces.

The savages can drain the active magical power — a wearer cannot enter a savage's area of effect freely without the armour rapidly failing.

## Practitioners

Marissa Zorantis. Kendall (a compact Angel engine/wing pack kept on his bridge). The Bizhjerai Valkyries (squad-trained for aerial combat). The Skylander Angel Corps (their own variant).

## Visible signature

Materialises as a visible suit around the wearer, with visible wing structures. Engine fire is described as a "shriek" — audible. Wing-tip jets produce sound and visible flame. The armour corset is crystal-based and physically present (cracked in ch22).
