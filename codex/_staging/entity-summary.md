## Resolution status of the six previously-flagged continuity ambiguities

**(a) Anjali's death location (Utopeia vs Dun Traelak)** — **RESOLVED**: Anjali died at the breach of Utopeia. Book 1 ch.49 is explicit. The book-2 codex's flag of this as "probably a drafting error needing correction to Dun Traelak" was itself wrong — book 2 ch.27 was correctly remembering the event. **The drafting error was elsewhere**: book 2's text says Marissa's "birth mother" died at Utopeia; the correct statement is that Marissa's **adoptive mother Anjali** died at Utopeia. Marissa's biological mother (unnamed in source) died years earlier on the Skylands.

**(b) Marissa's age at the fall of Utopeia** — **RESOLVED**: Approximately 12-13 years old. Book 1 establishes Marissa as 11 in book-1 ch.5 (the year of the Dragonkin invasion); approximately a year passes before Kyra's relocation to Dun Traelak and a further year-and-a-half before Anjali's death at Utopeia. Marissa is therefore about 13 at book-1 ch.49 → book-1 ch.51. Book-3 ch.4 confirms approximately mid-fifties by book 3, set ~35 years after the Groundlands fell, which itself is ~10-15 years after Utopeia — putting Marissa at ~24 in book 2 ch.1 and ~25 by book 2 ch.28. Recommended ages for codex character writing: ~24-25 in book 2; ~55 in book 3.

**(c) "Micah burned at Utopeia"** — **PARTIALLY RESOLVED, LEANING METONYMIC**: Book 1 ch.49 describes the burning of Utopeia and Kyra's mass kill — many Skylanders burned. Micah is not named on-page during the assault. His subsequent burn-scar on jaw/neck (book 2 references) is consistent with partial survival of fire-burning. The most defensible reading is: Micah was personally burned (he was at Utopeia) but not killed; his forces were also burned. The codex can confidently treat him as a personal survivor of the Utopeia burning.

**(d) Varl's death location (shield tower vs the Prowler)** — **STILL AMBIGUOUS**: Books 1 and 3 do not address this. Varl is Kendall's old teacher per the existing codex; he is referenced in book 3 only in passing. The book-2 ambiguity stands.

**(e) "Mom" vs Anjali vs Marissa's birth mother three-parent structure** — **RESOLVED**: Marissa had two adoptive mothers (Kyra and Anjali) and a biological mother (Greg's unnamed wife, dead before book 1). Kyra is "mother", Anjali is "mom". Marissa's biological mother is not Anjali. The book-2 codex's wording — "her biological mother ('mom'; Kyra's partner; the Red Terror's partner)" — conflated Anjali (Kyra's wife, the Red Terror) with Marissa's biological mother. Book 1 makes the structure unambiguous: Greg's wife (biological mother, dead before book 1) → Greg (biological father, killed in Dragonkin invasion book-1 ch.4) → Kyra and Anjali (adoptive parents, both at Dun Traelak through Marissa's childhood). Book 3 ch.5 echoes this: "If he had not died that night then I would most likely not have been adopted by the legendary Dragonbane. I would not have moved with her to the Outside. I would never have had mom, Malkareth or grandma, Uncle Kenny, Auntie Alissa, Marie or any of the others."

**(f) "Marie" in book 2 ch.27** — **RESOLVED**: This is Marie the Bizhjerai warrior-then-Chief, who survived a hip wound at Utopeia (book 1 ch.49 — "I felt Marie go down, wounded in the hip. She would live."). Aurean's reference in book 2 ch.27 is to the same Marie. She is alive throughout book 3 (~70 years old by then) and is Chief of Chiefs of the Unified Clans. She is NOT Marissa's birth mother, NOT a separate-Bizhjerai-matriarch beyond the named role, and NOT a Marissa-variant.

## NEW continuity ambiguities introduced by books 1 and 3

1. **The book-2 codex's "Niklas Zorantis" as Marissa's father** — There is no Niklas in any source. Marissa's biological father is Greg (book 1 ch.2-4). The "Niklas Zorantis" line in the previous book-2 codex was an extrapolation/error and has been corrected.

2. **The Red Terror flagship's namesake** — Book-2 ch.26 explicitly says the flagship is named after "mom's nickname during the war"; that means Anjali (the Red Terror). The book-2 codex previously had this as "named after Kyra Zorantis (The Red Terror)" — that was wrong. Corrected to: named after Anjali.

3. **The Dragonkin / spell-beast soul-rebirth question** — Book 1 ch.39 establishes that dragons are spell-beasts with a soul-containment enchantment that can re-form a body after death. Malkareth dies in book 1 ch.51 — the source treats this as final death. But the rule it established earlier suggests Mal's pattern could in principle re-form. The source does not address this. Flag as: **canonical decision needed if any rewrite wants to use this hook** (recommended: treat as final death; the source treats it as final).

4. **Zandor's exact post-book-3-ch.15 location** — Marissa pursued him; he escaped; the source does not say where. Flag as: **STILL AMBIGUOUS** (open hook).

5. **Whether Wylah survived the Groundlands collapse** — Book-2 codex flagged her as "unknown"; book 3 makes no reference to her. Flag as: **STILL AMBIGUOUS** (she is presumed dead by Marissa in book 3 narration).

6. **The pre-Apocalypse civilisation's identity** — Book 3 makes much of pre-Apocalypse machines (Marissa learns "GPS, satellites, geosynchronous orbits, communism, capitalism" from them). The source implies we are a post-Apocalypse Earth, but never confirms whether "the Apocalypse" was specifically the rise of Wild Magic from the unrestricted ley-line tapping that the Mages of old did. The book-3 explanation in ch.3 ("The Mages of old had had no knowledge of Wild Magic, and so in their quest for liberation they ushered in the doom of their society") essentially confirms this. Flag as: **NEWLY CLARIFIED, NOT AMBIGUOUS**.

7. **Marissa's daughter** — Book 3 ch.18 has Epitome describe "I watched in horror as Barengar struck Marissa down" and "She had been my daughter, and now she was dead." Epitome considers herself Marissa's daughter via Reliquary integration. Marissa has no biological daughter on-page. Flag as: **NOT AMBIGUOUS** — Epitome is Marissa's "daughter" in the sense of a created entity she nurtured into existence.

8. **The Brengar vs. Barengar question** — Book-2's "Brengar" (gate-guard at the Groundlands, killed by Alissa) and book-3's "Barengar" (Marissa's apprentice) are **distinct characters** with different spellings. The names are similar; the people are not. Resolved.
