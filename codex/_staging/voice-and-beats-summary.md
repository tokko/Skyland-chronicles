# Voice and Beats — Extraction Summary

## Voice in five bullets

- **First person, very close, very honest.** Marissa narrates as she calculates — every observed person and surface is judged, every choice is second-guessed on the page, the contradictions are exposed live (admiring Zandor and remembering she has come to kill him in the same paragraph).
- **The default sentence is medium-to-long, comma-spliced or "and"-strung; short sentences and fragments are the punchline.** Long sentences live in process, exposition and engineering thought; short sentences live in killing, grief and reversal.
- **Bawdy, profane, and engineering-precise at the same time.** The same narrator who can describe the topology of a meta-magic spell ("a three-dimensional squiggly line of power channels") will also note "fucked, and not the good kind." Both registers are voice — neither is to be sanitised.
- **The world's signature material is crystal and the world's signature sensation is pressure.** Materialised magic surfaces "tinkle"; founts "geyser"; the body "drinks" power; the air "thins" when magic is spent. Keep this fluid-and-glass palette.
- **Mother-tongue and mercenary register sit on top of a small private lexicon: "mother", "mom", "Now and forever", "Cursed be her name", "There are no innocent Skylanders".** These phrases are the emotional anchors and the rewrite must use them deliberately — not as filler.

## Source strengths (rewrite priorities to preserve)

1. **The voice is genuinely distinctive.** First-person POV with an engineer's precision, a mercenary's profanity, and a 23-year-old's libido is a rare combination. The book is at its best in the kill-scene (ch.1), the cliff-edge sacrifice (ch.22), the box (ch.20), and the post-genocide silence (ch.27). These passages should anchor the rewrite's tone.
2. **The magic system is load-bearing, internally consistent, and engineering-real.** Materialised-magic substrates, ley-line founts, focusing/receiver towers, manufactoria, spell-stones, blood magic, meta-magic, the savages' draining aura — the cosmology is a working machine and supports both industrial-scale set pieces (the wall, the dam, the gun manufactoria) and intimate scenes (the dagger-as-vessel). The world rewards the reader for noticing.
3. **The moral architecture is honest.** Marissa does an unambiguously evil thing for reasons the book takes seriously. The shade-of-mother debates, the killing of Alissa, the shooting of Zandor, the "I'm the last Skylander" line — the book never lets her off the hook and refuses to pretend the genocide is righteous. The thematic spine ("there are no innocent Skylanders" interrogated to "I have become one") is hard, clean, and earns its bleakness.

## Source weaknesses (rewrite priorities to fix)

1. **Pacing collapses in the middle.** Chapter 12 ("Construction Mage", 13.5k words) is largely Marissa lecturing Valia about magic theory and Marissa thinking-out-loud about her workshop. Chapters 11, 12, 13, 17, 23 all stall on the same shape (Marissa explains, friend nods, magic happens). The rewrite must redistribute the magic-system reveals into scenes that already have plot work, and cut at least 30% of the lecture-mode prose.
2. **Voice has tic-density problems.** Over-frequency of "suddenly", "of course", "indeed", "I sighed/shrugged/nodded", reflexive height-jokes, "the big man" as Zandor epithet, "fucked, and not the good kind" repeated as punchline. These are character-true in moderation; at source-density they read as AI-slop adjacent (per `codex/style/ai-isms.md` §§1, 4). Catalogued in `polish-targets.md` §1.
3. **The mother-shade has a single scene-shape that the source runs eight times.** Marissa hits a wall → shade appears → shade comforts and absolves → shade fades. Same beats, same emotional arc, same closing line ("now and forever"). The intervention degrades from rare and mythic to a recurring confessional booth. The rewrite must vary the shade's function: sometimes she should be wrong, sometimes she should sting, sometimes she should ask Marissa something the reader does not yet know.

## Concerns for the user (tiering questions)

These are calls the orchestrator wants the user to sign off on before the per-book beat lists are produced:

1. **The mother-shade appearances.** Source has eight. I have proposed four as hard (`H-mother-shade-first-appears`, `H-uncle-kenny-tries-to-take-her-home` which is also a shade scene, `H-the-dagger-is-the-vessel`, and the farewell at the epilogue) and four as soft. Does the user want all eight kept (and just reshaped) or are we genuinely cutting half of them as I propose?
2. **The intent-vs-outcome parable (ch.14).** I marked this `S-mother-on-intent-vs-outcome` as soft, but it is the trilogy's most explicit articulation of its central ethical claim. If the user wants the rewrite to lean philosophical, promote to hard.
3. **Alissa's arrival (`S-alissa-arrives-in-disguise`).** I left soft because the function (rune-disguise + covert second) could be split or recombined. But Alissa is hard-tier as a role (`H-meet-alissa-as-shadow-ally`) and the user may want her arrival promoted to hard for emotional weight.
4. **Zandor surviving.** Source leaves him as a missing bike — ambiguous, hopeful, deliberately untreated. Book 3 (Marissa in the ruins) gains enormously if Zandor returns, but that is a major plot promotion. Does the user want a hard beat planted now committing to his return, or do we keep it as ambiguity?
5. **The savages-as-pre-Apocalyptic-engineered-humans reveal.** Marked hard because it is the book-3 hook. But it is delivered in two paragraphs of epilogue dialogue in the source — easy to miss, no on-page setup. The rewrite will need to set it up earlier (Kendall's library, or Marissa noticing the egg chamber's craftsmanship). User confirmation that this is the trilogy's outer frame would lock the book-3 spine.
6. **The polyamorous Marissa/Valia/Zandor triangle.** Marked soft because the specific shape is reshapeable, but the emotional payload of ch.22 (Valia falling, knowing who Marissa is) only lands if the triangle is genuinely a triangle, not a love-triangle with one favourite. If the user wants the trilogy to lean into the polyamory as theme (Bizhjerai vs Skylander structures of family), promote `S-zandor-and-valia-and-marissa` to hard.
7. **The genocide-as-title.** Chapter 27 is named "Genocide" in source and the book does not flinch. The rewrite's market position depends on whether this is kept at the same intensity or softened. I have classified the genocide beats hard (the bombardment, the methodical sweep, the "I'm the last Skylander" line) on the assumption that the rewrite keeps the source's commitment. If the user wants a less brutal climax, multiple hard beats need re-classification.
