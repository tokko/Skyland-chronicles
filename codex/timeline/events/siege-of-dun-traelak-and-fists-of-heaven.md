# Siege of Dun Traelak; Anjali's Father Dies; Anjali Becomes Chief

## Summary

In a winter siege the Skylanders — supported by the Skyship Hand of Glory and her companion vessels — brought ten thousand troops to Dun Traelak's gates. The siege dragged on for months, food running short. The southern City states sent the Fists of Heaven (four coal-powered Skyships under Commodore Raistlin) to relieve the siege in exchange for future trading rights. The Skylanders cut three of the Clans' artificial ley lines as the siege began. The shields fell. The Hand of Glory bombarded the keep. Skylander Valkyries breached the walls in places. The defenders held, then nearly broke. Anjali's father — the Chief — sacrificed himself holding the wall breach so the surviving warriors could fall back to the inner wall. Anjali succeeded her father as Chief of Clan Bizhjerai. The Fists of Heaven engaged the Skyships and one Fist was destroyed. The siege ended with the surviving Skylanders fleeing back to the coastal colony. The defenders kept the fortress at the cost of one Fist and hundreds of Bizhjerai dead.

## Participants

- **The Bizhjerai Chief** (Anjali's father): leads the wall defence at the breach; killed.
- **Anjali**: succeeds her father; succeeds him as "the Red Terror."
- **Kyra**: leads the response.
- **Kendall**: builds anti-air cannons; routes the shield-tower spell to a new underground storage to protect it from sabotage; kills two would-be saboteurs from his own honour-guard.
- **Commodore Raistlin / the Fists of Heaven**: city-state mercenary fleet; one Fist lost.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Dun Traelak survives. Anjali becomes Chief.
- **Long-term**: Anjali's father's death darkens Anjali ("the Red Terror" nickname spreads). The Clans recognise the Skylanders as an existential threat and commit to total war.
