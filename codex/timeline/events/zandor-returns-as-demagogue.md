# Zandor Returns as the Demagogue of Ca'el

## Summary

Zandor — Marissa's lover from book 2, presumed dead when the Groundlands fell — survived. Severely burned in the cataclysm (half his face melted to the bone, ear gone, hair white), he made his way south to Ca'el Empire. There he became "the Demagogue," a charismatic religious and political leader whose stated mission was vengeance against Marissa and her people. He was trained in magic by Mathias Varl (Skylander Mage, presumed dead at the Battle in the Skies but having actually escaped). Zandor learned Construction Magic and Blood Magic. He took multiple young female apprentices — all young, attractive, well-endowed — bound them to himself via channelling-umbilicals, and used their souls to amplify his own magic.

In book 3 ch 8-9, Zandor's army assaulted the Bizhjerai-allied Ca'el revolutionaries. Marissa, breaking her years-long oath of academic neutrality, sortied with Kendall to defend her people. She tried to kill the Demagogue with an all-out magical attack; he survived it. They fought; he broke her right leg and crushed her hip. Kendall died defending her — Zandor stabbed him repeatedly in the throat with a dagger. Mother (Kyra-shade) saved Marissa by transferring her remaining magic to Barengar's Angel armour to escape.

## Participants

- **Zandor "the Demagogue"**: warlord; Marissa's former lover; the principal antagonist of book 3.
- **Marissa**: defeated; loses her right leg.
- **Kendall the Builder**: killed by Zandor.
- **Barengar**: present; escapes with Marissa.
- **Mother (Kyra-shade)**: intervenes magically; banished by Zandor.
- **Mathias Varl** (off-page): revealed to have trained Zandor.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa is grievously wounded. Kendall dies. The Collegium is now in open war with Ca'el.
- **Long-term**: Marissa commits fully to the war and to fusing herself into Epitome. Zandor commands the Ca'el armies against the Clans for the remainder of book 3.
