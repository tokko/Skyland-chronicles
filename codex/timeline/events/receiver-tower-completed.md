# Receiver Tower Completed / Magic Supply Online / Groundlands Named

## Summary

Marissa completes the central receiver tower — the hub that aggregates power from all the artificial ley line crossings and distributes it to the settlement. The tower also serves as the primary anchor for the main shield dome. With the magic supply online at full capacity, Micah holds a grand assembly at which he formally names the settlement "the Groundlands" and declares the founding of a new Skylander civilisation. A second ley line crossing (to a mountain to the south) is also completed around this time.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: primary architect of the tower.
- **Micah**: declares the settlement's name and founding.
- All ~initial Skylander settlers.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: The Groundlands is fully powered; formal settlement declared.
- **Long-term**: The receiver tower is the Achilles heel Marissa later identifies and destroys. Its dual-function design (shield nexus + ley line receiver) in a single structure is the fatal architectural flaw.
