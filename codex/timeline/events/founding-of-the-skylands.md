# Founding of the Skylands

## Summary

Survivors of the Apocalypse — a mixed-peoples nation — fled and eventually established the Skylands, a floating archipelago sustained by magic drawn from the Great Geyser below. The colonists adapted Grasax's shield tower to also serve as a receiver for artificial ley lines, creating the technological-magical infrastructure that defined Skylander civilisation. Over generations the founding ideals calcified into a culture of aggressive expansion and exploitation of weaker peoples.

## Participants

No named on-page participants for the founding itself. Kyra and Marissa both reference it as ancestral history.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: establishment of the Skylands as a magical floating nation.
- **Long-term**: The single-tower dependency (shield + ley line receiver in one structure) became a fatal architectural flaw — the same mistake Marissa later replicates in the Groundlands, and ultimately exploits to destroy it.
