# Bizhjerai Assault Begins / Shield Tower Destroyed

## Summary

At night, the sound of cannon fire announces the Bizhjerai fleet's arrival. The Groundlands garrison is debilitated by Alissa's poison. Marissa breaks out of the Groundlands, fights to the shield tower, kills or incapacitates the guards, and enters the tower. Zandor, who has known her identity for some time, attempts to stop her and is shot in the thigh. Inside the tower Marissa triggers the explosive cores (destroying both cliff-face munitions magazines with a simultaneous detonation), then overloads the storage crystals beneath the tower. She exits and the tower explodes — a crater replaces a fifth of the Groundlands. She also kills Micah with a heart shot and kills the Mage accompanying him. The Prowler joins the battle.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: primary agent; destroys the tower; kills Micah and accompanying Mage.
- **Zandor**: attempts to stop her; shot in the thigh; survives.
- **Micah**: killed (heart shot from Marissa's revolver).
- **Mathias Varl** (on the Prowler, or at the tower — text implies he is at the tower as a guard Mage; killed by Marissa's enchanted sword).
- **Bizhjerai fleet**: assaulting from the air.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Shield tower destroyed; main dome shield collapses; ley line network disrupted; munitions magazines exploded; Micah dead; Varl dead.
- **Long-term**: The Groundlands is now defenceless against aerial bombardment. Wild Magic begins accumulating in the crater. The Prowler and anti-air guns still function briefly until ammunition runs out.
