# Cave Battle / Egg Chambers Discovered / Valia and Felaan Die

## Summary

The party flees into the savage cave system. Inside a large atrium chamber they discover hundreds of savage eggs carefully tended in wooden-walkway nests. Marissa sets the ramp and chamber on fire to destroy the eggs and slow the savages, breaking her femur in the process. The group is separated: Felaan gives his life so Wylah can escape when Zandor falls. Marissa and Valia become cut off on a cliff overlook with no escape route. Valia — having known Marissa's true identity for months — pushes them both off the cliff, sacrificing her own power to keep Marissa airborne long enough to save herself. Valia falls into the tree canopy and dies on impact. Zandor and Wylah survive and meet Marissa at the bikes.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: broken femur; burns the egg chamber; survives by using Valia's gifted power.
- **Valia**: sacrifices herself; falls to her death.
- **Felaan**: killed giving his life for Wylah.
- **Zandor**: broken arm; survives.
- **Wylah**: uninjured; survives.
- Savages: pursuing force; eggs destroyed.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Valia and Felaan dead; Marissa injured; majority of egg cache destroyed or damaged. The surviving savages that the group did not kill re-cap many eggs.
- **Long-term**: Valia's death becomes Marissa's central grief throughout Ch 23–27. The egg destruction / magical saturation from the subsequent tower collapse causes the massive horde that attacks the Groundlands in Ch 24.

## Notes

The discovery that savages are intelligent enough to tend eggs in deliberate nests and that the "rocks" are eggs is a significant world-building moment. Valia's statement "I've known since I saw your Bizhjerai Rune" confirms she and Zandor had known Marissa's identity for some time.
