# The Skylander-Clan War; Summer of Raids

## Summary

The peace between the Clans and the Skylander colony collapsed. Skylander raiding parties harassed Clan herds and burned farms. Bizhjerai Mages-in-training (taught by Kendall and Kyra) fought back, mostly outclassed by trained Skylander Mages but holding their own thanks to dragon-borne Riders. The Reavers — Clanless honour-warriors with a Soul-draining aura, the same lineage Grasax had created centuries ago — joined the Clan side. The summer cost the Clans many trained Mages and twenty herds of buffalo, while Skylander harvests were torched and their settlers harassed.

## Participants

- **Kyra and Anjali**: lead the Bizhjerai forces.
- **Kendall**: trains the Bizhjerai Mages.
- **Malkareth**: aerial support; magic-feeder to allied Mages.
- **The Reavers**: ground assault force; many die Archoning.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Stalemate; both sides bleed.
- **Long-term**: Winter arrives. The Skylanders are reinforced from the floating city, swelling Utopeia colony's population to ~100,000.
