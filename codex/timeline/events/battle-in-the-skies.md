# Battle in the Skies / Prowler Destroyed

## Summary

The aerial battle between the Bizhjerai fleet and the Prowler (and the Groundlands' surviving anti-air guns). Marissa exposes her Bizhjerai Rune and fights against the Skylander Angels. She boards Kendall's flagship the Red Terror, fights boarders alongside Kyra's ghost, and reunites with Kendall. The Fist of Heaven (Raistlin's ship) is disabled by the Prowler but survives. Kendall uses the flagship's prow bombardment cannon to make an improbable shot and destroys the Prowler, killing most of its crew. Kyra fades after expending her power.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: fights airborne; boards flagship; reunites with Kendall.
- **Kendall**: flagship commander; destroys the Prowler.
- **Kyra** (ghost): fights aboard the flagship; expends her power and fades.
- **Aurean** (Kendall's husband): on the bridge.
- **The Prowler** (Skylander military Skyship): destroyed.
- **Fist of Heaven** (Raistlin's ship): disabled, survives.
- Skylander and Bizhjerai Angels: aerial melee.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Prowler destroyed; Fist of Heaven disabled; Skylander air power eliminated.
- **Long-term**: The Groundlands is now fully exposed to Bizhjerai bombardment. Varl may or may not have died on the Prowler. Kyra temporarily exhausted.
