# Kyra's First Expedition to the Elweyr Forest

## Summary

Kyra led or participated in an early Skylander expedition to the Elweyr forest, accompanied by Alissa Skylander (later known as Alissa Turncloak). This expedition is the origin of Kyra's connection to the forest region and to Alissa, and it pre-dates Marissa's birth. Marissa's birth mother died in the Elweyr forest during a later, related expedition or conflict — the exact overlap is not detailed in the text.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: expedition leader or senior member.
- **Alissa Skylander** ("Alana Kelvari" / Alissa Turncloak): participating Guard.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: establishes Skylander presence and intelligence about the Elweyr region.
- **Long-term**: Forms the bond between Kyra and Alissa that becomes central to the story. Alissa's eventual defection to the Bizhjerai traces back to this shared history.
