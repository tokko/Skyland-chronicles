# The Apocalypse

## Summary

A catastrophic world-ending event in the ancient past. The Skylands were founded by refugees from a pre-Apocalypse mixed-peoples country that no longer exists. Magic use was widespread and uncontrolled before the Apocalypse; in its aftermath, only scattered pockets of power remained — including the Great Geyser below what would become the Skylands. The savages of the Groundlands valley are also linked to pre-Apocalypse magical experimentation: ancient Mages created them as a magic-fed race, and the Reavers were later derived from the same base.

## Participants

No on-page participants. Referenced as historical fact by Kyra's memories and by Kendall in the epilogue.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: destruction of the old world civilisation; survivors fled and founded new peoples, including the Skylands colony and the Bizhjerai Clans.
- **Long-term**: The Great Geyser was left leaking magic beneath the Skylands. Savage "precursor" creatures survived in dormant eggs in the Groundlands valley. The magical scarcity that followed shaped all factions' relationship to power.
