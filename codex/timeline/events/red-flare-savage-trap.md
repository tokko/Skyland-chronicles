# Red Flare at the North Tower / Savage Ambush

## Summary

The northern focusing tower fires a red flare — the signal that the tower has been destroyed or jammed, not merely that the weapon has fired. Marissa, Valia, Zandor, Felaan, and Wylah ride north on bikes to investigate. They find hundreds of savage corpses around the tower and discover the ammunition caches are empty (the spell that manufactured ammunition had degraded). Before they can retreat, hundreds of living savages surround them from behind — a deliberate trap. The group flees into a cave system. This event begins the extended cave sequence of Ch 21–23.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: leader of the investigation party.
- **Valia**: companion.
- **Zandor**: companion.
- **Felaan**: companion.
- **Wylah**: companion.
- Savages: the surrounding force.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Group trapped in the caves; savage horde revealed to have circled behind them.
- **Long-term**: Cave sequence leads to Valia's death, Felaan's death, Marissa's broken femur, and the discovery of the savage egg chambers. The savage egg hatching (triggered by the magical saturation after tower destruction) produces the horde that attacks the Groundlands in Ch 24.
