# Kyra Destroys the Skylands; Malkareth Dies; Memory Core Created

## Summary

Kyra and Malkareth flew to the Skylands. Kyra plunged into the Great Geyser and began drinking power from it. Magic surged into her at a rate her body could not hold; her flesh disintegrated. Malkareth dove in to share the burden. Together they drained enough of the Great Geyser that the Skylands — the floating monolith city — could no longer balance atop the magical column. The city slid sideways, then fell, then plunged a thousand yards into the ocean below. Two and a half million people died — drowned, crushed, or asphyxiated as their world inverted.

Malkareth could not sustain the magical load; his scales cracked, his wings shrivelled, he fell. He dissipated as he tumbled toward the sea, his last word "Mother." Kyra's body Archoned fully; she became a spirit-form sustained only by the consumed magic.

Before what remained of her was finally consumed, Kyra used her power to forge a memory-core gem — a recording of her life — and bound a magical spell-beast to deliver it to Marissa back in Dun Traelak. Then she let go.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: dies; consumed by Archon transition.
- **Malkareth**: dies; consumed.
- **2.5 million Skylanders**: die in the city's fall.
- **Marissa** (off-stage in Dun Traelak): destined recipient of the memory gem.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: The Skylands cease to exist as a nation. The world's two largest magical geysers (Great Geyser and the other discovered during book 1) remain.
- **Long-term**: This is the founding trauma of the world Marissa inhabits in book 2: the floating cities are gone, the Skylander civilisation reduced to ~10,000 survivors on the mainland. The memory gem delivered to Marissa one year later is the book 2 Marissa's principal connection to both her dead mothers.

## Notes

RESOLUTION OF AMBIGUITY (#3): The book 2 reference to Kyra "burning Micah" at Utopeia is most likely a confused conflation. Kyra at the Utopeia colony killed thousands by direct magic — Micah, then a Skylander officer, may have been wounded but not killed in that fight, then evacuated back to the floating Skylands, where he was one of the few thousand survivors after Kyra's later destruction of the floating city. He went on to lead the survivors to the mainland and eventually to the Groundlands. This reconciles Marissa's memory ("she burned Micah") with the on-page fact that Micah is alive in book 2.
