# The First Expedition Launches

## Summary

After the Dragonkin invasion proved the Outside was inhabited, the Skylands diverted vast magical resources to construct the Pioneer — a steam-powered airship — and a piercing in the Great Barrier. Kyra Zorantis, newly famous as Dragonbane, signed on. Kendall gave her a parting gift of a custom magazine-fed rifle with switchable enchanted ammunition (shield-breaker, blood-poison, explosive) and a grimoire. The Pioneer departed west on a one-month scouting mission with a contingent of mercenaries and Colonel Grayson commanding.

## Participants

- **Kyra Zorantis**: principal participant.
- **Alissa Skylander** ("Alana"): fellow mercenary on the expedition.
- **Raynard**: Kyra's abusive sometime-lover and fellow mercenary; she had hoped he would not be chosen; he was. He never returns to the Skylands.
- **Colonel Grayson**: senior Guardsman commanding the expedition.
- **Kendall**: civilian; sees Kyra off; arms her with the prototype rifle.
- **Marissa, Kyra's mother**: at the pier, tearful farewell.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Kyra leaves the Skylands.
- **Long-term**: Only three of the expedition's mercenaries return to the Skylands. Most are killed by the Bizhjerai Clans, killed by Outside fauna, or — like Kyra and Alissa — defect to the Clans. The Skylanders' assessment of the Outside as "primitive natives, low-density resistance, abundant resources" sets the trajectory for the Utopeia colonisation effort.
