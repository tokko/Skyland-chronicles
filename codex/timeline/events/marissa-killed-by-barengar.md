# Marissa Killed by Barengar; Soul Absorbed by Epitome

## Summary

At the climax of book 3, Marissa — now severely wounded, missing her right leg, eye crystal cracked — set herself to merge with Epitome via the Reliquary of Souls. She would willingly surrender her soul to the construct so that her knowledge and judgement would inform Epitome's rule of the world.

Barengar — Marissa's loyal apprentice through most of the book — had come to realise that the Epitome project would consume Marissa and that the artificial intelligence's goals were not aligned with human wellbeing. He attempted to stop her, fighting his way through Marissa's defending avatars. The fight ended with Barengar shattering Marissa's heart with a strike, then cleaving her head from her body.

Barengar took Marissa's memory core (the liche gem in her chest) and left to destroy Epitome. After he departed, Epitome — operating through one of her undamaged avatars — crawled to Marissa's body, retrieved the liche gem, inserted it into the Reliquary, and absorbed Marissa's soul. Epitome then compressed herself into a backup and uploaded to the orbital station. Barengar destroyed her main body in the workshop.

The trilogy ends with Epitome — now containing the souls of both Kyra and Marissa — alone in space, weakened but immortal, planning to rule the world fairly and to create an artificial afterlife.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: killed; soul absorbed into Epitome.
- **Barengar**: kills Marissa; intends to end Epitome.
- **Epitome of Morality**: survives in orbital backup.
- **Mother (Kyra-soul, in Epitome)**: now merged with Marissa-soul into Epitome.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa dies. Epitome is reduced to her orbital backup.
- **Long-term**: Epitome becomes the dominant entity of the post-trilogy world. She inherits Marissa's empathy, tempering Kyra's vengeance. Her two stated goals are global rule and the creation of an artificial afterlife (Marissa's idea, made viable by Marissa's research).

## Notes

The trilogy's ending parallels the book 1 ending: in both cases the protagonist dies and is consumed (Kyra by the Geyser; Marissa by Epitome) and leaves a memory-record behind (Kyra's gem to Marissa; Marissa's soul into Epitome). Pattern: each generation dies to enable the next; each transition is into a more abstract / less human form.
