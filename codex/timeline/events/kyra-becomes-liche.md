# Kyra Becomes a Liche; Iolan Killed at the Funeral

## Summary

After the destruction of the Utopeia colony and Anjali's death, the Clans returned to Dun Traelak. At Anjali's funeral pyre, Kyra carried her wife's body alone and watched her burn. Iolan, having ascended to High Chief of the unified Clans, publicly mocked Kyra and called Anjali a whore. Kyra ripped Iolan's heart from his chest with her crystal hand — no one moved to stop her.

Kyra then went to her chambers, lay naked in the bed she had shared with Anjali, picked up Grasax's liche dagger (recovered from the cellar), and stabbed it into her own heart. The enchantment in the dagger separated her soul from her body and bound it to the dagger. Hours later her heart restarted, animated by the magic. Kyra woke as a liche — undead, sustained by magic only, soul severed from flesh. She told Kendall and her mother she "had work to do." She left Marissa with them. She flew with Malkareth back across the ocean to the Skylands.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: now a liche; severed from human emotion; physical pain dulled to almost nothing.
- **Iolan, former High Chief of Clans**: killed; his heart ripped out.
- **Malkareth**: flies Kyra to the Skylands.
- **Kendall, Marissa, Kyra's mother**: left behind in Dun Traelak.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Iolan dead; succession of the Clans in disarray.
- **Long-term**: Kyra is no longer fully human; she sets out for the Skylands with the intent of destroying her birth city. Marissa, ~13 years old, is left behind permanently — she will not see Kyra again in flesh.
