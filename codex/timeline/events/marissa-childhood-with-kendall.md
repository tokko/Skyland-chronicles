# Marissa's Childhood in Dun Traelak; Raised by Kendall and Alissa

## Summary

After Kyra's death (when Marissa was ~13), Marissa grew up in Dun Traelak under the care of Kendall ("Uncle Kenny") and Alissa Skylander (who had defected to the Bizhjerai and survived the fall of the Utopeia colony). Kyra's mother — Marissa's adoptive grandmother — also lived with them until her own death about seven years before book 2 ch 1. Marissa was given the Bizhjerai Mark in her own right. Alissa — the same Alissa Kyra had first met in the Elweyr forest — trained Marissa in covert operations; Kendall trained her in magic and engineering. Kendall taught her English (a pre-Apocalypse language) to allow her access to Grasax's ancient tomes.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: child, then adolescent.
- **Kendall**: raises her; teaches magic, engineering, English.
- **Alissa**: trains her in espionage and combat.
- **Kyra's mother**: lives with them until ~7 years before book 2 ch 1.

## Consequences

- **Long-term**: Marissa develops into the woman who later infiltrates the Groundlands; she is the spiritual and literal heir of Kyra and Anjali. Her Bizhjerai-engineer voice is set in this period.

The book 2 internal references to her age are consistent with that. The earlier ambiguity stemmed from conflating the destruction of the floating Skylands (where Marissa was not present, having been left in Dun Traelak) with the Utopeia colony assault (where Marissa was also not present, having stayed with Kyra's mother). Marissa was never AT either event.
