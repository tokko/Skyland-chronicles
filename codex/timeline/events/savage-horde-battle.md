# Battle of the Savage Horde at the Northern Wall

## Summary

Tens of thousands of savages — mostly newly hatched from the egg chambers, fed by the magical saturation — assault the Groundlands' northern wall. Skylander artillery, mortars, Angels dropping mortar rounds, and rifles inflict massive casualties. The savages bring crude ladders and nearly breach the wall in several places. The Groundlands holds. Approximately thirty thousand savages are killed; Skylander casualties are relatively few. Marissa participates in the defence while covertly using the opportunity to plant her explosive power cores in the unguarded munitions magazines on both cliff faces.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: defending while secretly planting bombs.
- **Zandor**: fighting on the wall.
- **Micah**: commanding overall defence.
- Skylander garrison: all available troops.
- Savage horde: ~30,000+ (most killed).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Horde defeated; ~30,000 savages killed; Skylander casualties light.
- **Long-term**: Marissa plants explosive cores in both cliff munitions magazines during the battle; this will silence the anti-air guns during the Bizhjerai assault.

## Notes

The battle is won decisively but the Groundlands does not pursue or exterminate the remaining savages. Marissa privately reflects that the savages may have "done her work for her" if they had been strong enough, but they were not.
