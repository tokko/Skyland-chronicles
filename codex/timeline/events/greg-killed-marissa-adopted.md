# Greg's Death and Marissa's First Adoption

## Summary

During the Dragonkin invasion of the Skylands, Greg — a senior mercenary, Kyra's friend, and the widowed father of the young Marissa — was incinerated by a Mage's fireball while charging the dragon Savistlin's foreleg. After Kyra woke from her four-day coma, she went to find Marissa (then ~11 years old). She brought Marissa back to her own mother's apartment, where the girl was effectively adopted into the family for the duration of the First Expedition. When Kyra later returned to the Outside permanently with Anjali's Clan, she eventually re-adopted Marissa formally during the marketplace reunion in the Groundlands.

## Participants

- **Greg**: dies in the dragon fight; widowed, leaves behind one daughter.
- **Marissa** (then ~11, formerly Greg's daughter): orphaned; sheltered first by Kyra's mother in the Skylands, later joins Kyra and Anjali in Dun Traelak.
- **Kyra**: takes responsibility for Marissa; the bond becomes the emotional anchor for both her descent into liche-hood and her final gift (the memory core).
- **Kyra's mother** (Marissa's adoptive grandmother): raises Marissa in the Skylands until Kendall brings her and her grandmother out to the Bizhjerai lands.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa loses her birth father. Kyra adopts her in spirit but leaves her with grandmother and Kendall when she joins the First Expedition.
- **Long-term**: Marissa is the principal reason Kyra returns from the Outside (briefly). Marissa becomes the inheritor of Kyra's memory gem and the Bizhjerai legacy. The death of Greg seeds the "Marissa was already orphaned once" tragedy that book 2 inherits.

## Notes

CRITICAL ENTITY NOTE for the entity sibling agent: Marissa, the protagonist of book 2, is Greg's biological daughter — NOT Anjali's nor Kyra's biological child. She is adopted twice (first by Kyra and grandmother in the Skylands after Greg's death; second by Kyra and Anjali in the Bizhjerai camp). The "three-parent" confusion in the existing codex stems from this. Anjali ("mom") is Marissa's adoptive co-mother by marriage to Kyra. Kyra ("mother") is Marissa's adoptive co-mother by adoption. Greg is her biological father. Greg's wife (Marissa's biological mother) died "years ago" before book 1 ch 1 — she is the woman who "died in the Elweyr forest" is FALSE; that detail belongs to a different ambiguity (see notes). The Elweyr-forest birth-mother story in book 2 is Marissa's own confused understanding; per book 1 the birth mother died in the Undercity in Marissa's infancy.
