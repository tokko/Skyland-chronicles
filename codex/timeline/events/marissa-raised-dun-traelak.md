# Marissa Raised in Dun Traelak by Kendall and Alissa

## Summary

After surviving Utopeia, Marissa was raised in the Bizhjerai stronghold of Dun Traelak by Kendall (her "Uncle Kendall," a major Bizhjerai leader) and Alissa (a former Skylander Guard who had defected). Her biological grandmother also lived there and died peacefully some years before Ch 1. Kendall married Aurean at some point during this period. Marissa grew up in a household of mixed Skylander-turned-Bizhjerai adults, learning magic and the art of subterfuge. This is also where she encountered the young inventor Lukas Bizhjerai, for whom she built a magical engine for his bicycle invention.

## Participants

- **Marissa** (ward / adopted child).
- **Kendall** ("Uncle Kendall"): guardian; Bizhjerai fleet commander.
- **Alissa** ("Turncloak"): guardian / mentor in subterfuge and assassination.
- **Aurean** (Kendall's husband): present in the household.
- **Grandmother**: lives and dies peacefully at Dun Traelak (~7 years before Ch 1).
- **Lukas Bizhjerai**: young inventor Marissa helps.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa develops magical, combat, and espionage skills under expert tutelage.
- **Long-term**: Her entire identity — Skylander-born, Bizhjerai-raised — is forged here. Her loyalty to Kendall and complicated feelings for Alissa originate in this period.
