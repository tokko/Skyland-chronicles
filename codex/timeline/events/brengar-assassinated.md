# Brengar Assassinated / Unknown Man Killed

## Summary

Alissa, operating in her "Alana Kelvari" cover, assassinates Brengar — the officer who led the kill-team against the farmers on Micah's orders. In the same chapter, an unknown man recognises Marissa (presumably identifying her as Kyra's daughter or a known Bizhjerai operative); Alissa kills him before he can raise the alarm. This action is effective but risks accelerating suspicion about who is operating inside the Groundlands.

## Participants

- **Alissa** ("Alana Kelvari"): assassin.
- **Brengar**: killed.
- **Unknown man**: recognises Marissa; killed by Alissa.
- **Marissa**: target of the unknown man's recognition; present.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Brengar eliminated; the immediate threat of the unknown man neutralised.
- **Long-term**: The two deaths increase Skylander security awareness; leads toward Varl's arrival and Marissa's eventual exposure (Ch 19).
