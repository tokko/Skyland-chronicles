# First Savage Encounter / Kyra's First Manifestation

## Summary

During the northern expedition, Marissa and her companions are attacked by savages at a stream. Marissa is nearly killed. During the crisis, Kyra manifests as a ghost for the first time — drawn by the dense magic field and by Marissa's proximity to death. This is the first time Marissa perceives her mother directly rather than through the dagger. The encounter establishes the savages as a genuine threat and demonstrates that the magic field is dense enough to sustain Kyra's manifestation.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: attacked; nearly killed.
- **Kyra** (ghost): first manifestation in the story.
- Savages (unnamed creatures).
- Expedition companions (named elsewhere in character codex).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa survives; first savage contact logged; Kyra manifests.
- **Long-term**: Kyra's presence as Marissa's spirit adviser becomes a recurring feature. The savages are established as the secondary antagonist force.
