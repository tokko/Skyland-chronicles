# Destruction of the Groundlands / Genocide

## Summary

After the Prowler is destroyed, the Bizhjerai Skyships methodically bombard the Groundlands. The southern wall, eastern and western cliff gun nests, and eventually the interior of the settlement are reduced to rubble over several hours. Bizhjerai ground troops with field guns and siege mortars assault from the north. House-clearing squads execute any survivors found in the ruins. Eleven thousand Skylanders — including women and children — are killed. No prisoners are taken on Kendall's explicit order. When it is over, Marissa reflects that she has committed genocide. Zandor's bike is missing from the ruins, suggesting he survived.

## Participants

- **Bizhjerai fleet and ground troops**: executors.
- **Kendall**: overall commander.
- **Marissa**: participant; kills at least one woman sniper.
- **~11,000 Skylanders**: killed.
- **Zandor**: apparently escaped (bike missing).
- **Wylah**: fate unconfirmed (not seen in the days before the assault).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: All Skylanders in the Groundlands killed; settlement destroyed; Wild Magic accumulating in the crater.
- **Long-term**: Marissa is the last Skylander. She experiences no triumph — only emptiness. The destruction becomes the moral weight she carries into the epilogue.

## Notes

The chapter title "Genocide" is the story's own word for this event. Marissa explicitly calls what she did genocide and concludes she has become Skylander through this act. Kendall's order "Take no prisoners. There will be no survivors today" is the order that triggers the mass killing.
