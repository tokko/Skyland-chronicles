# Marissa Leaves Dun Traelak / Decides to Return to Groundlands

## Summary

Months after the genocide, Marissa is in Dun Traelak standing at the shield tower's magic fount. She cannot feel at home among the Bizhjerai. She tells Kendall she is leaving. Her stated purpose: return to the Groundlands to contain the Wild Magic accumulating in the crater, investigate and potentially help the remaining savages, and atone. Kendall gives her a leather-bound tome about the savage precursor creatures (possibly also information about Reavers) and a summoning gem. She rides out alone. The story ends with her reflecting that she will try to use her skills to help people and oppose "would-be Skylanders" as "the Last Skylander."

## Participants

- **Marissa**: deciding and departing.
- **Kendall**: farewell; gives the tome and gem.
- **Kyra** (ghost): present; embraces Marissa; stays in the dagger for now.
- **Aurean**: briefly present.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa departs Dun Traelak alone.
- **Long-term**: Sets up the sequel premise — Marissa heading back into the Wild Magic zone to deal with the savage population and the magical aftermath of the Groundlands destruction.
