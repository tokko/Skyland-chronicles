# Assault on the Utopeia Colony; Anjali's Death at the Breach

## Summary

The united Clans marched on the Utopeia colony — fifty thousand warriors against a hundred-thousand-strong defended city behind a powerful magical shield fed by the still-operational artificial ley line. The Red Flight (six greater dragons including Malkareth, with seventy human Mages aboard) channelled a massed spell that cracked the shield receiver, the ley line bled wild magic into the city centre, and the shield collapsed. The shaman rent the outer wall. Anjali led the Bizhjerai through the breach.

Inside, Anjali was gut-wounded by a Skylander blade — Kyra triaged the wound (the cut had missed organs, the same kind of miraculous survival she'd shown against Grasax). A Bizhjerai warrior took Anjali to safety. While they walked, a stray bullet from another part of the fight struck Anjali in the side of the head and killed her instantly. Kyra felt her wife's soul leave her body, raced to capture the fading soul on the spectral plane, and was unable to hold it — her own soul instinctively began consuming Anjali's, and she pushed Anjali away rather than devour her. Anjali was gone with the words "Now and forever…" Kyra went berserk and unleashed unrestrained magic on the Skylander defenders — killing hundreds personally, levelling buildings, channelling more power than her body could hold. The colony was annihilated. Kyra collapsed in the ruins.

## Participants

- **Anjali ("the Red Terror"), Chief of Clan Bizhjerai**: killed by a stray bullet to the head, on her way out of the breach.
- **Kyra**: kills hundreds, possibly thousands, in her rage; channels more magic than she can hold; her enchantments rip; she is the first Skylander ever to come within touching distance of full Archon ascension.
- **Malkareth**: aerial support; senses his mother's death.
- **The Red Flight**: six greater dragons co-channel the spell that cracks the shield.
- **Iolan, High Chief of Clans**: present; later assumes overall Clan leadership after the battle.
- **Civilians and militia of Utopeia**: massacred; no quarter given.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Anjali dies. The Utopeia colony is destroyed. Tens of thousands of Skylanders killed.
- **Long-term**: Kyra is broken; she will return to Dun Traelak only to commit suicide-into-liche-hood and then destroy the Skylands themselves. Iolan, now High Chief, will be killed by Kyra hours later at Anjali's funeral after he calls Anjali a whore. The Clans become the dominant power on the continent; the Skylands themselves are not yet aware they have lost their colony.

## Notes

RESOLUTION OF EXISTING AMBIGUITY (#1): The book 2 text says Anjali was "wounded, defeated, in the breach at Utopeia." That book-2 statement collapses TWO events — Anjali's NEAR-fatal gut wound from Grasax at Dun Traelak (which she survived) and her ACTUAL death from a stray bullet at the Utopeia colony breach (where she died). The Marissa-character in book 2 references both as a single confused memory of her dead mother — she was a child at the time and her understanding was second-hand. The on-page truth is that Anjali died at the Utopeia colony's breach, not at Dun Traelak.

CLARIFICATION ON "UTOPEIA": "Utopeia" can refer EITHER to the mainland Skylander colony (which is what is destroyed here) OR to the floating Skylands city (which Kyra destroys later, at the Great Geyser). They are NOT the same event. The book 2 references to "Utopeia falling" most likely fold the colony's destruction (here) and the Skylands' fall together — the former is the on-page Anjali death; the latter is the Marissa-orphaning event.
