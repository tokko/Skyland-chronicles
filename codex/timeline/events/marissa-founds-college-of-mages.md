# Marissa Founds the College of Mages in the Groundlands

## Summary

In the decades after the destruction of the Groundlands and the genocide of the Skylander refugees, Marissa established a school of magic — the Collegium — in the same valley where the Groundlands had been. She invited Kendall ("the Builder") to co-found and serve as principal. The school grew into a city of ~10,000 inhabitants (2,000+ students, hundreds of teachers, support staff). The graduate Oath forbids students from using magic to harm others except in self-defence or the defence of others — a magically-enforced binding.

Marissa stepped back as Headmistress after the school stabilised, leaving Headmistress Alana (her co-founder) in charge of operations. Marissa retreated into her own workshop tower, equipped with a private elevator and panoramic views of the western ocean, where she pursued advanced research. She used Blood Magic to slow her aging — by book 3 ch 2 she is in her mid-fifties but looks barely older than her students.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: founder; researcher; ages slowly via Blood Magic.
- **Kendall ("the Builder")**: co-founder; teaches Construction Magic.
- **Headmistress Alana**: operations head.
- **Generations of students**: include Barengar (the book 3 Barengar POV).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: The Bizhjerai lands gain their first true Mage academy. Marissa's spells are taught to a new generation.
- **Long-term**: The college becomes the largest magical institution on the continent. Marissa's reputation eclipses Kendall's; she becomes "Lady Marissa," the most skilled Mage in the world.
