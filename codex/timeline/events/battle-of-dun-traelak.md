# Battle of Dun Traelak (Skylander Assault)

## Summary

The Skylander army marched on and besieged the Bizhjerai stronghold of Dun Traelak. The Skylanders deployed Skyships and artillery; Anjali was wounded in the breach and died of a stray shot while being escorted to the healers. Hundreds survived on the Skylander side; Micah ultimately withdrew to defend Utopeia. The siege is the crucible that forged Marissa's earliest memories of war — she was a child inside the walls during the fighting.

## Participants

- **Skylander army** (aggressors): besieging force; Micah commanding.
- **Bizhjerai Clan** (defenders): Anjali fighting; Kendall and other leaders present.
- **Anjali**: wounded in the breach; killed by a stray shot while being taken to healers.
- **Micah**: Skylander commanding general; withdraws after the siege fails.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Skylander assault fails to take Dun Traelak; hundreds of Skylander survivors retreat; Anjali killed.
- **Long-term**: Anjali's death in the breach is the proximate trigger for Kyra's destruction of Utopeia. The Prowler and the Fist of Heaven are later used to avenge this battle.
