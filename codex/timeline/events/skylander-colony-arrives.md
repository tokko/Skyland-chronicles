# The Skylander Colony Arrives; First Contact at the Coast

## Summary

Kyra and Malkareth on a routine flight spotted a large Skyship — the Hand of Glory — building a colonisation outpost on the coast. The Skylands had discovered a second magical geyser in their absence, ending magic scarcity; they had built up the new outpost as a colonisation effort to expand their resource base. Kyra returned to Dun Traelak and rode out at the head of a Bizhjerai delegation to parley. At the outpost she was reunited with Kendall (now serving in the Skylander special forces, ostensibly to find her). She learned that the colonists came in numbers and that war was likely. She introduced Anjali (her wife) and Malkareth (her dragon son) to Kendall. Kendall later defected to the Bizhjerai, bringing with him Marissa (now ~12) and Kyra's mother.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: leads the Bizhjerai delegation.
- **Anjali**: accompanies her wife.
- **Malkareth**: accompanies on the ground (running, not flying — too provocative).
- **Kendall**: revealed to be on the Skylander side; defects to the Bizhjerai.
- **Marissa** (the protagonist, then ~12): arrives shortly afterward with grandmother and Kendall.
- **Kyra's mother** (Marissa's adoptive grandmother): arrives at the marketplace.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: First Skylander–Clan trade contact. Kendall defects. Marissa joins the Bizhjerai family in the Outside.
- **Long-term**: The colonisation is the trigger for the war that consumes the rest of book 1. The Skylanders intend to expand at the Clans' expense. The fragile peace lasts barely a year before raids begin.

## Notes

Note: "Utopeia" in book 1 refers to the Skylander COLONY on the mainland that grows from the Hand of Glory outpost. The actual SKYLAND CITY — also called Utopeia in retrospect — is a separate place, the floating city itself. Book 2 uses "Utopeia" for the mainland colony and "the Skylands" for the floating original.

CLARIFICATION: This is decisive evidence that the "fall of Utopeia" event in book 2 chronology actually refers to TWO separate events: (a) the destruction of the Utopeia colony on the mainland, which happens at the end of book 1; and (b) the fall of the floating Skylands city, which Kyra causes by draining the Great Geyser. See `anjali-killed-at-utopeia.md` and `kyra-destroys-skylands.md`.
