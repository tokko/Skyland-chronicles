# Alissa Arrives Undercover as "Alana Kelvari"

## Summary

Alissa Turncloak — Marissa's mentor and Bizhjerai operative — arrives at the Groundlands undercover, using the alias "Alana Kelvari" and disguising her appearance with blue-dyed hair. She joins the settlement as an apparent recruit. Her purpose is intelligence gathering and, eventually, the direct support of Marissa's sabotage mission. She and Marissa maintain their distance publicly to protect both their covers.

## Participants

- **Alissa Turncloak** (as "Alana Kelvari"): arriving operative.
- **Marissa**: aware of Alissa's identity; maintains distance.
- **Skylander settlers**: unaware of Alissa's true identity.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Alissa is inside the Groundlands as a hidden asset.
- **Long-term**: Alissa assassinates Brengar (Ch 18); kills an unknown man who recognises Marissa (Ch 18); is captured and forced onto the stage at Marissa's trial (Ch 20) where Marissa is compelled to kill her.
