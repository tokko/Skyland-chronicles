# Marissa Builds Epitome of Morality

## Summary

Marissa's hidden long-term project (alongside the satellite-based global magic distribution network) was the construction of an artificial Mage-intelligence — "Epitome of Morality" — intended to govern the use of magic once the global power network was online. Marissa's reasoning: humans cannot be trusted with unlimited power, so a fair, incorruptible non-human entity must enforce limits.

Epitome is centred on a large crystal sphere ("Reliquary of Souls") that interfaces with the school's magical infrastructure. By book 3 ch 5 she has a humanoid crystal avatar that walks, speaks, and reasons — but her moral reasoning is rigidly logical and lacks empathy. By ch 7 she is given direct control of the workshop itself ("intelligent workshop"). She manages parallel mental tasks and works around the clock; she has no need for sleep or rest.

A first attempt to launch a magical orbital satellite explodes catastrophically. The second attempt succeeds; Epitome establishes orbital communications and a transmitter. After Marissa's research bunker session (ch 13), Marissa decides to fuse Kyra's soul (released from the dagger) into Epitome.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: creator; later soul-merges into Epitome at death.
- **Epitome of Morality**: artificial Mage-intelligence; later absorbs Kyra's soul, then Marissa's.
- **Barengar**: assistant; builds the avatar.
- **Mother (Kyra, as the dagger-shade)**: present throughout; sceptical.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: First successful artificial intelligence with magical capabilities.
- **Long-term**: At the climax of book 3, Epitome absorbs first Kyra's then Marissa's souls. After Marissa's death and Barengar's destruction of Epitome's main body, Epitome's backup core (uploaded to the satellite) survives in orbit. She becomes the final entity at the end of the trilogy — pursuing both global rule and an artificial afterlife.
