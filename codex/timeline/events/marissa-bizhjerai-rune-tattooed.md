# Marissa Tattooed with the Bizhjerai Rune

## Summary

At some point before the story's opening, Marissa received the Bizhjerai Rune tattoo on her shoulder. The Rune channels power and signals allegiance to the Bizhjerai Clan. She later had Alissa tattoo over it to disguise it when she infiltrated the Groundlands, passing as "Mary." The disguise tattoos eventually faded after her cover was blown. In Ch 26 she deliberately exposes the Rune blazing to signal her allegiance to the Bizhjerai Angels during the aerial battle.

## Participants

- **Marissa** (recipient).
- **Alissa** (applied disguise tattoos over the Rune before the mission).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa is marked as Bizhjerai.
- **Long-term**: The Rune is the detail that Valia and Zandor both use to identify Marissa's true identity. It becomes a plot-critical identifier.

## Forbidden mentions

## Notes

The tattoo is separately discussed for Kyra too — Uralnor tattooed Kyra after the Grasax duel. The text implies it is the same type of Rune, suggesting the Bizhjerai Rune is a formal initiation mark.
