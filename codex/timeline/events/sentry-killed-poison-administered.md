# Sentry Killed / Poison Administered to Water Supply

## Summary

The night after the savage horde battle, Marissa slips out of the Groundlands through a gap in the wall she deliberately left under-constructed. She carries Alissa's poison jug to the water filtration plant. A secret minder (a woman with rose hair) follows her; Marissa disables her — hamstrings, severs tendons — and leaves her helpless while she connects the poison jug to the water main at 1 part per mille. On her return, she hangs the disabled woman from the wall in a staged apparent accident and kills her. She also crushes the signal crystals extracted from her breasts four nights earlier to summon the Bizhjerai fleet.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: executioner and saboteur.
- **The sentry** (unnamed woman, rose-bob hair): killed.
- **Bizhjerai fleet**: summoned by crystal signal (Raistlin's fleet + Kendall's fleet).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Poison in water supply; fleet summoned; sentry dead.
- **Long-term**: The poison manifests as mass illness across the Groundlands within days, debilitating the garrison before the Bizhjerai assault.
