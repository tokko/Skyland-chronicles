# Savages Identified as Pre-Apocalypse Precursors (deoch draoidheil)

## Summary

The savages of the Groundlands — the same creatures Marissa fought in book 2 — were revealed to be a magic-fed precursor race created by pre-Apocalypse Mages. They are called "deoch draoidheil" by the Clans (translates roughly to "magic-drinker"). Their key trait is a passive aura that drains magic from anything around them — including enchanted weapons. They are mostly mindless tribal creatures that move in herds, breed in eggs in magic-rich areas, and resist conventional magic. They are NOT created by Grasax (as feared in book 2 chs 27-28); the Reavers ARE — Grasax used the same pre-Apocalypse spell as the basis for the Reaver creation. The savages predate Grasax by centuries.

By book 3, surviving savages have nested in the magical hot zone where the Groundlands fell and have spread northward; their elimination has become a college student examination challenge. The deoch draoidheil have ~300 eggs basking in the Wild Magic at the ruined Groundlands site by book 3 ch 1.

## Participants

- **The savages / deoch draoidheil**: precursor race.
- **Marissa**: confirms the identification via Grasax's old tomes and her own research.
- **Barengar** (student): brings back a live specimen as his graduating exam.

## Consequences

- **Long-term**: The school's curriculum includes hunting and pacifying savages. Marissa's research project (the satellite-based magic distribution network) is in part motivated by safely siphoning the magical surplus that breeds them.
