# The Dragonkin Invasion of the Skylands

## Summary

The Great Barrier protecting the Skylands suddenly failed during a rare rainstorm and a Greater Dragon — later identified as Savistlin of the Gray Flight — punched through with a swarm of her Drakonid offspring and a dozen human Mages riding on her back. The invaders rampaged through the streets of the Skylands, killing thousands. Kyra Zorantis, a mercenary, rallied a column of musketeers and led them onto a square where the dragon had set down. She cut the riding harness off Savistlin's belly, climbed onto the dragon's back, and shot the leader Mage between the shoulder blades and another in the head before being thrown clear by a fireball. The dragon, savagely wounded by massed rifle-fire after losing its shield, retreated into the night. Greg — Kyra's mercenary friend and the father of the young Marissa — was killed by a fireball during the fight. Kyra was wounded (deep claw-gouge in the thigh) and was drained almost to death by the dragon's magic-eating aura. She was unconscious for four days. She was hailed as "Dragonbane" — the city's hero.

## Participants

- **Kyra Zorantis** ("Dragonbane"): mercenary; leads the musketeers, climbs the dragon, kills several Mages; nearly dies of magical exhaustion.
- **Savistlin** (Greater Dragon, Gray Flight): the invader; driven off but not killed; one eye lost.
- **Savistlin's Mages** (a dozen): mostly killed by Kyra on the dragon's back.
- **Greg**: Kyra's mercenary friend; killed by a fireball while charging the dragon's foreleg; leaves behind his daughter Marissa.
- **Marissa** (then ~11): orphaned by Greg's death; Kyra takes her in.
- **Kendall**: Kyra's lifelong friend; ensures she gets the same medical care as a Guard officer.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Hundreds of Skylanders killed. Greg dies. Kyra becomes "Dragonbane." Marissa orphaned and adopted by Kyra's mother.
- **Long-term**: The Skylands learn the Outside is inhabited — overturning centuries of doctrine that the world beyond the Barrier was a Wild Magic wasteland. The First Expedition is commissioned to scout. The Barrier is rebuilt with a permanent gate. The Skylands awaken to the possibility of conquest.
