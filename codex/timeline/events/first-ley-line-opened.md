# First Ley Line Crossing Opened to the North

## Summary

Marissa leads the construction of the first artificial ley line crossing — a focusing tower that taps the dense magic field of the Groundlands valley and routes it into the settlement's power grid. The work involves a journey north with a small party to establish the tower. This is the first major technical accomplishment of Marissa's infiltration and dramatically increases the Groundlands' magical capacity. Chapter 5 ("Power unleashed") depicts the power coming online.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: architect and primary Mage for the construction.
- Construction team (unnamed Skylanders).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Groundlands gains reliable magic supply from the north tower.
- **Long-term**: Marissa's construction of the ley line network is the foundation of the Groundlands' survival — and the means by which she later calibrates its destruction (destroying the towers starves the settlement; overloading the central tower causes the explosion).
