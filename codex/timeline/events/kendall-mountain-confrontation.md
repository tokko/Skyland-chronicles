# Kendall Confronts Marissa on the Mountain / Kyra Intervenes

## Summary

During or after the third ley line crossing construction at the upper mountain (Ch 10), Kendall arrives at the Groundlands under some pretext and confronts Marissa, either challenging her cover story or pushing her about her mission. Kyra appears as a ghost and intervenes to protect Marissa or defuse the confrontation. This is a tense family moment: Kendall knows something is wrong; Kyra's appearance warns him off or reassures him.

- **Kendall**: confronting Marissa.
- **Kyra** (ghost): manifests to intervene.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Confrontation resolved without blowing Marissa's cover.
- **Long-term**: Kendall's visit foreshadows his later involvement in the final assault; his relationship with Marissa is complicated by what he knows or suspects.
