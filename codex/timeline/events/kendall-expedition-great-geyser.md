# Kendall's Expedition to the Great Geyser

## Summary

At the story's opening, Kendall has dispatched three ships on an expedition to the Great Geyser — the ancient magic source beneath what used to be the Skylands. The expedition's purpose is not stated explicitly in Ch 1; it is mentioned as the reason Kendall is absent. The Great Geyser is described as still leaking magic (it is mentioned in the epilogue context) and is presumably a resource or strategic target. The expedition is ongoing when Marissa departs for the Groundlands and does not factor directly into the main plot.

## Participants

- **Kendall**: expedition organiser (absent from Dun Traelak).
- Three ships of Bizhjerai crew (unnamed).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Kendall is occupied; Marissa departs without extended farewell.
- **Long-term**: Not directly resolved in the manuscript. The Geyser's leaked magic is a background element of the world's magic ecology.
