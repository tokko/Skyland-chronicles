# Kyra Meets Anjali in the Elweyr Forest

## Summary

After the Pioneer dropped Kyra and Alissa into the Elweyr forest, the two Skylanders explored on the ground. They were attacked by a bear; Kyra killed it with one rifle shot but believed Alissa had been killed. Kyra encountered a Bizhjerai woman foraging at the forest edge, chased her, and was knocked unconscious by a young red-haired woman wielding a spear. She woke up as a prisoner in the Bizhjerai camp, manacled with magic-suppression cuffs and guarded by the red-haired woman whom Kyra named "Red." Over several days Kyra was interrogated by Red and by Red's father (the Bizhjerai Chief), tried publicly, given a temporary stay of judgement, and assigned to Red's custody. During this period Red and Kyra began their love affair, sparring daily and trading stories. Red was revealed to be Anjali, daughter of the Bizhjerai Chief.

## Participants

- **Kyra Zorantis**: prisoner.
- **Anjali** ("Red", later "the Red Terror" / "mom" to Marissa): Kyra's captor and future wife.
- **Bizhjerai Chief** (Anjali's father; name unnamed on-page in book 1 — referred to as "Father" / "the Chief"): tries Kyra and accepts her into protective custody.
- **Gaelon**: backup Bizhjerai guard; later dies defending women in the Outcast raid.
- **The Bizhjerai shaman**: present at trial; senses Kyra's truthfulness via the Elements.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Kyra becomes part of the Bizhjerai camp; meets Anjali. Alissa is presumed dead (she has actually fled into the wilderness; she returns much later).
- **Long-term**: Kyra and Anjali fall in love. Kyra is adopted into Clan Bizhjerai with the Bizhjerai Rune tattooed on her shoulder. The alliance that follows becomes the Clan resistance against Grasax and (later) against the Skylanders.
