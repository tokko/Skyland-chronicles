# Gereon's Death at the Ball / Marissa's Cover Established

## Summary

On the night before departing for the Groundlands, Marissa attends a ball or social gathering under a cover identity. A man named Gereon is killed — whether by Marissa directly or as a consequence of events around her is not spelled out in the opening pages, but his death cements her cover and allows her to depart. Alissa is present. Marissa boards a train or transport for the first leg of her journey. Chapter 1 establishes the full mission: Marissa is to infiltrate the Skylander settlement being established in the Groundlands and destroy it from within at the appointed time.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: operative; establishes cover as "Mary."
- **Alissa**: present; assists or observes.
- **Gereon**: killed.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa's cover identity as "Mary" is established; she departs for the Groundlands.
- **Long-term**: The false identity will hold until Mathias Varl arrives in Ch 19 and exposes her.
