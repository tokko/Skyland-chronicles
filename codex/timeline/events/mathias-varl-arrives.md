# Mathias Varl Arrives with the Prowler

## Summary

Mathias Varl — a powerful old Mage, Kendall's former teacher — arrives at the Groundlands aboard the Prowler, the Skylander military Skyship. He immediately blows Marissa's cover: having known Kyra personally, he recognises the daughter. Marissa is arrested and imprisoned in a tiny cell. The Prowler's arrival also provides the Groundlands with significant military reinforcement. Varl works closely with Micah thereafter, installing additional magical protections and overseeing Marissa's work under supervision.

## Participants

- **Mathias Varl**: arriving Mage; recognises Marissa; orders her arrest.
- **Marissa**: identity exposed; arrested.
- **Micah**: receives Varl; endorses the arrest.
- The Prowler (Skylander military Skyship).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa's cover destroyed; she is imprisoned.
- **Long-term**: Varl installs oversight preventing Marissa from acting alone; the Prowler becomes a target in the final battle. Varl's identification of Marissa as "Kyra's daughter" alters the power dynamics of the settlement.
