# North Focusing Tower Destroyed by Savages

## Summary

As Marissa, Zandor, and Wylah ride back from the caves toward the Groundlands, the northern focusing tower is destroyed by the savages. A pillar of dust and a flash of actinic white light are seen from a distance — the artificial ley line overhead winks out. The explosion triggers a magical saturation in the cave system and valley that causes a mass hatching of the dormant savage eggs, producing the enormous horde seen in Ch 24.

## Participants

- **Savages**: destroy the tower (method unspecified; implied explosive or magical).
- The northern focusing tower.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Northern ley line gone; section of valley lost to the Groundlands' network.
- **Long-term**: The magical saturation causes the egg hatching; the resulting savage horde of tens of thousands assaults the Groundlands in Ch 24.
