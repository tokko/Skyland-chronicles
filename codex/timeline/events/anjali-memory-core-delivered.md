# Anjali's Memory Core Delivered to Marissa

## Summary

Approximately one year after Kyra's departure following Utopeia's fall, a spell-bug appeared on Marissa's pillow one morning and delivered Anjali's memory core — a magical artefact containing Anjali's memories, personality, or essence. This is how Marissa came to know her second mother, since Anjali was dead before Marissa could form memories of her. The memory core became a formative object in Marissa's childhood.

## Participants

- **Marissa** (recipient; a young child at the time).
- **Kyra** (presumed arranger of the delivery, off-stage).
- **Anjali** (the core contains her; she is already dead at this point).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa gains a connection to her dead mother Anjali.
- **Long-term**: Shapes Marissa's identity and emotional attachment to both mothers; informs her understanding of what she is fighting for.
