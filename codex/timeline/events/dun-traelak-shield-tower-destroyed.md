# Dun Traelak Shield Tower Destroyed; First Duel with Grasax

## Summary

Kyra, Anjali, and a small band of Ithlaki warriors infiltrated Dun Traelak hidden in the cover of the shield tower's permeable barrier. They holed up in the fortress armoury and Kyra built a layered composite spell — shield-breaker, explosive-burst, timer, storage — to destroy the central shield generator, which she discovered was fed not by a stored capacitor but by a magical fount (a smaller version of the Great Geyser) bubbling up through the tower floor. To power the spell, Kyra drained the souls of two dying Outcasts and absorbed their essence. The spell detonated, the shield collapsed, and the Clan shaman simultaneously rent the earth beneath the outer wall, breaching the fortress. Grasax himself emerged and joined the fight. After a desperate duel — Grasax killed an Ithlaki by telekinetic crushing, impaled Anjali on his staff (gut-wound) and drained part of her soul; Kyra lost her left arm to a fireball; Anjali tackled Kyra out of the fount; Kyra wrestled Grasax's soul on the spectral plane and consumed it — Grasax's body was destroyed. The shaman cremated his corpse; only his ornate liche-dagger survived. The fortress fell to the Clans. Kyra emerged half-Archoned, her body covered with glowing magical wounds that bled power and her eyes turned violet.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: combatant and spellwright; loses left arm; transitions to half-Archon state.
- **Anjali**: combatant; nearly killed by Grasax's staff; survives with a permanent belly scar.
- **Grasax**: combatant; body destroyed; soul flees into his dagger (discovered later).
- **Ithlaki Chief**: leads the breach; killed by Grasax's wall-of-force.
- **Uralnor**: combatant; later tattoos Kyra to seal her soul wounds.
- **The Bizhjerai shaman**: rends the earth to breach the wall; later cremates Grasax.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Dun Traelak captured. Grasax's body destroyed. Kyra is grievously wounded — left arm missing, multiple soul-wounds bleeding power, irreversibly transformed toward Archon state. Anjali survives with a permanent gut scar (the same scar Marissa will see decades later).
- **Long-term**: Clan Bizhjerai claims Dun Traelak as its hold. Iolan demands Anjali's hand to back off his Clan's competing claim; she refuses. Grasax's soul is later revealed to inhabit his dagger; Kyra binds it in the cellar. The fount becomes the Bizhjerai magic supply for the next decade. The runes Uralnor tattoos around Kyra's soul wounds form the same pattern that becomes the "Bizhjerai Rune" Kyra is later adopted with.
