# Fall of Utopeia / Kyra's Rampage

## Summary

In retaliation for Anjali's death (and the broader Skylander war), Kyra destroyed the Skylander home city of Utopeia. She mounted the dragon Malkareth and flew on the Skylands, killed millions, and burned Micah (he survived somehow, or this detail is metaphorical — he appears alive in the story). Marissa was a child at Utopeia at this time; her father was killed in the destruction, and she survived. By all estimates fewer than one in a hundred Skylanders survived Utopeia, leaving roughly ten thousand alive. After the destruction, Kyra became a liche, the Skylands themselves were destroyed, Malkareth died, and Kyra's soul was sealed into the liche dagger.

## Participants

- **Kyra** ("mother"): perpetrator; uses dragon Malkareth; becomes liche.
- **Malkareth** (dragon): carries Kyra; dies in the attack.
- **Micah**: described as "burned" by Kyra, yet appears in the story — either he survived or the burning was not fatal.
- **Marissa's father**: killed during the destruction.
- **Marissa** (then a child, ~6–9 years old — age inconsistent, see notes): survives.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Millions of Skylanders killed; Utopeia destroyed; Skylands destroyed; Malkareth dies; Kyra becomes liche and her soul enters the dagger.
- **Long-term**: ~10,000 Skylander survivors scatter. Marissa is orphaned (father dead, birth mother had already died in Elweyr forest). Micah gathers survivors and eventually leads them to the Groundlands. Kyra's soul is carried by Marissa throughout the story.

## Notes

AMBIGUITY — MARISSA'S AGE: The text gives conflicting indicators. At various points Marissa's age during Utopeia is implied to be anywhere from ~6 to ~14. Her exact birth date is not stated. This affects the calculation of her current age in the story. Flagged as a continuity ambiguity.

AMBIGUITY — MICAH'S SURVIVAL: Marissa says Kyra "burned Micah" during the Utopeia attack, yet Micah leads the Skylander survivors and appears in full health in Ch 2. Whether he escaped the fire, was healed, or the text uses "burned" loosely is unresolved.
