# The Bizhjerai-Ithlaki Alliance and March to Dun Traelak

## Summary

In the wake of the Outcast raid, the Bizhjerai broke camp and rode to meet Clan Ithlak. Iolan, son of the Ithlaki Chief, was attracted to Anjali and demanded her hand as the price of Clan Ithlak's participation; Anjali refused. The Ithlaki Chief committed regardless, persuaded by Kyra's plan to assault Dun Traelak. Combined Bizhjerai and Ithlaki cavalry rode ahead to scout the fortress while the rest of the Clans mustered. Kyra and Anjali examined Grasax's permanent barrier from a distance and determined it was permeable for friendly patrols but otherwise impenetrable to outside force. They infiltrated the fortress hidden among returning Outcast patrols by luring a monster to the gates as distraction.

## Participants

- **Kyra Zorantis**: planner and strategist.
- **Anjali**: leads alongside her father.
- **Bizhjerai Chief**: commits Bizhjerai forces.
- **Ithlaki Chief**: ultimately dies leading the breach.
- **Iolan**: son of the Ithlaki Chief; predator-suitor for Anjali; survives book 1.
- **Uralnor**: the Bizhjerai master inker, also a senior warrior.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: An Ithlaki-Bizhjerai host marches on Dun Traelak.
- **Long-term**: Iolan becomes a persistent antagonist. The two-Clan alliance is the foundation for the much larger united-Clans force that later attacks Utopeia.
