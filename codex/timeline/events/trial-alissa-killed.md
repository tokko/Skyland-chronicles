# Marissa's Trial / Alissa Captured and Killed

## Summary

Marissa is put on trial publicly on a stage before the Groundlands population. During the trial, Alissa is produced as a captured co-conspirator. To complete the public demonstration of her "loyalty" or under coercion of some kind, Marissa is forced to kill Alissa herself. The exact mechanism of coercion — threat to others, a trap, direct order — is not fully detailed, but Marissa kills Alissa with her own hand. This is the most personally devastating act of the story prior to Valia's death.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: the accused; the executioner.
- **Alissa**: captured; killed by Marissa.
- **Micah** / **Varl**: orchestrating the trial.
- Groundlands population: witnesses.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Alissa dead; Marissa publicly "proven" not to be an active enemy — but her true identity and parentage are now known.
- **Long-term**: Marissa's grief over Alissa is suppressed (she cannot mourn openly) until Valia's death allows it. The killing haunts her and contributes to her dissociation during the Groundlands genocide.
