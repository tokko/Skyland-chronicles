# Outcast Raid on the Bizhjerai Camp

## Summary

A night raid by the Outcasts — Bizhjerai exiles who serve the liche Grasax — struck the camp where Kyra was being held. Kyra was bound by her magic manacles ("Kyrtos!") and could not fight until Anjali freed her with a counter-command ("Sytrok!"). The Outcasts killed approximately 50 warriors and 50 non-combatants and abducted around 70 captives to be taken to Dun Traelak (Grasax's fortress) for sacrifice and experimentation. Gaelon died defending a tent of women and children. The raid was revealed to be a distraction whose real purpose was the abduction. The Bizhjerai Chief refused to pursue immediately. Anjali insisted on a rescue mission and Kyra outlined a plan to ally with Clan Ithlak and assault the fortress.

## Participants

- **Outcasts**: raiders; many killed, the rest withdraw with captives.
- **Kyra**: armed by Anjali; fights at the camp.
- **Anjali**: drops the magic manacles; leads counter-attack.
- **Gaelon**: dies defending the camp.
- **The Bizhjerai Chief** (Anjali's father).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: ~100 Bizhjerai dead, ~70 captured.
- **Long-term**: Triggers the Bizhjerai-Ithlaki alliance and the assault on Dun Traelak. Cements Kyra's standing as a useful military asset rather than a prisoner.
