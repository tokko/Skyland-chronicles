# Kyra's Soul Wounds Sealed; Adoption into Clan Bizhjerai

## Summary

After Grasax's defeat, Kyra discovered in his library a technique for sealing Archon-style soul wounds via rune-pattern tattoos in magical ink. Anjali drew ink from the fount in the courtyard; Uralnor tattooed the runes across each of Kyra's wounds, sealing the soul-leaks that would otherwise have killed her within days. Once stable, Kyra received the Bizhjerai Rune tattoo at a public ceremony and was formally adopted into Clan Bizhjerai. She and Anjali consummated their relationship that night.

The Bizhjerai also learned that Grasax's mind survived in his ornate dagger; Kyra trapped him in a containment cell in the fortress cellar and connected the wards to the fount for permanent power.

## Participants

- **Kyra**: receives the runes and the Bizhjerai Mark.
- **Anjali**: harvests the magic ink; participates in the tattoo work; sponsors Kyra's adoption.
- **Uralnor**: master inker.
- **The Bizhjerai Chief**: presides over Kyra's adoption.
- **The Bizhjerai shaman**: officiates.
- **Iolan**: present, hostile; storms off when Kyra publicly kisses Anjali at the celebration.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Kyra stabilises. The Bizhjerai Mark on her left shoulder is the same rune that Marissa later wears decades later. Kyra and Anjali openly become a couple.
- **Long-term**: Grasax's dagger remains in the Dun Traelak cellar — the same dagger that, after Anjali's death, Kyra will use to commit suicide-into-liche-hood and that Marissa will carry through book 2.
