# Farmers Refuse Trade / Micah Orders Their Murder

## Summary

Marissa and others travel to nearby farmsteads to negotiate trade. The farmers refuse. Micah responds by ordering Brengar to take a kill-team and eliminate the farmers and their village. Approximately two hundred people are killed. This act is the concrete moment that hardens Marissa's conviction that the Skylanders cannot change; it becomes the moral lynchpin she returns to when confronting Zandor in Ch 25 ("How many innocent lives is it worth, Zandor?"). Zandor is present but does not support the order.

## Participants

- **Micah**: orders the massacre.
- **Brengar**: leads the kill-team.
- **Marissa**: present for the trade refusal; learns of the massacre.
- **Zandor**: present; explicitly does not support the order.
- Farmers (~200 killed, unnamed).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: ~200 farmers killed; Groundlands obtains or avoids whatever trade item was at issue.
- **Long-term**: Cements Marissa's moral case for destroying the Groundlands. Brengar becomes a target; Alissa eventually assassinates him (Ch 18).
