# Kyra Trains as Guard Under Micah

## Summary

Kyra was trained as a Skylander Guard under Micah, who was at that time her commanding officer. The details of this training are not given in full; it is referenced to explain Kyra's extraordinary combat and magical abilities, her lifelong familiarity with Micah, and the deep personal betrayal she felt when their paths diverged. Kyra came to regard Micah as a trusted superior before eventually turning against Skylander imperialism.

## Participants

- **Kyra** (trainee Guard): gains fighting and magical expertise; forms complex relationship with Micah.
- **Micah** (commanding officer / trainer): instils discipline and loyalty; later becomes her enemy.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Kyra becomes one of the Skylands' most capable Mage-warriors.
- **Long-term**: Mutual familiarity between Kyra and Micah becomes a recurring undercurrent; Kyra's eventual destruction of Micah and Utopeia is framed as a betrayal of a former bond.
