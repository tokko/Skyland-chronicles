# Marissa Finalises the Sabotage Plan

## Summary

During her week of grief-isolation after Valia's death, Marissa finalises her plan to destroy the Groundlands. The plan has four components: (1) signal the Bizhjerai fleet by crushing the crystals implanted in her breasts; (2) lace the water supply with Alissa's hidden poison; (3) plant explosive power cores in the munitions magazines on both cliff faces; (4) destroy the central shield tower by overloading its storage crystals. She finds Alissa's poison jug hidden behind a false wall panel in her workshop.

## Participants

- **Marissa**: planner and sole executor.
- **Alissa** (posthumous): poison and intelligence assets.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Sabotage timeline set; all four components eventually executed.
- **Long-term**: The plan's execution in Chs 24–25 triggers the Battle of the Groundlands.
