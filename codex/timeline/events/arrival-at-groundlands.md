# Marissa Arrives at the Groundlands / Meets Micah

## Summary

Marissa arrives at the nascent Skylander settlement in the Groundlands under her cover identity as "Mary." She meets Micah — who does not recognise her as Kyra's daughter — and begins integrating as a Construction Mage. Micah is the supreme commander; the settlement is in early stages, consisting largely of tents and the beginning of materialised-magic construction. Marissa assesses what she will need to destroy.

## Participants

- **Marissa** ("Mary"): arrives undercover; begins construction and intelligence work.
- **Micah**: greeting and assigning the new arrival.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Marissa establishes her role in the settlement as a trusted Construction Mage.
- **Long-term**: Her technical contributions to the Groundlands — ley lines, receiver tower, shields — become the very infrastructure she later uses to destroy it.
