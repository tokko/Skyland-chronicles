# Duel with the Liche Grasax

## Summary

Kyra fought and defeated the liche Grasax in single combat. During the duel, Grasax stabbed Anjali fatally with his staff. Kyra killed Grasax, gaining his power and suffering near-fatal wounds of her own. Afterwards, Uralnor (a Bizhjerai figure) tattooed Kyra — presumably the Bizhjerai Rune that marks her body. Kyra then killed the Clan leader Iolan (heart ripped out) after Anjali's cremation, apparently punishing him for some role in the circumstances that led to Anjali's death.

## Participants

- **Kyra** ("mother"): combatant; gains power, suffers near-fatal wounds.
- **Grasax** (liche): combatant; killed by Kyra.
- **Anjali** ("mom"): fatally stabbed by Grasax's staff during the duel.
- **Uralnor**: Bizhjerai figure; tattoos Kyra with the Bizhjerai Rune afterwards.
- **Iolan** (Clan leader of Clan Ithlak): killed by Kyra after Anjali's cremation (heart ripped out).

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Grasax dies; Kyra gains his power; Anjali mortally wounded and dies shortly after.
- **Long-term**: Kyra's grief over Anjali's death fuels the Utopeia destruction. Kyra inherits or acquires the liche dagger (which later holds her soul). Grasax's research on the Reavers — creatures derived from pre-Apocalypse savage precursors — was known to Kyra.
