# The Dragon Malkareth Hatched

## Summary

A pair of dragons fought in the skies above Dun Traelak; the loser fell to the plains and was killed by the Bizhjerai. While the corpse was being butchered for meat, Kyra discovered a dragon egg forming in the dying body — its magic was being drawn to a new locus. She claimed it (over Iolan's furious objection) and placed it in the fount in the courtyard, surrounded by a power-gathering enchantment and a shield. After several months of soaking up the fount's magic the egg hatched and a small red dragon emerged. Kyra and Anjali raised him as their son. Anjali named him Malkareth, after a dragon-patriarch of old. He was telepathically bonded to Kyra from birth and to Anjali through Kyra. He spoke his first words at three months old.

## Participants

- **Malkareth**: red dragon; Kyra and Anjali's adopted son; the bonded telepathic dragon of book 1; later sacrifices himself at the fall of the Skylands.
- **Kyra**: foster-mother and bond-holder.
- **Anjali**: foster-mother through the bond Malkareth later extends to her.
- **Iolan**: tried to claim the egg; refused; eventually given the dragon's armoury and meat as compensation.

## Consequences

- **Immediate**: Clan Bizhjerai becomes the only Clan with a tame dragon. The fount-tower regrows around the egg.
- **Long-term**: Malkareth grows rapidly through the seasons of book 1. He flies, learns to carry riders, and ultimately gives his life alongside Kyra at the Great Geyser to destroy the Skylands.
