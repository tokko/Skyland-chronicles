# Ford — Identity

## Status

Alive (book-1 ch.1). Subsequent appearances TBD.

## Role

Detective. Runs unsanctioned anti-Stardust raids. The only sustained law-enforcement pressure on the cartels in book-1; the Constabulary leadership is corrupt and accepts cartel bribes, so Ford operates off-book, funding his operations with embezzled or confiscated money. He hires mercenaries per-job under separate contracts — there is no permanent unit, no shared insignia, no faction. Each hire is paid per engagement and dismissed.

## Physical description (on-page, book-1 ch.1)

Minimal on-page description in ch.1. Constabulary affiliation implied by the "Constabulary-grey men" at the courtyard. Further description deferred to later chapters.

## Voice and register

Direct, transactional, honest in payment. Ford does not cheat his contractors. He executes captives (surviving workers, surrendered cartel labour) at raid's end as a matter of policy, framed as a "greater good" deniability calculus: no witness, no continuity thread, no cartel retaliation traced back to a living informant. He does not present this as cruelty; he presents it as accountancy.

## Known operational facts (book-1 ch.1)

- Contracted Kyra and Anton for the book-1 ch.1 raid (among other hires on the same block, not tracked).
- Pays honestly on completion.
- Executes surviving captives at raid's end — policy, not impulse.
- Has run "multiple raids" against Stardust manufacturers prior to ch.1 (pre-book-1 backstory, not dramatised).

## Notes

- Ford does not contract the Spire Wardens. His mercs are Guard-tier and below.
- His unsanctioned status means the cartel's corruption of the Constabulary is not a problem he can solve from inside; he is solving it from below.
- Future appearances in book-1 (ch.3 onward) may expand this stub.
