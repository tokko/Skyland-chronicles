# Voice — Marissa Zorantis

## Register

First-person, close interior narration. Colloquial to the point of coarse — she swears freely, uses "gonna," "ain't," "gotta," "fuck" as punctuation. But her underlying education shows in flashes: precise technical language when discussing spells and construction, dry sardonic wit, occasional rhetorical formality when making arguments about Skylander ideology. She shifts between registers within a single paragraph: hard profanity giving way to precise engineering vocabulary, then back to gut-level emotion.

## Vocabulary

Domains she draws from: spell mechanics (ley lines, power cores, telekinesis, Blood Magic, meta-magic), engineering and construction (load-bearing, ratio, lattice, stabiliser), weapons (bore, calibre, breech, sights, revolver hammer), flight dynamics (vector, thrust, attitude, torque).

Words she reaches for under stress: "shit," "fuck" (both as expletive and emphasis), "dammit," "shit." Also "figure out," "clever," and self-deprecating labels ("not a warrior, a scholar").

Words she does not use: therapy-register emotion naming ("process," "trauma," "triggered"). She names grief as a practical problem to be managed ("the grief won't serve me now"). She does not say "beautiful" without irony. She is not given to floral imagery.

## Syntax

Short declarative sentences under pressure. When she is thinking through a problem, sentences lengthen and gain subordinate clauses — she reasons on the page. Her inner monologue has a habit of asking herself a question and answering it bluntly in the next sentence. She interrupts her own trains of thought when action demands it and picks them up later with "Anyway." or "Where was I."

Under emotional extremes her sentences fragment into staccato notation — she stops completing thoughts and just records sensory data.

## Tics

- "Shit." as a complete standalone sentence when surprised.
- Self-commentary on her own physical limitations: "I was more scholar than warrior."
- Using "clever" approvingly of others (Valia especially); rarely of herself.
- The phrase "sample size of one" (borrowed from someone else's argument, deployed ironically against herself).
- Addressing herself internally in second person when self-critical: "What a selfish cunt you've been."
- Calling Kendall "Uncle Kenny" only in moments of genuine warmth or vulnerability.

## What they avoid saying

Sentiment. She narrates Valia's death with engineering precision (trajectory, impact, bone fragmentation) before permitting herself a single "Fuck, why Valia?" She will describe what she does with a corpse before she will describe how she feels about the person. Grief appears in action beats — the bridal carry, the violet shroud, refusing to leave the body — not in named emotion.

She never says she loves someone easily in dialogue; when she does ("I love you, Zandor"), the repetition signals it is a farewell and she knows it.

## Examples

```
"Shit." — said when she realises the focusing tower was a trap; pure reflex; the period is the whole sentence.

"I was more of a scholar than a warrior, dammit!" — said while struggling to keep pace in the cave chase; self-deprecation as frustration management, never false modesty.

"When in doubt, blow shit up." — repeating Kyra's advice to herself; the quote becomes a character beat whenever she deploys it, flagging that she has given up on the subtle approach.

"That question, and your answer, is the reason why I must do this, Zandor." — confronting him at the shield tower; formal construction, no profanity; she only loses the colloquial register when making ideological arguments she has rehearsed for years.

"Not today, mother. Not today. But one day." — dagger scene in ch28; the only time she permits herself three consecutive short declarative sentences without irony.
```

## Anti-examples

```
"She felt a profound sadness settle over her as she contemplated what she had lost." — too named, too passive; she would never write her own emotions this way.

"The tapestry of her past unravelled before her eyes." — tapestry is on the AI-ism blacklist; she also does not use that kind of metaphor; she thinks in physical objects.

"She smiled softly, her eyes glistening with unshed tears." — she does not observe her own face; she observes what her hands are doing and what she can't bring herself to look at.
```
