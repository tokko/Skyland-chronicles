# Marissa Zorantis

## Identity

Born to a Skylander father (Niklas Zorantis, killed by Skylanders after refusing to fight) and a Skylander woman named simply "mom" (later identified as the Red Terror's partner). Her dragon-brother Malkareth hatched from a ley-line fount in Dun Traelak. Her adoptive mother is Kyra Zorantis (slug: kyra-zorantis), the Bizhjerai general known as "The Red Terror" who destroyed the Skylands.

After the destruction of Utopeia (the surviving Skylander settlement that replaced the Skylands), Marissa is raised in Dun Traelak by Kendall and the Bizhjerai Clan. She becomes a Construction Mage. She infiltrates the new Skylander settlement, the Groundlands, under the alias Mary Laier — planning to facilitate a Bizhjerai fleet attack that will destroy the last Skylanders.

At the epilogue she has fulfilled her mission and exiled herself from Dun Traelak, riding back to the Groundlands alone to contain the Wild Magic left by the tower explosion and begin atonement.

## Physical

Short woman; small-framed; brown hair with crimson and violet streaks; green eyes. Carries the physical marks of extensive Blood Magic use: visibly countable ribs by the end, halved breast and hip volume from years of muscle/fat converted to power. By chapter 24 she has a healing broken femur (right leg, telekinetic splint), a cracked armour corset, a knife wound in the left shoulder (self-cauterised), and a broken nose (earlier chapters). Has a Bizhjerai Rune tattooed on her left shoulder — the source of magical strength boost when activated; originally disguised with Alissa's tattoo work. Wears Angel armour (chapter 1 onward).

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Complete her infiltration mission — sabotage the Groundlands' defences and signal the Bizhjerai fleet.

**Deeper goal**: Fulfil the oath she swore to her mothers — avenge their deaths and ensure the Skylanders can never again threaten the world.

**Misbelief**: That destroying the Skylanders will bring peace and allow her to feel something — relief, belonging, completion. By chapter 27 she discovers it left her empty and questions whether she has simply become what she hated.

## Arc

**Start (ch 1)**: A focused agent, cold and mission-driven, suppressing grief over her mothers' deaths.

**End (ch 28)**: The mission is complete but hollow. She rejects both Skylander and Bizhjerai identity, choosing exile and atonement. Decides to contain the Wild Magic, understand the savages, and teach worthy mages to use power ethically.

**Change**: From ideological certainty (Skylanders are irredeemably evil, their destruction is justice) to moral ambiguity — she recognises that genocide made her the thing she destroyed.

## Notes

- The "Siever" nickname is used by Skylanders as a slur/label for her mage ability to sieve power; she wears it ironically.
- She has breast implants containing crystals — the summoning crystals were embedded there for concealment.
- Her Blood Magic use visibly degrades her physique; this is a recurring motif.
- The dagger that contains Kyra's spirit is referred to as "mother's dagger" throughout.
- She addresses Kyra as "mother" and her birth mother (Kyra's partner, the Red Terror) as "mom" — these are distinct characters.
- Kendall is not a blood relative; she calls him "uncle" as an honorific.
