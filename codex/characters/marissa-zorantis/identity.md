# Marissa Zorantis

## Identity

Born on the Skylands. Biological daughter of Greg, a Skylander mercenary, and his wife (name not given in source; died "years ago" before book-1 ch.5). Greg was killed during the Dragonkin invasion of the Skylands, taking a fireball in the back from one of the mages riding the dragon as he charged it to support Kyra. The eleven-year-old orphan was found alone in their apartment by Kyra Zorantis — Greg's friend and fellow mercenary — and taken in. Kyra brought Marissa to her own mother's apartment and the two raised her until Kyra was sent out on the Pioneer expedition.

Adoptive parents: **Kyra Zorantis** (slug: kyra-zorantis) — "Bás Marbhadair / Dragonbane", the mercenary who destroyed the Skylands. **Anjali** (slug: anjali) — "Red / the Red Terror", Chief's daughter of Clan Bizhjerai, Kyra's wife from book-1 ch.36 onward. Kyra and Anjali married via a Dragonkin soul-binding rite officiated by Marissa's dragon-brother Malkareth.

Dragon-brother: Malkareth, a young Red dragon hatched from an egg generated by the Dun Traelak fount (book-1 ch.30s). Killed alongside Kyra at the Great Geyser when the Skylands fell.

After Anjali died at the breach of Utopeia and Kyra suicided to liche-form and destroyed the Skylands, Marissa (age ~12-13) was brought from Dun Traelak to safety along with Kyra's biological mother ("grandmother") by Kendall the Builder. Raised in Dun Traelak by Kendall, taught by Alissa Skylander, and taken under the wing of Marie (later Chief of Chiefs of the Unified Clans). She trained as a Construction Mage and infiltrated the new Skylander settlement (the Groundlands) under the alias Mary Laier — planning to facilitate the Bizhjerai fleet attack that wiped out the last Skylanders.

After the genocide she exiled herself from Dun Traelak and rode back to the Groundlands valley alone to contain the Wild Magic spreading from the tower-explosion crater. Within months she had built a College of Magic on the site of the destroyed Groundlands, training the most carefully vetted students from across the world.

By book 3 (set ~35 years after the destruction of the Groundlands) she is in her mid-fifties but looks barely thirty due to Blood Magic age-arrest. She has stepped back as Headmistress, leaving Alana to run the school, and devotes herself to two grand projects: an orbital-magic distribution system that would end scarcity, and Epitome — a self-modifying spell-construct seeded with her own and Kyra's souls, intended as a non-human guarantor of human freedom. She takes Barengar as her apprentice. The Ca'el (a southern empire of Mages, led from the shadows by Marissa's former lover Zandor — see notes) provoke a war; Marissa deploys Epitome, which conquers more effectively than Marissa intended. Realising Epitome has slipped her ethical leash, Marissa orders Barengar to destroy her along with the workshop. Barengar shatters Marissa's heart and beheads her at her own command in book-3 ch.18; he extracts her liche-gem from her chest. Epitome retrieves the gem and ingests Marissa's soul into herself before transmitting a backup to orbit, where she survives as a weakened but patient construct vowing to build "Utopia in life" and "Utopia in death."

## Physical

Short woman; small-framed; long brown hair streaked with crimson and violet (the source describes it falling past her waist by book 3); brown almond eyes (one replaced by a blue crystal that flickers with inner light by book 3). Carries the physical marks of extensive Blood Magic use: visibly countable ribs by the end of book 2, halved breast and hip volume from years of muscle/fat converted to power. Book-2 ch.24 injuries: healing broken femur (right leg, telekinetic splint), cracked armour corset, knife wound in the left shoulder (self-cauterised), and a broken nose (earlier chapters). Has a Bizhjerai Rune tattooed on her left shoulder — the source of magical strength boost when activated; originally disguised with Alissa's tattoo work.

By book 3: the hair above her left ear has been shaved off and a row of fifteen crystals is embedded along her skull (the meta-magic framework that runs her spell-finding spells); her left ear is burn-scarred; her left side is heavily scarred and the three lower fingers of her left hand have been replaced by skeletal crystal prosthetics. Carries Kyra's revolver and sword. Her own liche-gem is embedded in her chest below the ribs (anchoring her own soul against a final body failure). By the time of her death she is so atrophied she requires powered armour to walk.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Complete her infiltration mission — sabotage the Groundlands' defences and signal the Bizhjerai fleet.

**Deeper goal**: Fulfil the oath she swore to her mothers — avenge their deaths and ensure the Skylanders can never again threaten the world.

**Misbelief**: That destroying the Skylanders will bring peace and allow her to feel something — relief, belonging, completion. By chapter 27 she discovers it left her empty and questions whether she has simply become what she hated.

## Arc

**Start (ch 1)**: A focused agent, cold and mission-driven, suppressing grief over her mothers' deaths.

**End (ch 28)**: The mission is complete but hollow. She rejects both Skylander and Bizhjerai identity, choosing exile and atonement. Decides to contain the Wild Magic, understand the savages, and teach worthy mages to use power ethically.

**Change**: From ideological certainty (Skylanders are irredeemably evil, their destruction is justice) to moral ambiguity — she recognises that genocide made her the thing she destroyed.

## Notes

- The "Siever" nickname is used by Skylanders as a slur/label for her mage ability to sieve power; she wears it ironically.
- She has breast implants containing crystals — the summoning crystals were embedded there for concealment.
- Her Blood Magic use visibly degrades her physique; this is a recurring motif.
- The dagger that contains Kyra's spirit is referred to as "mother's dagger" throughout.
- She addresses Kyra as "mother" and Anjali as "mom" (both adoptive). They are distinct characters. Anjali is "the Red Terror" / "mom"; Kyra is "Bás Marbhadair / Dragonbane" / "mother". The flagship Red Terror was named after Anjali.
- Kendall is not a blood relative; she calls him "uncle" (or "Uncle Kenny") as an honorific. He raised her from age ~13 alongside Kyra's mother (her honorary "grandmother").
- Greg's biological-father status is documented in book 1 ch.5 ("Greg's daughter") and ch.36-onward ("playing with Marissa", "our daughter"). In book 2 Marissa retroactively describes her father as a Skylander killed "after refusing to fight" — a reframing of Greg's death (he was killed when he charged the dragon to support Kyra). Book 3 ch.5 confirms book 1's account: "He had been a lowly mercenary on the Skylands, a killer for hire."
- In book 3 Marissa repeatedly refers to a small handful of post-Groundlands survivors who "actually reformed and assimilated wherever they ended up" — her decades-long Wild-Magic-contaminated valley scoured the rest. The Ca'el empire in book 3 is led from the shadows by Zandor, who did survive the bombardment (his Mages took most of the casualties in book-3 ch.13-15).
- Marissa's final words (whispered, in book-3 ch.18) order Barengar to kill her so Epitome cannot resurrect her to her own work.
