# Barengar

## Identity

A young Mage from the eastern Bizhjerai lands who attends Marissa's College of Magic in book 3. Tall, dark-haired, dark-eyed, sinewy rather than bulky. Not the brightest student in his cohort but disciplined, methodical, ruthless, single-mindedly loyal to whomever he serves. He is selected as Marissa's personal apprentice after he completes the school's graduation challenge — bring back a live "deoch draoidheil" (savage) — by methodically maiming his prey rather than risk a clean capture. Marissa picks him precisely because he interpreted the order literally and creatively without sentiment for the prey.

He spends book 3 as Marissa's right hand: assistant in her workshop, witness to her revelation that she is the Last Skylander, sworn vassal under magical oath to keep her secrets. They develop a wary master-apprentice intimacy that does not become romantic — Marissa repeatedly notes he reminds her of Zandor but he never replaces him; Barengar himself becomes fond of Marie's cousin-niece Jani (a Bizhjerai sergeant) in the back half of the book.

His final act in book 3 is to kill Marissa at her own command — shattering her heart and beheading her with his axe on the platform above her workshop — then to extract her liche-gem from her chest before destroying the workshop and triggering its overload. He flies the Archangel back to Dun Traelak with Marissa's and Kyra's memory-cores in his pocket, intending to transcribe both women's lives into a written record so the world will know how vengeance destroyed them.

He is the closing POV of book 3 (alongside Epitome's epilogue chapter).

He is **NOT** the same character as **Brengar** in book 2 (the corrupt gate-guard at the Groundlands, killed by Alissa in book-2 ch.18). The spellings are similar but the characters are unrelated — Brengar is a Skylander; Barengar is a Bizhjerai-region Mage three decades younger. Resolved as: **two distinct characters, similar names**.

## Physical

Tall — taller than Marissa by a head and a half. Sinewy, not powerfully built. Shoulders not broad. Dark hair, dark eyes. Skin "dusky" (book-3 ch.2 description from Marissa's perspective). Wears a crystal mail shirt with armoured pauldrons and a thick belt during expeditions; carries a long-hafted single-bladed axe across his back (the same axe with two enchantments — heat for cauterising, kinetic for shielded blows), a long dagger, and a revolver. Refuses the rifle in book-3 ch.3 ("I'm no marksman"); Marissa quietly intends to fix that.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Become Marissa's apprentice and learn her secrets — particularly meta-magic and the spell-finder array.

**Deeper goal**: Be useful enough to survive and matter. He grew up in a Bizhjerai border community where dogged competence was his only currency.

**Misbelief**: That loyalty alone is what Marissa wants from him. She actually wants creative ruthlessness — which he has — but he keeps confusing the two through the early chapters.

## Arc

Disciplined-but-cocky graduate → Marissa's apprentice → witness to Marissa's grief and her workshop secrets → executioner of his master at her own command → keeper of the chronicle. The first POV character in the trilogy who survives all three books.

## Knowledge state

By book-3 ch.18: knows the existence and location of the primary workshop (above the Great Geyser site), the bunker (pre-Apocalypse machines), the meta-magic spell-finder framework, the Archangel armour design, the orbital station system, the existence of Epitome and her Reliquary of Souls, Marissa's biography (from her own memory-core gem), and Kyra's biography (from her memory-core gem). Has taken the magical oath of secrecy that prevents him sharing most of it.

Does **not** know whether Epitome's transmitted backup to the orbital station succeeded (he flies away before the orb expands; he sees it bloom and wink out and assumes destruction).

## Notes

- Barengar's name in book 3 ALL caps headings and Marissa's dialog is spelled with two A's: "Barengar". The book-2 character "Brengar" is one A — different name, different person.
- His relationship with Marissa is mentor/apprentice, never sexual; book-3 ch.3 he catches himself looking at her body and reminds himself "she was old enough to be my mother, and then some."
- He is the closest the trilogy has to a moral-compass POV by book 3 — not because he refuses to kill (he is happy to kill) but because he keeps asking what the killing is for.
