# Marie

## Identity

A Bizhjerai warrior of Kyra's and Anjali's generation. Survived the breach at Utopeia despite a hip wound just before Anjali's death. After Anjali's death and Iolan's execution by Kyra at the funeral, Marie was elected **Chief of Chiefs of the Unified Clans** — the position that consolidated the Clans into a single political body across the post-war years. She holds the position into book 3, roughly seventy years old by then.

She was a maternal figure to Marissa in the Dun Traelak years between Marissa's adoption and the Groundlands infiltration. Book 2 ch.3 names her among Marissa's teachers and protectors: "I had been taught by Alissa Skylander and Kendall the Builder and was the protégé of Marie, the Chief of Chiefs." Marie also personally hand-picked and trained the Bizhjerai Angel corps (book 2 ch.26).

Her husband is the unnamed Bizhjerai ground commander seen briefly in book 2 ch.27. He survives into book 3 chapter 3 where Marissa sees him commanding the city guard at Lahar.

Her daughter "Anjali" — named for Marissa's mom — becomes General of the Unified Clans by book 3 and commands the army in the Ca'el war. Her other daughter (unnamed in source) is also a senior military officer.

Her relationship with Marissa fractures over book 2's events and the subsequent decades. By book 3 they have not spoken in nearly twenty years. Marie loves the child Marissa was but hates the genocidaire she became — "Marie had adored Marissa when she was a child and had taken her under her wing when Marissa's parents had died... Marie hated what Marissa had become yet loved the child she had been and she respected the skills and inventions that Marissa had produced." (book 3 ch.18)

She is the receiving Chief who hears Marissa's full confession about Epitome — including Kyra's voluntary dissolution — at the climax of book 3, and weeps for Kyra.

## Physical

In book 3 (~age 70): "a striking woman on the older side" — at least a decade older than Marissa but looks "thirty years older" due to lack of Blood-Magic age-arrest. Grey-streaked hair, lined face. Book 1 ch.49 establishes her as a Bizhjerai warrior in her prime fighting at Utopeia; the source does not give her physical description from that era beyond competence.

## Voice

Bizhjerai warrior register; commanding, formal-but-warm with friends, cold with adversaries. Older-statesperson cadence in book 3 — measured, deliberate, with the weight of decades of rulership. Profanity rare. Uses "Spirits" rather than "Gods". Calls Marissa by name; never by any title.

## Goals

**Surface goal (book 3)**: Hold the Unified Clans together; end the Ca'el war on favourable terms; preserve the Bizhjerai way of life through the next succession.

**Deeper goal**: Honour the legacy of those who died at Utopeia — Anjali, Kyra (in spirit), her own fallen comrades.

## Arc

Bizhjerai warrior at Anjali's side (book 1) → wartime survivor and elected Chief of Chiefs → Marissa's mentor and aunt-figure → decades-long estrangement from Marissa over the Groundlands genocide → final reconciliation through Kyra's sacrifice (book 3).

## Notes

- Book-2 ch.27's Aurean-reference to "Marie wounded at the breach at Utopeia" is **resolved as referring to this same Marie**. Marie was wounded in the hip at Utopeia, survived, and went on to be elected Chief of Chiefs. The book-2 codex flagged this as ambiguous; book 1 and book 3 both confirm: same Marie, alive, leads the Clans.
- "Marie's husband" appears in book 2 ch.27 and again in book 3 ch.3 commanding Lahar's city guard. The source never names him.
