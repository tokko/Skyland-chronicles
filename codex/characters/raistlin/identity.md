# Raistlin

## Identity

Mercenary captain. Marissa's contact who was contracted to bring his steam-powered Skyship — the Fist of Heaven — as part of the assault fleet. He commands a large, grey metal, heavily armoured vessel with a "big prow cannon." He is not Bizhjerai but is allied for the mission.

In chapter 26 the Fist of Heaven is disabled by the Prowler — its rudders are shot. It begins to withdraw for repairs but is not destroyed. Raistlin's personal fate is unknown.

## Notes

- Ioana is noted as the captain of Raistlin's fleet (or his captain — the exact relationship is ambiguous). She should have been approaching when the crystals were crushed in chapter 23/24.
- The Fist of Heaven is a steam-powered Skyship, distinguishable from the crystal Bizhjerai vessels.
