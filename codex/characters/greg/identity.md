# Greg

## Identity

A Skylander mercenary, Kyra's closest professional friend on the Skylands. Widower; raised his daughter Marissa alone in the Undercity (a fairly safe lower-level neighbourhood). Distinguished by impeccable manners despite his trade. Skilled fighter; the wickedly-curved short sword he carried earned him a reputation across the Undercity. Fenced viciously with it; ran into engagements howling but came out clean.

His wife "died years ago" — book 1 mentions her only in passing and never names her. Marissa is his only child. He doted on her. Kyra is implied to have visited their apartment several times before book 1 opens.

Greg dies in the Dragonkin invasion (book-1 ch.4). When Kyra recklessly climbs onto the back of the attacking dragon and kills the Mages riding it, Greg charges the dragon's foreleg to support her. One of the surviving riding-Mages incinerates him in a single fireball before Kyra can intervene. His death is the engine for Marissa's adoption by Kyra in book-1 ch.5.

In book 2 (decades later) Marissa recollects her father as "a Skylander killed for refusing to fight" — a partial misremembering or sanitising of what actually happened. Book 3 ch.5 confirms book 1's account: "He had been a lowly mercenary on the Skylands, a killer for hire."

## Physical

Tall (book-1 ch.5 implies Marissa inherited her black hair from her mother and her piercing blue eyes from Greg). Pale skin. Wears typical Skylander mercenary kit — armoured jacket, his curved short sword always at his side, a small shield projector. "Long blond braids" by book-1 ch.4. (Note: the source describes him as "blond" in ch.4 and "blue-eyed" in ch.5 — consistent.)

## Voice

Polite, dry, slightly courtly for a mercenary. Calls Kyra by name. Compliments easily. Adores his daughter and talks about her often. No `voice.md` warranted — he is a function character whose voice colour appears only in two short ch.2-4 exchanges.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Earn enough mercenary pay to move from the Undercity to the surface, where Marissa would be safer.

**Deeper goal**: Raise his daughter well after her mother's death.

## Notes

- The book-2 codex's "Niklas Zorantis" naming for Marissa's father is **a drafting error**. Marissa's biological father in book 1 is named Greg. Niklas does not appear anywhere in the source.
- Greg's body is left on the Skylands surface; not recovered for any funeral. Kyra and Marissa do not return to view it.
- His curved short sword is implied (not stated) to be among Marissa's posessions later — Marissa carries a sword from Dun Traelak in book 2; the source does not establish whether it is Greg's, Kyra's, or new.
