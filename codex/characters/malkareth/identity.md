# Malkareth

## Identity

A young Red Dragon hatched from an egg materialised by the Dun Traelak fount at the moment Kyra was being formally adopted by Clan Bizhjerai. Named for "the great Malkareth of old", a legendary patriarch among the Red Flight (the patriarch dragon his bloodline rebirth-mechanism may eventually iterate into).

Kyra raises him from hatching; he is mind-bonded to her from the moment he first crawls into her lap and licks her face. Within a year he is large enough to carry Kyra in flight; within two years he is approaching adult Red size. He grows abnormally fast — Marius the Dragonkin attributes this to the rich magic field at Dun Traelak.

He officiates Kyra and Anjali's marriage in book-1 ch.36 by binding their souls in a Dragonkin rite that he learned from the Dragonkin Serena and Marius. After that, the three of them share a mind-bond.

He nearly dies in book-1 ch.38 giving Kyra the last of his power to expel Grasax. He recovers but is left structurally vulnerable.

He dies in book-1 ch.51 — flies to the Great Geyser with Kyra and dives into the magic stream alongside her to drain the Skylands of power. His body comes apart under the pressure; his power surges out in a violent storm; his last word is "Mother."

Marissa's epithet through books 2-3 — "my dragon brother" — refers to him.

## Physical

Red Dragon. Glittering scales. Two large wings; serpentine body. Doubles in size every few months for his first two years. By death he is large enough to fight wyverns of his own flight but not yet a fully-grown Greater Dragon — adult dragons would have towered over him.

## Voice

Telepathic only. Communicates mind-to-mind with Kyra, Anjali, and Marissa. Speaks formally — "Mother", "Other Mother", "Mothers" — and sometimes with teenage exasperation. Has a sense of humour ("Ugh," he rolls his eyes when his mothers kiss in book-1 ch.39).

## Notes

- The fact that dragons are spell-beasts with a soul-containment enchantment that allows them to be reborn after death (Kyra discovers this in book-1 ch.39) raises the unanswered question of whether Mal's soul-pattern could in principle re-form in a new dragon. The source does not pursue this — Mal is treated as definitively dead.
- His egg's creation from the Dun Traelak fount happens during Kyra's adoption-ceremony — the timing is the source of his lifelong bond to Kyra.
