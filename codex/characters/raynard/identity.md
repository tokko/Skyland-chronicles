# Raynard

## Identity

A Skylander mercenary; tall, shaved-and-waxed scalp, "deep, masculine, smooth" voice. Cruel, charismatic, sexually controlling. He had a hypnotic erotic hold over Kyra throughout her Skylands career — book-1 ch.1 establishes the pattern: he interrupts her after a raid, brutally has sex with her, deliberately denies her release, then humiliates her by paying her in cash like a whore. She hates him; her body responds to him anyway; she cannot resist him until she meets Anjali.

He goes out with the same Pioneer expedition (or a subsequent expedition) that brings Kyra to the mainland — book-1 ch.5's Kendall confirms "Raynard did not return" from the First Expedition. Kyra later finds him alive among the prisoners after the battle against Clan Kriath in book-1 ch.17 — he has joined the Kriath as an exchange-marriage / alliance broker. Kyra realises Raynard is the architect of the inter-Clan war that has just devastated thousands. With Anjali's silent support she shoots him in the face with her revolver.

His death is structurally the turning point of book 1's first half — Kyra is finally free of his sexual hold over her, and the freedom is what lets her fall in love with Anjali.

## Physical

Tall, broad, shaved-and-waxed scalp, "handsome face", calloused hands. Possibly tattooed (the Mark of Clan Kriath appears on his shoulder by book-1 ch.17). Brown eyes. Wears Skylander armoured kit through book 1.

## Voice

"Smooth", deep, mocking. Speaks easily and confidently. Issues commands rather than requests. Calls Kyra by name, never by endearment.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Power, money, sexual conquest. Brokers the Skylands-Kriath alliance for his own elevation.

**Deeper goal**: Domination — sexual and otherwise.

## Notes

- The codex previously had no entry for Raynard. He is plot-important: his sexual hold over Kyra is the recurring weight in book 1's first third, and his death is the emotional pivot of the Kyra-Anjali relationship.
- His exact alignment is murky — he is "Ghost" in book-1 ch.2 (the client who hires Kyra and Greg for the Undercity Stardust escort) — Kyra realises this in book-1 ch.17 once she sees him among Kriath captives. (Confirm: "His presence here. The Reavers. ... You did this? ... The alliance was the price wasn't it?")
- His death lets Kyra recognise Anjali's love for her as something separate from Raynard's domination.
