# Aurean

## Identity

Kendall's husband. He insists on Kendall wearing the heavy armour suit even when inside the protected flagship. He manages the bridge when Kendall goes outside. He shows his character in a single exchange: pressing Kendall's armour replacement and whispering "Please come back to me" before Kendall goes into the battle.

## Physical

Slightly shorter than Kendall (noted he is slightly shorter). No other physical details.

## Voice

Private, devoted. His speech in chapter 27 is brief and raw: "I'm going down there, inspire the troops. The fleet is yours, my love." / "Please come back to me." 

## Goals

Kendall's safety. The fleet's success. Presumably wants to go home.

## Notes

- He thinks about Marie's death at Utopeia when Kendall prepares to go outside — it colours his anxiety.
- "Marie" is mentioned as having been wounded in the breach at Utopeia and then killed by a stray shot while being escorted to healers. It is not clear if this is Marissa's birth mother (Kyra's partner, also called "mom"); the manuscript names "Marie" as someone whose death Aurean remembers. This is a continuity ambiguity requiring clarification.
