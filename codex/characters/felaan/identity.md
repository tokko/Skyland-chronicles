# Felaan

## Identity

Part of the core companion group in the Groundlands. Wylah's partner. A skilled ranger and tracker — "expert tracker." He is calm under fire to the point of seeming cold. He fights with a hatchet as primary and carries a revolver and a single-shot smoothbore pistol. He died in the cave battle saving Wylah when Zandor fell — interposing himself while Wylah helped Zandor, giving his life. His body was never recovered.

## Physical

Swarthy complexion. Golden-streaked dark hair. Not described as tall; does not appear to be large in stature.

## Voice

Calm to the point of affectlessness. His reports are clipped to pure information: "I might have a concussion, and I'm bleeding freely from my side" — delivered with "his usual stoicism." He makes tactical calls clearly and without drama.

## Goals

Survive; protect the group. He succeeds in one (the group) but not the other.
