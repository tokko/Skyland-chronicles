# Wylah

## Identity

Part of the core companion group in the Groundlands. She is stoic, experienced, and decisive. She is Felaan's partner. She carries a bolt-action rifle and uses it with expert precision. She is described as "more or less unscathed" after the cave battle but is out of bullets. She separated from Marissa and Valia at the cave junction (chapter 22) to lead Zandor to safety, calling back instructions: "Meet up by the bikes. Just go if you can't lose them, the Groundlands must know of this!"

Felaan gave his life for her when she stopped to help Zandor after he fell.

## Physical

Silver streak in her hair. Described as "stoic" in appearance and bearing. Carries a bolt-action rifle. Her height is not specified, but Marissa and Valia are noted as "shorter girls" compared to the group.

## Voice

Clipped, tactical, decisive. She is the one who calls tactical commands in the cave battle ("One volley to make them hesitate"). Does not waste words. Her grief at Felaan's death is expressed by a shaking of the head and "wet" eyes for a moment before she snaps back to "We have to go."

## Goals

Survive; protect the group; report the savage threat. Presumably reached the bikes and the Groundlands ahead of the attack.
