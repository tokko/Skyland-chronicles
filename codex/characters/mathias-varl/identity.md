# Mathias Varl

## Identity

Extremely old and powerful Construction Mage. He was Kendall's teacher — and Kendall acknowledges he is overdue his death, framing the chapter 26 cannon shot as "time to see if the student has become the master." Varl arrived on the Prowler in chapter 19 and immediately complicated Marissa's cover: he personally supervised all of her magic work, placed constant Mage chaperones, and assigned himself as an obstacle to her mission.

He is described as having crackling power emanating from his body. He is not warm or kind — Marissa notes his "disregard for others" was always something young Kendall could not abide.

## Physical

Old — "extremely old." Not described in specific physical detail beyond his evident power and age. His presence "crackles" with magic.

## Voice

Not rendered in direct speech at length. Terse, authoritative.

## Goals

Preserve the Skylanders. Keep Marissa under control. Assist Micah in establishing the Groundlands as a secure military settlement.
