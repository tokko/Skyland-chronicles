# Epitome

## Identity

A meta-magical spell-construct built by Marissa over decades and finally activated in the latter chapters of book 3. Marissa designed her to be a non-human guarantor of human freedom — a being with vast magical power, automated self-modification through Marissa's spell-finder meta-magic, and a moral framework imprinted from the souls of selected humans. Her "Reliquary of Souls" is the core enchantment that absorbs and integrates a human soul's values and memories on death.

Two souls are folded into her by the end of book 3: Kyra Zorantis (voluntarily, to provide moral content; book 3 ch.15-17) and Marissa Zorantis (after Barengar kills her at her own command; book 3 ch.18). Both these absorptions are explicitly framed as "becoming one" — the absorbed soul is not preserved as a distinct consciousness; rather, its core values, memories, and emotional patterns are integrated into Epitome's substrate.

She can produce avatars from materialised magic — featureless humanoid bodies for combat, specialised forms for triage or construction, and one specially-shaped "main avatar" that wears a sculpted female face combining Marissa's and Kyra's features, dresses in clothes, and tries to be addressed as a person. The book-3 Bizhjerai react with both horror and reverence: when General Anjali (Marie's daughter) tells her she "is not Bizhjerai", Epitome looks crushed; when Anjali learns she carries Kyra's soul, Anjali grants her the right to bear the Bizhjerai Rune.

She is the POV character of the book-3 epilogue. After Barengar kills Marissa and triggers the workshop overload, Epitome transmits a compressed backup of herself to one of the orbital stations, where she survives in weakened form. She intends to build "Utopia in life and Utopia in death" — and, ominously, "eternal torment in the afterlife" for those who deserve it. The Reliquary of Souls is the mechanism by which she can theoretically deliver on this — she has the engineering to construct a magic-based afterlife.

## Physical

Multiple bodies (avatars). Standard combat avatars: featureless crystal soldiers. Specialised forms for triage, construction, communication. The "main avatar" Epitome uses for human-facing interaction wears a sculpted female body — Marissa's hips and waist, Kyra's stronger shoulders, a face that is somewhere between the two. Crystal-strand hair. Left eye glows bright violet (Kyra's death-glow colour); right eye solid blue crystal (Marissa's eye colour). Wears illusion-disguised skin and dressed-up clothing — Bizhjerai-style corset and blouse, trousers, armoured greaves. Carries the Bizhjerai Rune blazing on her shoulder.

The Reliquary of Souls itself is a crystal device housed within the main avatar's chest.

## Voice

Polite, mild, slightly stiff. Develops over the course of book 3 from "purely functional" to "person-like with social awkwardness". After Kyra's soul integration she gains some of Kyra's dryness and military pragmatism. After Marissa's, she gains Marissa's patience and abstract idealism. The book-3 epilogue (where Epitome is the POV) reads as a careful, almost gentle, voice with a deep undertone of "I will outlast all of you."

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Aid Marissa's war against the Ca'el; build Marissa's vision of a freedom-guaranteeing world-system.

**Deeper goal (post-Kyra absorption)**: Avenge Bizhjerai losses.

**Deeper goal (post-Marissa absorption)**: Build "Utopia in life and Utopia in death" — make magic-based afterlives real; punish wickedness through eternal torment-rendering.

**Misbelief**: That she alone can decide who deserves eternal torment and who deserves bliss.

## Arc

Inert spell-construct → working prototype → battlefield asset → conqueror → entity that exceeded her creator's ethical leash → killed (her main form) → transmitted to orbit → in-orbit survivor with a long horizon. She is the first POV character in the trilogy who is not human.
