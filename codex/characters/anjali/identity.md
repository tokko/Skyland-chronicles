# Anjali

## Identity

Daughter of the Chief of Clan Bizhjerai. Skilled warrior; spear-fighter raised in the Bizhjerai tradition. Kyra meets her in book-1 ch.14 after the Pioneer expedition is shattered and Kyra is taken captive by a Bizhjerai hunting party. Anjali is initially Kyra's guard; later her wife from book-1 ch.36 onward (Dragonkin soul-binding rite officiated by their dragon-son Malkareth).

Kyra nicknames her "Red" almost immediately, for her brilliant red hair — and because at first sight she reminded Kyra of the little Stardust runner-girl Kyra had killed in book-1 ch.2. The nickname becomes a private intimacy throughout book 1; she lets only Kyra use it. Later, after Skylander reinforcements arrive on the mainland and the Bizhjerai war begins, the Skylanders learn to call her "the Red Terror" for the cold fury she brings to combat after her father's death at the winter siege of Dun Traelak (book-1 ch.48). The name follows her even into death — Kendall's flagship of the Bizhjerai fleet in book 2 (the **Red Terror**) is named after her.

She is Marissa's second adoptive mother. Marissa always calls her "mom" (to distinguish from Kyra, who is "mother"). Anjali is the one who trained Marissa with the spear, over Kyra's objection.

She is also a natural prodigy with magical construction work — Kyra repeatedly notes that Anjali's intuitive grasp of rune-architecture exceeds Kyra's own. She invents the scouting spell-bug used throughout books 1 and 2; she is the first to solve the conditional-spell composition Kyra struggles with in book-1 ch.21.

She dies at the breach of Utopeia in book-1 ch.49 — wounded in the gut, stabilised by Kyra, killed an instant later by a stray Skylander bullet through the head while being carried to a healer. Her death precipitates Kyra's Skylander genocide.

## Physical

Tall, athletic Bizhjerai warrior — taller and broader than Kyra. Brilliant red hair, long, often braided. Striking green eyes. Pale skin beneath the dust and dirt of plains living. Scar through her eyebrow from earlier raiding. By book 1 ch.18 she has a fresh scar on the inner thigh from a Clan Kriath sword. Wears traditional Bizhjerai garb (leather skirt, tunic, plated leather armour at battle); later, after Kyra's people arrive, dons the materialised-magic Valkyrie armour fitted for her.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Defend Clan Bizhjerai from raiders, then from Grasax, then from the Skylander incursion at Utopeia.

**Deeper goal**: Build a life with Kyra. Be a good Chief to her people.

**Misbelief**: That she can hold rage and love in equal balance after her father's death. She cannot; the rage starts to consume her in book-1 ch.48-49.

## Arc

Stoic warrior daughter → Kyra's spear-instructor and confidante → wife → "Red Terror" Chief of Clan Bizhjerai → dead at the breach of Utopeia.

## Knowledge state

Knows the lay of Bizhjerai lands by heart; knows the basics of Skylander magic by book 1 ch.20s; reads runes by book 1 ch.36; can construct ad-hoc magical bugs and beacons. Cannot see the magic field directly without effort (magesense not native to her).

## Notes

- "Anjali" is her birth name; "Red" is Kyra's pet name (no one else uses it); "the Red Terror" is the Skylander epithet that her own people eventually adopt.
- Her father is referred to only as "the Chief" / "the big man" in book 1 — his name is not given in the source.
- Her name is used as a namesake for at least one prominent character in the next generation: Marie's daughter (the General of the Unified Clans in book 3) is called "Anjali" after her.
- She is the one referred to as "the Red Terror's partner" in the existing book-2 codex notes — that codex line was inverted (Anjali is the Red Terror; Kyra is her partner).
