# Grasax

## Identity

The ancient liche-Mage who held Dun Traelak for centuries before book 1. No one knew who he had been in life — Anjali tells Kyra he had "lived in Dun Traelak for as long as anyone can remember." A Mage who sacrificed his humanity for immortality by binding his soul into an enchanted dagger and feeding on the souls of captives.

He maintained Dun Traelak as a fortress within a permanent magical barrier, raided the surrounding Clans for slaves to experiment on and to feed his unlife, and ran a permanent host of monstrous spell-beasts (humans warped by his blood-magic experiments) that ranged the plains around the fortress. He also accepted "Outcasts" — Bizhjerai exiled by their Clans for irreconcilable crimes — into his service, turning them into shock troops for cross-Clan attacks.

Book-1 ch.20-31 covers his defeat. Kyra (with Anjali, Alissa, and Clan Bizhjerai-Ithlak united forces) lures a spell-beast against his shield, slips inside, infiltrates the powder armoury, blows it from inside, brings down the central shield-tower, and confronts him personally. He nearly possesses Kyra's body but is expelled when Malkareth pours his power into her. The defeat costs Kyra her right arm (she takes a crystal prosthetic) and burns half her face. The dagger that had contained his soul is retained by Kyra — it is the same enchantment she later uses to bind her own soul in book-1 ch.50.

A lingering soul-fragment of Grasax remains within the dagger after Kyra captures it. In book-1 ch.38 Kyra (foolishly, alone) attempts to ritually destroy that fragment in the cellar of Dun Traelak. The fragment partly possesses her, attacks Anjali through her, and is finally expelled when Malkareth gives her his last reserves. Mal nearly dies. Anjali is nearly killed. Grasax is fully gone after this.

His library at Dun Traelak survives him — Kendall, Anjali, and Kyra use it for years afterward as their primary reference text for advanced enchantment work.

## Physical

In life, presumably human — book 1 never describes his living body. As a liche he manifests as a robed figure with shrunken features and burning eyes; in possessed form, looks out through Kyra's own face with a cruel smile. Carries an enchanted staff in his ground-floor encounters.

## Voice

Cruel, archaic. His dialog through Kyra's body in book-1 ch.38 is dark and clipped: "Not anymore." Otherwise spoken-of, not spoken directly. No `voice.md` warranted.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Persist. Expand his unlife into greater power. Feed on captive souls.

**Deeper goal**: Conquest of all Clan lands; eventual elimination of any nearby Clan resistance.

**Misbelief**: That his power within Dun Traelak is permanent. Kyra's combination of Skylander magitech, Bizhjerai numbers, Ithlaki shaman tunnelling, and Anjali's spell-architecture defeats him.

## Notes

- His dagger and library are arguably the most consequential possessions in the entire trilogy.
- The book-2 magic.system overview's note on "liche-enchantment" can now cite Grasax as the original known liche; Kyra and (by extension) Marissa-via-Epitome are the second and third recorded.
- The structure of his soul-binding is the basis for everything Marissa does in book 3 with Epitome's "Reliquary of Souls".
