# Micah

## Identity

The driving force behind the Groundlands — the Skylander settlement's military and political commander. Former Guard Captain in the Skylands. He is the one who organised the survivors of Utopeia into a functional community. Marissa acknowledges that without him the Groundlands would not exist. He ordered the massacre of a farming village ("almost two hundred innocent people") near the Groundlands — the act that permanently destroyed Marissa's conditional goodwill toward the Skylanders.

He had Marissa imprisoned when suspicion grew. He ordered her to execute Alissa as a loyalty test. He placed Varl's Mages as constant chaperones for her magic work. He was not a Mage himself. Died in chapter 25 by Marissa's revolver — a heart shot.

## Physical

Cerise (pink/magenta) hair. Vast, scarred body. Burn scar on left jaw and neck. Very large. Does not carry a firearm (his last mistake — he approached Marissa with a sword).

## Voice

A powerful voice. Issues orders that others follow without question. Direct, forceful. No hesitation when making hard decisions (massacres, executions). Marissa notes he "brought a bubble of calm with him" in a crisis.

## Goals

Establish the Groundlands as a lasting Skylander society — by any means necessary. Believes the Skylanders can and must survive outside the Skylands. Will sacrifice individuals (farmers, Alissa) for the group.

## Arc

Starts as a suspicious but pragmatic authority figure. As Marissa's suspicion deepens, he moves from constraint to active antagonist. His death is not triumphant — Marissa felt she had to do it, not that she wanted to, except for the farmer massacre.
