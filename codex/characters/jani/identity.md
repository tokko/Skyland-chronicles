# Jani

## Identity

A Bizhjerai warrior, cousin (in the Clan sense) to Marissa. Daughter of one of Marie's siblings or of one of Marissa's other adopted-uncles. Marie's "second daughter" Anjali (the General) is **not** her — Jani is a separate Bizhjerai of Marissa's generation.

At her first on-page appearance (book-3 ch.9) she greets Marissa with a warm "Cousin!" and an unfeigned embrace, then embraces Barengar warmly too. She is a platoon sergeant by this point — her father, the previous platoon commander, was killed in the Ca'el war just before book-3 ch.9. She was demoted from her aide-position when her father died.

Beautiful: book-3 ch.10 has Barengar internally lamenting that they are both wearing armour so he can't feel "the softness of her curves pressed into my body." Dimpled smile. Green eyes. Red hair (Marissa-and-Anjali-coded; the source emphasises this). Personally fond of Barengar from their first meeting; the relationship deepens through their joint deployment with Epitome's fortress.

She is the Bizhjerai member of Barengar-Jani-Marissa triad at the Epitome fortress. Her loyalty splits: she is a Bizhjerai sworn to General Anjali's command, and she is Marissa's cousin and Barengar's lover. The source keeps her in this honest tension without resolving it; she chooses Barengar (and Marissa, by extension) when the moment demands.

After Marissa's death, the source closes with Barengar planning to return to Dun Traelak — "the memory of red hair and a soft smile called to me" — for Jani.

## Physical

Tall (taller than Barengar by a hair). Red hair. Green eyes. Dimples. Athletic build typical of Bizhjerai warriors. Wears Bizhjerai infantry armour with shoulder-rune.

## Voice

Bizhjerai-warrior register; warm with friends, professional with subordinates. Short sentences in command. Uses "cousin" for both blood-relations and close-Clan-kin. Calls Barengar by name; calls Marissa "Marissa" but addresses her as "cousin" once Marissa accepts the embrace. Profanity-tolerant but not foul-mouthed.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: End the Ca'el war; honour her father's memory; serve the Clan.

**Deeper goal**: A life with Barengar after the war.

## Notes

- Jani's father is the unnamed Bizhjerai officer killed in the Ca'el war just before book 3 ch.9 — her grief is fresh when she meets Marissa for the second time.
- She is a key relational character for Barengar's epilogue — the source ends with Barengar flying back toward her.
- Marie's daughter Anjali treats her as expendable (reassigns her command to Marissa's detail), which contributes to Jani's eventual alignment with Marissa over Anjali.
