# Anjali (Marie's daughter)

## Identity

The second daughter of Marie, Chief of Chiefs of the Unified Clans. Named for Marissa's adoptive mother Anjali — the original Red Terror — and a constant living reminder to Marissa of the woman whose name she carries. By book 3 (~35 years after the Groundlands fell) she is General of the Unified Clans' army, in active command of the war against the Ca'el. A few years older than Barengar.

Tall, dark-brown hair already shot with grey, pretty mouth set in a perpetual frown, eyes prematurely lined. She has been at war for nearly her whole career and it shows.

Her relationship to Marissa is sour. She uses "cousin" sarcastically — "Tell your dog to heel, cousin" — and calls Marissa to account for the eight years Marissa has spent in retreat from the war: "It just took you eight years to pull your head out of your fucking ass." Marissa, for her part, finds it galling that "such a sour woman bore the name of her mom."

She is, however, professionally competent. She accepts Marissa's Epitome force after a single demonstration. She grants Epitome the right to bear the Bizhjerai Rune once she learns of Kyra's voluntary sacrifice ("If you truly carry the Soul of Bás Marbhadair, then you may carry the Mark"). Her bitterness toward Marissa is personal, not strategic.

## Physical

Tall — taller than Barengar. Brown hair greying. Lined face. Bizhjerai officer's kit; bears the Bizhjerai Rune; carries a long sword and a sidearm.

## Voice

Bizhjerai-officer register: clipped, profane under pressure, dismissive of bullshit. Calls Marissa "cousin" sarcastically. Calls Jani by name when on duty, sometimes by rank ("Sergeant"). Calls Barengar nothing — does not acknowledge him to his face. Profanity comes easily.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Win the Ca'el war. Push the southern enemy back to their capital and break them.

**Deeper goal**: Hold the unified Clans together for her mother and after her mother's reign; protect Bizhjerai sovereignty against any external power including Marissa's.

## Notes

- The name confusion with Anjali (Kyra's wife) is intentional in the source. Both are Bizhjerai military leaders named Anjali; the younger is named in honour of the older.
- Her sister (Marie's first daughter, unnamed in source) is also a senior military officer.
- Jani is referred to by both Anjali (sister) and Marissa as "cousin" — the precise blood relation is not specified by the source but the Clan-cousin sense applies broadly.
