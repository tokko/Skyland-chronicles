# Alissa

## Identity

Originally a Skylander mage on the Pioneer expedition (book 1 ch.13-14) — green-haired in her original form. She was the only other Pioneer survivor Kyra encountered after the expedition scattered, and they trekked together through the Elweyr wilderness before being separated. Kyra grieved her as dead through much of book 1 ch.18+. Alissa survived independently, integrated with the Bizhjerai Clans (book 1 ch.32+ implied), and became a long-running shadow operative.

By book 2 she has worked for decades as the Bizhjerai's primary infiltration and counter-intelligence specialist; she also performed the liche enchantment on Kyra's dagger that bound Kyra's spirit (book 1 ch.51 references the work). She took Marissa in after Anjali died at Utopeia, acting as Marissa's "Auntie Alissa" and second-mother figure through Marissa's childhood and training years in Dun Traelak.

In the Groundlands she operated as "Alana Kelvari," disguised with blue hair, working as a turncloak. She killed Brengar (corrupt gate guard) in chapter 18. In chapter 20, when her cover was broken, she was brought to trial. Micah ordered Marissa to execute her as a test of loyalty. Marissa complied — a heart shot, quick and clean.

She had hidden a large jug of accumulative poison in Marissa's workshop wall nook (her last act of mission support). Her last intelligible words to Marissa hinted at this: "I should have killed you after I found that poison in the nook in the wall."

## Physical

Not described directly in body before the trial. In trial/death: long hair (colour in Alana Kelvari alias: blue). No other direct physical description found in manuscript.

## Voice

Not directly rendered; described from Marissa's perspective. Pragmatic, cold when necessary, warm with Marissa. "Good and well-meaning at heart, but cold and ruthless when she had to be." Her speech to Marissa about the Bizhjerai Rune ("I should have killed you after I found...") is the last thing she says.

## Goals

Mission: ensure Marissa completes the infiltration and the Bizhjerai fleet destroys the Groundlands. Long-term: probably the same as Kyra — end Skylander threat permanently.

## Arc

Arrives as a mystery (who is the blue-haired turncloak?), is identified as Alissa by Marissa, and dies at Marissa's hand as the price of mission continuity. Her death functions as the cruelest cost Marissa pays — she must kill someone she loves to prove she is loyal to people she intends to betray.

## Notes

- The poison she left was accumulative type — took days before symptoms became obvious, almost impossible to trace. Marissa deploys it in chapter 24 through the water supply.
- She performed the liche enchantment on Kyra's dagger (Marissa mentions this in backstory).
- Her death is the emotional wound that Marissa secretly compounds by Valia's death — she grieves both simultaneously, and feels guilt that Valia's death gave her permission to mourn Alissa openly.
