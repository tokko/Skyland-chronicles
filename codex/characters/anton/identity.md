# Anton

## Identity

A large Skylander mercenary hired alongside Kyra by Detective Ford for the opening raid on the Stardust drug-manufacturing site in book-1 ch.1. He is heavily muscled in a way that "could not have been entirely natural" — implying chemical or magical enhancement — and fights with a crowbar in one hand and a single-shot muzzle loader in the other.

He is overcome by berserker bloodlust during the raid; abandons his agreed partner-role with Kyra (she was to lead, he to cover) to charge from room to room slaughtering everyone in his path. Kyra revises her tactics on the fly and uses him as a distraction. Anton is shot in the back of the head by a Stardust guard's musket in the manor's grand hall and dies instantly.

He is a function character — his role is to establish the world of book-1 ch.1 (Skylander mercenary culture, the Stardust drug war, the Skylands surface architecture) and to demonstrate Kyra's combat improvisation. He has no arc.

## Physical

Tall, broad-shouldered, "twice as wide" as Kyra. Bulging muscles beneath a sleeveless vest. Carries a crowbar and a single-shot pistol.

## Voice

Single line of dialog only (book-1 ch.1 — howls of bloodlust, no real words). No `voice.md` warranted.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Kill Stardust dealers; collect mercenary pay.

**Deeper goal**: Bloodlust.

## Notes

- Anton's body is found in the manor hall. Kyra leaves him with the other dead.
- His enhancement chemistry is a hint that the Skylands has a sub-market in muscle-augmenting substances beyond Stardust itself.
