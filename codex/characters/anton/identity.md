# Anton

## Identity

A large Skylander mercenary hired alongside Kyra by Detective Ford for the opening raid on the Stardust drug-manufacturing site in book-1 ch.1. He is heavily muscled in a way that "could not have been entirely natural" — the result of an **artificial muscle graft**, a Skylander surgical-magical procedure that strips strips of donor muscle from cadavers (or, in the cheaper underworld variant of the procedure, from the still-living poor who have run out of other things to sell) and threads them into the recipient's own muscle bed alongside woven layers of materialised-magic fibre that hold the borrowed tissue alive on a slow trickle of mana. The graft delivers explosive strength on demand but runs hot under sustained load and can fail catastrophically (burnout, seizure, cardiac arrest, the donor tissue rejecting back into necrotic rot) when the recipient pushes past the graft's thermal envelope. The seam-scars across major muscle groups are diagnostic — graft-mercs carry visible surgical lines along the collarbones, shoulders, and inner arms where the dead-meat strips meet their own. Anton fights with a crowbar in one hand and a single-shot muzzle loader in the other.

He is overcome by berserker bloodlust during the raid — a known side effect of the graft once it's been driven hard enough to flood the system — and abandons his agreed partner-role with Kyra (she was to lead, he to cover) to charge from room to room slaughtering everyone in his path, including non-combatant Stardust cooks. Kyra, jaded by years of Skylander work and unsurprised by what graft-mercs do once the rage lights up, does not intervene; she revises her tactics on the fly and uses him as a distraction. Anton is shot in the back of the head by a Stardust guard's musket in the manor's grand hall and dies instantly.

He is a function character — his role is to establish the world of book-1 ch.1 (Skylander mercenary culture, the Stardust drug war, the Skylands surface architecture) and to demonstrate Kyra's combat improvisation. He has no arc.

## Physical

Tall, broad-shouldered, "twice as wide" as Kyra. Bulging muscles beneath a sleeveless vest. Carries a crowbar and a single-shot pistol.

## Voice

Single line of dialog only (book-1 ch.1 — howls of bloodlust, no real words). No `voice.md` warranted.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Kill Stardust dealers; collect mercenary pay.

**Deeper goal**: Bloodlust.

## Notes

- Anton's body is found in the manor hall. Kyra leaves him with the other dead.
- The artificial muscle graft is a hint that the Skylands has a sub-market in surgical-magical body modification beyond Stardust itself, and that the trade in cadaver and live-donor tissue is one of the city's quiet horrors. Grafts are expensive, illegal in the Guard, visible (seam-scars at the collarbones and shoulders; running hot — steam off the skin in cold rooms, visible flush when the graft engages), and require continuous low-grade mana from the recipient's Powerstone to keep the borrowed tissue alive. A graft-merc with a dead Powerstone has, within hours, a flesh-bed of necrotic strips inside their own muscle.
- The graft's failure modes (burnout, system collapse, donor-tissue rejection into rot) are part of why graft-mercs tend to die young; the bloodlust side-effect is the recognised reason graft-mercs are not hired for jobs requiring restraint.
