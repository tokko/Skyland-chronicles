# Kendall

## Identity

The most skilled Construction Mage known; creator of the Skymmer (telekinesis-based flying vehicle). He is not Marissa's blood relative; she adopted him as "uncle" during her decade with the Bizhjerai Clan after her mothers died. He trained under Mathias Varl (his "old master"), whom he now has complex feelings about — gratitude and contempt. He commanded the Bizhjerai fleet as Commodore aboard the Red Terror (flagship named after Kyra). He is married to Aurean. Described by Marissa as someone who "hates violence but will do what is necessary." He does not enjoy killing but ordered the Groundlands wiped clean of survivors in chapter 27 because he did not want a repeat of Utopeia (where Skylander survivors formed the Groundlands).

## Physical

Tall. Azure (blue) eyes. Crystal staff. Wears heavy Angel armour in battle (Aurean insists). Has a compact Angel engine/wing pack kept in a bridge compartment for rapid external deployment.

## Voice

Cultured, slightly arrogant in a non-condescending way — he is aware of his own excellence and that of his crew without looking down on others. Uses proper grammar. Affectionate diminutives with Marissa ("young one," "kid," "kiddo"). Military command diction is crisp; personal speech is warmer.

## Goals

Protect the Bizhjerai Clan and ensure Skylander remnants cannot reconsolidate. Long-term: build a better world with magic serving people. He wants Marissa to stay in Dun Traelak; he respects her choice to leave even though it devastates him.

## Arc

Present only in chapter 26-28 on-page. Functions as the human face of the Bizhjerai victory — someone who finds no joy in massacre but commits to it. His arc within the manuscript is a brief emotional closure scene with Marissa.

## Notes

- Invented the Skymmer — a telekinesis-based personal aircraft; design was later spied on and reverse-engineered by Varl.
- The Red Terror flagship is his ship; it is named after Kyra Zorantis.
- He carries the blueprints of a decade; Marissa copied all of them into a power core at his gift.
- He gave Marissa her summoning gems (both the original and the replacement in ch28).
- He has plans for Angel armour modification to repel boarders — but Marissa notes the designs were not yet implemented at the time of the battle.
