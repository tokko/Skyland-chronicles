# Voice — Zandor

## Register

Casual, warm, often self-deprecating. He makes jokes even in crisis — but the jokes feel earned rather than deflective. His emotional vocabulary is simpler than Marissa's but more direct; he says what he feels without the layers of irony she uses.

## Vocabulary

Physical domains — the vocabulary of a fighter and an outdoorsman. He does not use technical magic vocabulary (he's still learning). He uses "lads" as a group address. He teases Marissa with warmth.

## Syntax

Medium sentences, often with a trailing observation. He does not complete thoughts under grief — his description of Felaan's death is three sentences and then sobs. In his confrontation speech in ch25 he uses longer sentences than usual because he is reaching for arguments he has prepared.

## Tics

"Took you long enough" — his default greeting when someone is late. Laughs or chuckles at himself when he makes an error. Calls Marissa "Mary" throughout (her alias), then "Marissa" only in the chapter 25 confrontation — the name shift is the tell. "Fine" as a one-word capitulation when he loses an argument.

## What they avoid saying

He avoids talking about how much he loves someone until a definitive moment. He expresses love through proximity — wrapping his arm around someone, catching them mid-crash, cradling her to his chest. When he finally says it clearly it is because the moment forces it.

## Examples

```
"Took you long enough." — ch21, ch24; casual; the period is flat and comfortable.

"I believe in the Groundlands. I believe that our people deserve a future." — ch25; formal, deliberate; he has clearly thought about this; the repetition of "I believe" is uncharacteristic and marks the gravity.

"Fine." — ch25, when he capitulates to leaving the tower blast zone; single word; he resents it but he is not suicidal.

"Now and forever." — ch25 farewell; four syllables that carry the weight of the entire relationship.
```

## Anti-examples

```
"I understand that you've been through a great deal of trauma and that your choices stem from that pain." — therapy register; he would never speak this way.

"She smiled softly at him, and he knew everything would be alright." — he does not think in soft-focus; when he is uncertain, he says so.
```
