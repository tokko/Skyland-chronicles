# Zandor

## Identity

One of Marissa's closest companions in the Groundlands; her lover alongside Valia. He is a large man — the biggest in the group — who presents as carefree and jovially simple but is perceptive and more intelligent than his bearing suggests. He is learning magic throughout the manuscript (late to it, but progressing). He also knew Marissa's true identity (Bizhjerai Rune tattoo) since at least the same time Valia did — both kept silent, hoping she would choose their side.

In chapter 25 he confronted Marissa at the shield tower, refused to let her in, and was shot in the thigh. He did not betray her to the remaining Skylanders even then. His last on-page words are "I love you too, Marissa. Now and forever." His bike was missing from the ruins in chapter 27, suggesting escape.

## Physical

Tall — his longer legs put him ahead in runs; he is slower on stamina than speed. Large hands, prodigious physical strength. Green curly hair, green eyes. Wields a halberd / pole-axe as primary weapon. After chapter 22 he has a broken left arm (healed by end of chapter 23 period) from the cave battle.

## Voice

Jovial, self-deprecating, warm. Curses with less frequency than Marissa but without self-consciousness. Under pressure he shortens to single-word commands. When grieving he speaks in fragments. His philosophical speech in chapter 25 ("I believe in the Groundlands") is the most formal he ever sounds — he is reaching for words he does not usually use and it shows.

## Goals

Protect his companions. Believe in the Groundlands as a chance for Skylanders to become better. After Valia and Felaan's deaths, he grieves and hardens — but does not lose his capacity for love or moral reasoning.

## Arc

Starts as the warm heart of the group. Loses Valia (and Felaan). Is confronted by the woman he loves who intends to kill everyone he knows. Makes the principled choice to oppose her without harming her. Is shot. Let go. Probable survivor.

## Notes

- His statement "Now and forever" in ch25 mirrors Kyra's phrase — it lands as the most loaded moment in the confrontation.
- His moral argument ("sample size of one" used against Marissa's ideology in ch25) is the one she cannot fully rebut.
- The missing bike in ch27 is the only evidence of his survival; Marissa notes it as a "small twinge of hope."
