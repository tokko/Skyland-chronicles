# Valia

## Identity

One of Marissa's closest companions and lovers in the Groundlands. She was learning Construction Magic under Marissa's supervision. She was perceptive: she identified Marissa's Bizhjerai Rune (same as Zandor) when she tucked her into bed after Marissa exhausted herself on the plains, and kept the knowledge secret, choosing to love Marissa anyway.

Her death is the emotional centre of chapters 22-23: cornered on a cliff ledge with savages behind them, she kissed Marissa, told her she had always known who she was, then threw them both off the cliff to buy Marissa enough momentum to survive using her Angel armour. She wrested control of Marissa's Angel armour to push herself clear, giving Marissa all her power before doing so. She hit the treeline below and died on impact.

## Physical

Larger than Marissa. Metallic golden hair (shines in sunlight). Platinum eyes. Carries twin revolvers. Has a small sword (used in cave fighting). In the cave battle: used black powder grenades; improvised smoke/dust attacks with magic.

## Voice

Warm, practical, occasionally funny. Speaks with directness and economy. Her final speech to Marissa on the cliff is measured and sad-proud: "I've known since I saw your Bizhjerai Rune... I've known a long time who you are, and loved you anyway."

## Goals

Protect her companions. Learn magic. Be with Marissa and Zandor. She made peace with dying the moment she understood there was no other way out.

## Arc

Begins as the warm, capable member of the group. Carries a secret (knowing Marissa's identity) for a long time. Dies in an act of deliberate sacrifice that also functions as the most painful proof that she genuinely loved Marissa without reservation.

## Notes

- She was cremated with Skylander tradition (also ancient Bizhjerai tradition) after recovery; Marissa and Zandor attended in silence.
- The knife she stabbed into Marissa's shoulder to break her grip was later recovered by Marissa from the wound and returned to Valia's belt before the fall.
- Marissa's guilt about Valia's death: she didn't teach her Angel armour use — "I should have made time."
- She carried a jug of grenades into the cave battle; only one was used.
