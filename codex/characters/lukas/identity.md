# Lukas

## Identity

A Bizhjerai youth who designed the concept for the bike (magic-powered off-road vehicle used throughout by Marissa and her companions). He is mentioned as someone who was summoned along with Kendall when Marissa crushed the crystals in chapter 24: "Raistlin's captain Ioana, should be on her way. Lukas and Kendall likewise."

Not on-page in the manuscript; referenced only in backstory and in the summoning context. His current age and role in the fleet are not described.
