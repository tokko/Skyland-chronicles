# Voice — Kyra Zorantis

## Register

Authoritative, warm, pragmatic. She does not hedge. She does not explain herself unless asked. When she gives tactical advice it is blunt and memorable ("When in doubt, blow shit up"). When she addresses Marissa it carries a mother's weight — she will name hard truths but does so gently. She sounds like someone who has made peace with having done terrible things.

## Vocabulary

Military and strategic vocabulary: "defences," "fleet," "obstacles," "timing." Domestic endearments: "daughter," "love." She avoids euphemism. She does not dress up death — "she died because of her choice" not "she passed away."

## Syntax

Short declarative sentences. When she is angry or insistent, sentences get shorter. When consoling Marissa, sentences lengthen slightly. She never trails off — she completes her thoughts. She does not use questions to soften commands; her questions are genuine inquiries.

## Tics

"Daughter" as address — nearly every scene. "Now and forever" — the shared phrase with her dead partner, used only in moments of deep intimacy with Marissa. Dark chuckle when Marissa underestimates a problem.

## What they avoid saying

Regret. She has done terrible things — destroyed the Skylands, caused the deaths of millions. She acknowledges this with clear eyes and moves on. She will not say she is sorry for the war, only that she hopes it was necessary. She avoids sentiment about her own death, redirecting to what Marissa needs to do.

## Examples

```
"When in doubt, blow shit up." — tactical advice in ch21; the casual profanity is load-bearing; it shows she has distilled a lifetime of siege warfare into the simplest possible rule.

"Don't think about Alissa any more, daughter. She made her choice and she paid with her life for it. Just like we all will one day." — in ch21; brutal comfort; she names grief as a thing to be set aside for operational reasons without pretending it isn't real.

"You actually did it, love. You are about to bring the Groundlands down!" — in ch26 amid the ruins; her joy is ferocious, unsettling; she fought her whole life for this moment.

"Now and forever." — only in ch28 and in memory; carries the weight of her partner's death.
```

## Anti-examples

```
"She felt overwhelmed by the weight of all she had sacrificed." — she would never narrate her own feelings this way; she doesn't do emotional inventory.

"I just want you to be happy, Marissa." — too passive; she wants Marissa to complete the mission AND be happy; she does not separate the two.
```
