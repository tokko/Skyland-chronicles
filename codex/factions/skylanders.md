# The Skylanders

## Identity

The survivors of the destroyed Skylands — a colonial civilisation. They define themselves as a people with a shared culture and history. Marissa challenges this: she argues being a Skylander is an ideology (justifying violence for convenience) rather than a birth category. Not all individuals in the Groundlands were born on the Skylands; some are spouses and children of survivors.

## Goals

- **Stated goal**: Re-establish Skylander civilisation on the ground; build the Groundlands into a lasting society.
- **Actual goal**: Survive and rebuild; maintain Skylander culture and values.
- **Long-term vision**: Expand — the old colonial instinct does not fully die; the farmer massacre shows this.

## Structure

Military-civil hybrid. Micah as commander/governor. Mathias Varl as senior Mage advisor. Archibald as lead architect. Ms Vorka as housing coordinator. Angel Corps with a named colonel. Special forces contingent with bolt-action rifles. Common population of craftspeople, farmers, soldiers.

## Resources

At peak: ~11,000 people; a large dome shield; anti-air gun emplacements on eastern and western cliff faces; mortar batteries; rifled muskets and revolvers as standard arms; bolt-action rifles for special forces; Angel armour corps; the Prowler (steam Skyship). Power source: Marissa's artificial ley line network.

## Methods

Military discipline. Construction of fortifications. Magic-enhanced industry. Internal policing (Marissa's chaperones). Willingness to massacre non-combatants when deemed necessary (the farmers; Alissa's execution as loyalty test).

## Notes

- The farmer massacre is the moral inflection point for Marissa and the reader's perception of the Skylanders.
- Zandor, Wylah, and Marissa herself are the probable only survivors of the manuscript.
- Marissa's final label: "the last Skylander" — applied to herself, by ideology rather than birth.
