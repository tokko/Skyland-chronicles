# Clan Kriath

## Description

One of the Plains Clans before the Skylander incursion — adjacent to Clan Bizhjerai and Clan Ithlak. In book-1 ch.16, Kyra deduces that Clan Kriath has been brokered into an alliance with the Reavers (Outcast warbands) by Raynard (a surviving Skylander mercenary who joined Kriath and took their Mark). Kriath uses the Reaver alliance to step up raids against Bizhjerai, triggering the inter-Clan war that book 1 ch.17 documents.

Clan Kriath is **wiped out as a Clan** in the book-1 ch.17 battle — Bizhjerai-Ithlaki forces crush them. Surviving Kriath become Clanless (alone, possibly seeking reintegration with another Clan) or join Grasax's Outcasts.

## Allegiance

Brief alliance with Raynard (Skylander) and the Reavers. After their defeat, the Clan exists only as scattered survivors.

## Hierarchy

A Chief (unnamed, killed at the end of book-1 ch.17). The Mark of Clan Kriath was given to Raynard as the price of his alliance.

## Goals

(Pre-defeat) Raid Bizhjerai for resources. Establish dominance among the eastern Clans with Raynard's coaching.

## Notes

- The Kriath war's purpose for Raynard was strategic: stir up chaos that would soften the Plains for Skylander invasion. Kyra realises this when she sees Raynard among Kriath captives. Her execution of Raynard ends Kriath's leadership and the war's purpose.
