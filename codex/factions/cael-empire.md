# The Ca'el Empire (faction)

## Description

A southern Mage-aristocratic empire that the Unified Clans face in book 3. Led from the shadows by Zandor (Marissa's former Skylander lover, who survived the Groundlands). The Ca'el military is professional, well-armoured, well-armed (bolt-action rifles), and trains its own Mages. Builds in stone and metal; uses standard 19th-century-style siege artillery and machine guns. Wages war on the southern Bizhjerai frontier through book 3.

The Ca'el are the political "rival" book 3 needed for Marissa's story — a hostile power great enough to justify Epitome's deployment, but human enough to be relatable. Their soldiers, in book 3 ch.13-15, behave as ordinary fearful professionals.

The Mukhtar are tributary peoples of the Ca'el (book 3 ch.3 — the Qoch of Mukhtar acts as if he has Ca'el-level political authority).

## Allegiance

Independent; led by Mage aristocracy with Zandor at the apex.

## Hierarchy

- **Zandor** — the shadow leader; Mage of Skylander training; commands operationally though does not hold the throne.
- **Mage aristocracy** — hereditary; recruits Mages; staffs the officer corps.
- **Professional army** — trained troops; bolt-action rifles; artillery.
- **Tributary peoples** — Mukhtar and others; supply lower-ranking troops and labour.

## Goals

**Surface goal**: Expand northward; conquer or subjugate the Unified Clans.

**Deeper goal (Zandor)**: Force the world to acknowledge Skylanders had value; outlive Marissa's verdict that Skylanders deserved extermination; possibly to face Marissa directly in revenge for the genocide.

## Notes

- The "Ca'el" name is consistently apostrophised in the source.
- Their existence is the strongest hint that Marissa's "I killed them all" framing in book 2 was overstated — at least Zandor and a meaningful cohort survived.
