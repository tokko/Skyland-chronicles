# The Bizhjerai Clans

## Identity

In book 1: a confederation of nomadic Plains Clans — Bizhjerai, Ithlak, Kriath, Azwail, and others — sharing a language, Rune tradition, and herd-based economy. Pre-Skylander-contact they raided each other in seasonal cycles, suffered shared opposition from Grasax (the liche of Dun Traelak), and lived in tension with the more sedentary southern Cities.

The Skylander arrival forced them to industrialise. Across book 1 (ch.34-50) Kyra, Kendall, and Anjali rebuilt Dun Traelak as a manufacturing capital with an artificial-ley-line network across the Plains. The transition cost them their nomadic identity but earned them survival.

By book 2: the Clans are politically unified under Marie (Chief of Chiefs) and economically and militarily Bizhjerai-led with full Skylander-style magitech.

By book 3: the Unified Clans are an industrial polity with rail lines (book 3 ch.11), Skyships, an Angel corps, professional officers, and an existing decade-long war with the southern Ca'el empire. Dun Traelak has become a major city; the political seat shifts to Lahar (Marie's residence). They are described by Marissa as "generous and warm people" who took in a stray Skylander orphan. Their flagship is the Red Terror (named after **Anjali** — the codex previously had this wrong). Their warrior elite are the Valkyries (Angel armour users) trained by Kyra's methods, refined by Marissa and Kendall.

## Goals

- **Stated goal**: Peace; protection of their lands from Skylander reconquest.
- **Actual goal**: Eliminate the Skylander remnants permanently; ensure no revival.
- **Long-term vision**: Return to nomadic roots; live without the threat of colonial aggression.

## Structure

Clan-based. Kendall leads the fleet as Commodore. Named ground commander: Marie's husband (Aurean is Kendall's husband — the ground commander in ch27 is a different unnamed character referred to as "Marie's husband"). Valkyries as elite air-assault troops. Marines aboard the Skyships.

## Resources

A fleet of crystal Skyships (at least four in the assault: the Red Terror flagship, Fist of Heaven (Raistlin's contracted vessel), and two others). Angel armour corps (Valkyries). Ground troops with siege mortars and field guns air-dropped during the assault. Kendall's magical expertise. A library at Dun Traelak (where Kendall found the tome about the savages).

## Methods

Military precision combined with assassination and infiltration (Alissa's work). Kendall prefers not to use violence but will commit fully when necessary. The assault on the Groundlands is methodical: neutralise shields, destroy munitions, bombard systematically, then sweep for survivors.

## Notes

- Kendall's line in book-2 ch.27 — "there will be no survivors today" — is delivered with sorrow, not triumph.
- The Bizhjerai Rune on Marissa's shoulder is the physical marker of her identity — it blazed to identify her to the fleet during the aerial battle in book-2 ch.26.
- The transition from nomads to city-dwellers as a result of the war is noted as a loss for the Clan.
- Marie's elected reign as Chief of Chiefs is the political innovation that turned the Clans from a confederation into a Unified Polity. The reign begins in the post-book-1 reconstruction and continues through book 3 — about 50 years.
- The "Outcasts" sub-faction (Bizhjerai exiled for crimes) effectively dissolves with Grasax's defeat; many survivors are reabsorbed; a small minority continues as Clanless.
