# The Stardust Cartels

## Description

The criminal drug-manufacturing and -dealing network of the Skylands. Stardust is a white-powder narcotic with strong addiction profile (book 1 ch.1 implies it's the primary illegal drug; the cartels are the entrenched powers). Production sites are large mansions converted to manufactories, staffed by **forcibly retained women who work near-naked** (no clothing in which to conceal product) for twenty Coin a week; the cartel enforces continuous work by beating any woman who pauses, even under threat. The near-naked policy is enforced on entry — workers undress at the cooking-floor doors and dress again only when leaving — and the women have no recourse: they cannot afford legal Powerstones (priced thirty Coin a week with delivery), so the manufactory wage is the only path to a household power supply, which makes the labour pool self-replenishing. Distribution runs through Undercity Skyway connections and Upper-Skylands corner sales.

Multiple competing cartels exist; the Bowels-based Robin Hoods are a rival magic-power-stealing gang that complicates the market. The Constabulary leadership is corrupt and accepts bribes; Detective Ford's unsanctioned anti-Stardust unit using mercenaries (Kyra, Greg, Anton, others) is the only sustained law-enforcement pressure on the cartels.

Destroyed with the rest of the Skylands in book-1 ch.51.

## Allegiance

Independent criminal organisations. No external sponsor; pure profit motive.

## Hierarchy

Each cartel has its own leadership (Markus's lover was a Mage running one site; the "Ghost" who hired Kyra in ch.2 was a senior figure of one cartel). The cartels do not unite — they compete.

## Goals

Profit. Maintain Stardust supply chain. Resist Constabulary raids.
