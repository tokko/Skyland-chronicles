# Fists of Heaven

## Identity

Raistlin's mercenary fleet — named for his flagship, the Fist of Heaven. A steam-powered Skyship, grey metal, heavier armour than crystal Bizhjerai vessels. Contracted by Marissa (through Raistlin) to participate in the assault on the Groundlands. Ioana is named as the captain who should be approaching after the summoning crystals were crushed.

## Goals

Fulfil the contracted assault; survive; be paid.

## Structure

Raistlin as owner/commander. Ioana as fleet captain (or ship captain). Unknown number of ships beyond the Fist of Heaven.

## Resources

The Fist of Heaven — a steam-powered Skyship with a large prow cannon.
