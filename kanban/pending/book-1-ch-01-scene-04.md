## Writer notes
- **The source passage** (voice §Kyra anchor 1) has Kyra reporting body-facts one beat after they happen — "warmth splashed across my belly", "I rolled to my belly and pushed up to all fours". This is the model. The rewrite leans on it: honest, close, not romantic, not self-disgusted in an italicised way. The editorial lives in the gap between what her body does and what she says about it.
- **Raynard's characterisation**: he is physically dominant, cheerful in the way of a man who expects to get what he wants, and entirely uninterested in Kyra's inner state. He is not a villain-monologue; he is furniture that moves. His dialogue should be short and assume agreement.
- **The bills**: both Ford's payment and Raynard's contribution fold into the same coat pocket. This is the scene's quiet punchline — she has been paid twice for the same night. One physical action, no commentary.
- **The executions below**: Kyra walked past them on her way out. The prose acknowledges this as a fact — not as a moral crisis, not as a justification. The professional mask holds. The reader keeps the knowledge.
- **Anti-anchor (polish-targets §15)**: the scene is in scope but must not establish post-battle sex as the chapter's template. This is a single instance, situated in a long working career, not introduced as a formula.
- **Scene length**: this scene is the shortest of the four. The source's Raynard passage is short; the rewrite keeps it tight.
