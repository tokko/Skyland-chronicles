## Writer notes
- **The Skyway**: a tram-rail system running through the levels of the Skylands, powered by Powerstone conduit along the track. Kyra rides it alone, at the end of the post-midnight shift, toward the lower residential levels. The vocabulary is trade vocabulary: "Skyway", "Powerstone", "Barrier", "Geyser". No "vibrant city", no "bustling streets" (`ai-isms §1.7`, `§1.8`).
- **The Great Geyser**: the Skylands' power source — a volcanic-magic column rising through the monolith's core. Kyra can see its pressure plume from the tram window. This is the first visual reference to the thing she will later drain to kill the Skylands. No foreshadowing commentary; just the view.
- **The apartment**: indigo-crystal corridor, a single room, a tub that runs water from the magic dispenser on the wall. The dispenser tonight gives cold water when it should give hot. One sentence on this fact; no explanation of why.
- **The money**: Kyra counts it by feel in her coat pocket on the tram. The number is named (even if approximate — a wad, a count, a sum that means something to her rent and nothing to Ford). The two sources of the money are not re-examined; it is simply money now.

The beat is: a small, specific, apparently unrelated observation that reframes everything that just happened without naming the reframe.
