# Book 1, Chapter 05 — The Hand at the Door

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.5 (the hospital room with Kendall; the four-day coma; the "Dragonbane" news report; Kyra discharging herself) and source-book-1 / ch.6 (collecting Marissa from Greg's empty apartment; Kyra's mother's kitchen; the first night at the family home).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Kyra wakes from a four-day coma to learn she has been famous since the night of the dragon and that Greg has not been mentioned by the news. Kendall is at her bedside. The dragon was a magic-sink and she nearly died from being drained on its back. She refuses to stay in the hospital, dresses through her own corset's reflex armour, and crosses the city to Greg's apartment to find his eleven-year-old daughter waiting behind the door for a father who is not coming home. She takes Marissa to her own mother's apartment. The chapter ends with Kyra in her old childhood bed, a small body curling against her side in the dark. External goal: get Marissa to safety before the city's social-service apparatus loses her. Internal goal: discover that she has accepted the orphan before she has named the responsibility.

## Beats this chapter advances
- H-greg-dies-saving-kyra — the consequence beat. Greg dead → Marissa orphaned → Kyra carries her out of the apartment.
- H-the-magic-shortages — the dragon's drain mechanism named (Kendall's explanation). The reader now understands the supply problem as a strategic vulnerability, not just an economic one.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- _none_ — this chapter is the consequence-and-transition between H-greg-dies-saving-kyra (landed in chapter 04) and H-kyra-volunteers-for-the-expedition (landed in chapter 06). No new hard beat lands here; the chapter's load-bearing work is the relationship Kyra-Marissa-Kyra's-mother taking on its definite shape.

## Opening hook
The smell of clean sheets. Kendall's voice. Kyra's own hand reaching down to scratch an itch on her thigh and stopping when she realises her thigh is not where she remembers it being. The four days vanished without her permission.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra in the bed of her childhood. Her mother's nightgown two sizes too small across the chest. A small body climbing in beside her. The reader knows Greg is dead before the chapter says so — and they know this is the inheritance.

## Scene count (preliminary)
4 scenes.
- scene-01 — *The hospital room.* Wake-up. Kendall. The Dragonbane news in the green hololithic crystal. Greg's death replayed. The dragon-as-magic-sink mechanic named. Kyra refuses to stay.
- scene-02 — *The flashback (one of two).* Walking from the hospital to the Skyway, the crowd recognises her, hands on her shoulders, one hand on her breast — and the small body with red hair is suddenly at the corner of her eye. This is the **first** of the two planned stomp-the-girl flashbacks in Book 1. Brief, specific, physical: a child running across the platform with a paper bag, hair the wrong colour but the wrong-colour is the trigger. Kyra dives off the tram.
- scene-03 — *Greg's apartment.* Marissa behind the door. Kyra kneeling. The hug. The sandwich Marissa cannot finish. The case. The walk out, the door closed forever.
- scene-04 — *The mother's kitchen.* Kyra's mother seen on-page for the first time. The fungus dinner. The childhood bedroom. Marissa's footsteps in the hall. The reader understands the family now lives in this apartment, even if Kyra cannot say it yet.

## Writer notes
- **Greg is mentioned once in the news clip and once in dialogue.** The reporter calls him "the mercenary that was killed in the vid". The source has Kyra interject — "He was my friend" — and the rewrite preserves the line, voice-correct.
- **Marissa's idiolect.** Eleven years old. Pale skin, black hair, Greg's blue eyes. Speaks little; what she says is direct and structurally simple. "Dad's not coming home, is he?" is the source line and lands the chapter; the rewrite keeps it. She does not narrate her grief; she packs her case neatly and asks practical questions.
- **Kyra's mother (no on-page name — "Mom" only).** Mid-fifties, looks seventies. The grief of seven years ago (Kyra's father's execution) renders her physically smaller. She makes the fungus dish that was Kyra's childhood favourite. She accepts Marissa into the household without negotiation. Idiolect targets: warm, slightly faded, sentences finished but trailing.
- **Kendall and Kyra's mother are not in the same scene.** They have been working together for the four days of Kyra's coma. The reader infers this from Kendall's stubble. The chapter does not stage their interaction.
- **The Dragonbane footage in the green crystal** is a specific worldbuilding device — hololithic recording embedded in spell-stone. Voice-correct trade vocabulary; do not import camera or video terminology.
- **The "I killed a fucking child" interior beat** that follows the news report in the source is the one piece of dialog Kyra speaks to herself. The rewrite keeps the beat but compresses it into one short interior fragment, not a paragraph.
- **No on-page Raynard in this chapter.** He is mentioned only by absence — he is not at her hospital bed.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 7–9k words. Scene-03 (Greg's apartment) is the chapter's emotional centre.
