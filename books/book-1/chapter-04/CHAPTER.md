# Book 1, Chapter 04 — Dragonkin

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.4 (weeks of grief, drinking, men brought home; the Stardust raids that fall into routine; the rain on the Barrier; the Dragonkin invasion; Kyra's leadership of the musketeer column; the climb up Savistlin; Greg's death; the dragon's retreat).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Weeks pass. Kyra works a series of routine contracts that the chapter compresses into one passage — Ford, Darius, a small Stardust raid where she does not fire a shot. The grief settles into a working rhythm. Then the Skylands are invaded for the first time in living memory: rain hits the Barrier; the Barrier flashes and fails; gray scaled creatures pour through and a Mage-ridden dragon lands in a public square at the city's edge. Kyra runs toward the gunfire, takes command of a leaderless musketeer column from the special forces in training, leads them onto the square, climbs Savistlin's harness, kills four Mages on the dragon's back, and is thrown clear when the harness is cut loose. Greg charges the dragon to draw fire from her and is incinerated. The dragon retreats wounded into the night sky. Kyra cauterises her thigh and surrenders to the dark. External goal: fight the invaders, drop the leader of the Mages, save as many of her hired column as possible. Internal goal: be useful enough that the night does not let her stop.

## Beats this chapter advances
- H-the-magic-shortages — the Barrier overload that the Dragonkin exploit, named by Kendall later (in chapter 05) but seeded here: the Barrier flashed twice before it failed. The reader registers it as an external systemic stress.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- H-greg-dies-saving-kyra — full land. Greg charges to free Kyra from the dragon's fire and is killed by a Mage's bolt that hits his shield-arm. The chapter ends with Kyra wounded and falling unconscious; the codex updates Greg to dead.

## Opening hook
A bar in the upper district, Kyra drunker than the chapter wants her to be, slapping someone's hand off her shoulder, the small body never quite leaving the bottom of her glass. The grief has become a routine and the chapter has to compress weeks down to half a scene of it.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra alone on the trampled cobbles of the square. Two forms burning down the street. A wounded guardsman cradling his severed leg. The dragon a black shape against the sky. The clatter of armour approaching. Greg dead. Her thigh cauterised and her vision going. The next sentence she will hear is Kendall's voice in a hospital ward she has not yet reached.

## Scene count (preliminary)
5 scenes.
- scene-01 — *The weeks.* Compressed. Kyra at the bar; a Stardust contract with Darius now on her side; a small raid that does not fire a shot; the routine. Closes on the night of the rain, with Kyra outside admiring the lightning-blue rain hitting the Barrier with the crowd in the street.
- scene-02 — *The Barrier fails.* The first flash. The second. The boom. The rain hitting Kyra's face. The roar from beyond. The crowd's panic.
- scene-03 — *The Mage on the street.* Kyra climbs to a rooftop, sees the Dragonkin pushing the crowd forward and the Mage flinging spells from behind their line. She drops from the roof, sprints to the Mage, shield-breaker round through his shield, blood-poison through his chest, the kinetic round to finish. The Mage is young. The scar across his right eye is almost identical to her own. She turns to the line; the survivors form up; the first counter-charge clears the street.
- scene-04 — *The column.* Kyra finds the musketeers leaderless; takes command; her voice surprises her when it comes out as a sergeant's order. They form ranks, fire, charge, hold. Greg appears at her side fresh from saving a civilian family. The push toward the square.
- scene-05 — *Savistlin.* The square. The dragon. The Mages riding on its back. Kyra's musketeers join the elite special forces' firing line. The dragon leaps the line. Kyra rolls beneath it. Cuts the harness with her enchanted sword. Climbs. Drops the lead Mage and the woman who is about to set Greg on fire. Greg dies. Kyra is thrown from the dragon as another Mage's fireball lands. She kills that Mage with the explosive round at her last shield breaker. The harness slides loose; the remaining Mages tumble; the dragon takes its volley of holes and launches into the night sky. Kyra's thigh cauterised, vision going, the chapter ends.

## Writer notes
- **Greg's death is on-page.** The source uses one paragraph. The rewrite earns more, but not much more. Greg throws up his arms in a cross. His shield holds for a beat. The shield fails. He is gone. Kyra does not narrate her own grief in the moment; her body keeps fighting.
- **The young Mage with the scar across his right eye** is one of the source's signature touches — a near-mirror of Kyra. The rewrite preserves the resemblance and does not editorialise it. Kyra sees the scar; the reader sees Kyra see it; the chapter does not pause to name the parallel.
- **No flashback to the dead child** in this chapter — even though the source's later chapters use the post-Dragonkin convalescence to relitigate the small body. The flashback is held for chapter 05 (the hospital).
- **The dragon-as-magic-sink** mechanic is named visually here (the magic flowing from dragon to Mages, the dragon "shining like the sun" to Kyra's magesense) but Kendall explains it in chapter 05. The reader sees the mechanic before they hear the explanation.
- **Compress the source's "weeks" passage.** The source spends most of source-ch.4's opening on Kyra's drinking and the Stardust contracts. The rewrite gives this one scene that does the work in roughly 1,500 words. The chapter's bulk is the invasion.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 9–11k words. The longest chapter in the first arc of the book.
- **Saving the special forces is part of the scaffold but not the moral point.** The moral point is Greg.
- **The rifle is not in scope.** Kyra fights with revolvers and her sword in this chapter. The rifle Kendall promises in chapter 03 will arrive in chapter 06.
