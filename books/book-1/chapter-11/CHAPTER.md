# Book 1, Chapter 11 — The Outcast Raid

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.15 (the night raid: Kyra cuffed in the binding spell, Anjali freeing her on Kyra's oath, the run through the camp, the encounters with the flanking Outcasts, Anjali's first kill, the charge across the open plain to the enemy musket line, Kyra's spell-stone throwing soul-extraction, the rout) and source-book-1 / ch.16 (the immediate aftermath — fifty Bizhjerai dead, the funeral preparations, the realisation that the raid was a feint for an abduction of seventy of the Clan, Grasax and Dun Traelak named, the bath at the canal with Anjali, the long conversation about Outcasts and the liche).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
The horn. The camp under attack. Kyra cuffed motionless by Gaelon's binding-spell. Anjali bursts in, frees her on her oath, hands her an axe. They fight a path through Outcast flankers to Kyra's confiscated weapons; Kyra arms; together they run to the field where the Chief's main force trades musket fire with a much larger Outcast force. Kyra goes prone, picks off shielded musketeers, throws a soul-extraction crystal that destroys the centre of the Outcast line in a ring of crackling power. Kyra charges. Anjali charges with her. Together they break the line. The shaman calls fire from the ground; the Outcasts rout. Aftermath: fifty Bizhjerai dead, a similar number of non-combatants murdered in their tents — including Gaelon, who died defending women and children. The raid is revealed to have been a distraction: an abduction of seventy of the Clan, away to Dun Traelak. Grasax, the liche who holds the fortress, is named for the first time. Anjali rages; her father refuses to give chase. Kyra promises Anjali that they will go anyway, smartly, and that they will kill the liche. The chapter closes at the canal again — Kyra and Anjali bathing, Anjali finally sharing the story of her scar: Grasax's raid eleven years ago, her mother dead, her brother taken. External goal: survive the raid, free her hands, fight for the Bizhjerai. Internal goal: choose to belong to this Clan rather than to the Skylanders who would have been on the other side of any equivalent fight.

## Opening hook
Kyra on the floor of her tent, manacled by Gaelon's binding spell, gunshots in the dark beyond the tent walls and her own voice yelling to be let loose. Gaelon already running for the wounded — Kyra cannot know yet that he will die holding a door against seven men.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra and Anjali at the canal's edge in the late afternoon, both freshly washed. Anjali's scar visible. Kyra's body lighter than it has been in years — the blood-magic burn has taken weight off her. Anjali's voice low and even, telling the eleven-year-old story for the first time. The reader knows the chapter has closed the relationship's foundation and opened the trilogy's next motion: south, to Dun Traelak, in pursuit of an enemy older than either of them.

## Scene count (preliminary)
5 scenes.
- scene-01 — *The binding spell.* Kyra cuffed motionless in the tent. The fight outside. Anjali bursts in, frees her, hands her an axe. They emerge.
- scene-02 — *The flankers.* Three groups of Outcasts in the camp. Anjali's first kill — the perfect spear-thrust to the neck, the dazed look, Kyra's professional eye, the hand on her shoulder. Movement through the camp to Kyra's confiscated weapons. Kyra armours up.
- scene-03 — *The field.* The Outcast musket line on the open plain. Kyra prone, picking off shielded gunners. The soul-extraction crystal thrown over their heads — the ring of magical fire severing them at the waist. The charge. Kyra and Anjali back-to-back. The shaman's fire from the ground. The rout.
- scene-04 — *The aftermath.* Dawn. Fifty Bizhjerai dead. Gaelon found. The abduction revealed — seventy of the Clan taken. Grasax and Dun Traelak named. The Chief refusing to give chase. Anjali furious. The Chief's formal judgement: Kyra is a friend of the Clan, weapons surrendered again, escorted by Anjali.
- scene-05 — *The scar at the canal.* The bath. The blood. Kyra explains blood-magic. Anjali finally tells the story of her scar. The chapter closes on Anjali's voice, low.

## Writer notes
- **Anjali's first kill** is the chapter's emotional centre, more so than the rout itself. The line "That was my first / First what? / My first kill" is voice-correct and almost certainly survives the rewrite intact. The "very practised" reply from Kyra is the trilogy's signature register-shift; preserve it.
- **Anjali's scar story** is told in scene-05. Eleven years old. The mother killed. The brother taken. The Outcast knife meant for her mother's chest catching Anjali across the ribs. The rewrite respects the source's restraint — the scene does not dramatise the killing; it tells the story, plainly, in Anjali's voice.
- **The soul-extraction crystal** is one of Kendall's pre-departure gifts. The chapter is the first on-page use; the reader sees it work as Kyra throws it. Trade vocabulary; not over-rendered.
- **The Chief judges Kyra a friend-of-the-Clan, not a Bizhjerai.** The Mark and the formal adoption are held for Book 2. The chapter's title-image is the judgement, but the judgement is conditional and provisional — Kyra is on long leash, not enfranchised.
- **Gaelon's death** must be on-page in scene-04 — the source has Kyra find his body covered in wounds and place her breast in his still-warm hand, as a final acknowledgement of the man who had been staring at her chest for a week. The rewrite preserves this beat as black-comic and tender; the source instinct is correct.
- **No flashback. No "Time to X" closer.** The chapter closes on Anjali's voice telling her own story.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 10–12k words. The longest chapter in the book and the proper close. Scenes 3 and 4 are roughly equal in length; scene-05 (the scar) is shorter but emotionally weightier.
- **The Iolan / Clan Ithlak / Raynard-and-Kriath material is NOT in this chapter.** Source-ch.17 in the rewrite collapses into a brief coda at this chapter's end and does NOT land. See "What's leapt at the close" in `codex/beats/book-1.md`: the killing of Raynard at source ch.17 closes Book 1, but on the structural plan the Raynard execution is held over for the very end of this chapter's wrap or — preferably — handed off to a brief coda after scene-05. **Implementation note:** the Raynard execution beat (S-raynard-executed) is the book's closing image per `books/book-1/BOOK.md`. ## Spine flag (orchestrator escalation)
