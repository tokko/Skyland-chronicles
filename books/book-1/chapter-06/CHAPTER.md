# Book 1, Chapter 06 — The Rifle, The Pier, The Goodbye

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.6 (after Kyra has brought Marissa home — the morning at the apartment, Kendall arriving with the rifle and magazine system, the lesson, the new toys) and source-book-1 / ch.7 (the bar, the gem invitation to the First Expedition, the decision to go, the dock departure, the colonel's speech, Kyra kissing Marissa goodbye, Kendall pushing through the crowd at the last moment).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
A month has passed since the dragon. Kyra has not slept enough nights in a row. The Skylands have grown a pier through the Barrier and a Skyship — the Pioneer — beyond it. The First Expedition is recruiting volunteers. Kendall arrives with the promised rifle (semi-automatic with replaceable magazines and template-stones for spell-imbued rounds) and Kyra finally understands that he has known for weeks she will sign up. She tells her mother and Marissa. She makes her arrangements. The chapter ends at the pier — the colonel's speech, the boarding, Kendall arriving at the last moment with one more gift in a bag, Marissa's heartbroken scream of Kyra's name as the ramp pulls back. External goal: volunteer for the expedition, settle Marissa's care with Mom and Kendall, board the ship with her affairs in order. Internal goal: leave the Skylands without admitting why.

## Beats this chapter advances
- H-the-magic-shortages — the pier and shipyard built by withdrawing magic from "non-essential" districts of the city; one satellite Skyland decommissioned and consumed in the shipyards. The supply crisis is now visible at city-scale, not just at apartment-scale.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- H-kyra-volunteers-for-the-expedition — full land. Kyra commits, Mom and Marissa are arranged in Kendall's care, the dock-departure is on-page, the chapter closes on the Skylands receding.
- H-the-magic-shortages — the supply-stress beat lands here as the chapter that converts the ambient condition (threaded since chapter 01) into the structural motivation. The Skylanders sail because the supply has failed at city-scale: a satellite Skyland decommissioned, the shipyards built by withdrawing magic from "non-essential" districts, the pier itself grown through the Barrier at the cost of districts going dark. The codex records the shortage as the *cause* of the expedition.

## Opening hook
A bar table in the upper district. Kyra signing the invitation gem with a thought. The text scrolling across her eyes — *Welcome to the First Expedition* — that no one else in the room can see. The man at the bar smiling at her, hoping to be the next stranger she takes home. She tells him yes before she has put the gem back in her pocket.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra inside the gondola of the Pioneer, refusing to look out the viewport at the crowd. Raynard opening his mouth to speak. Kyra's glare shutting him up. Marissa's cry from the pier echoing in her ear long after the engines have caught. The reader watches the Skylands shrink behind the viewport she will not look at.

5 scenes.

- scene-02 — *Kendall and the rifle.* Kendall arrives with the wrapped bundle. The magazine system named and demonstrated. The two magazines — kinetic, template-spelled. The template-stones in the stock. The hug at the end. Kendall already mourning her departure.

- scene-05 — *The pier.* The crowd in the same square where the dragon died. The colonel's speech. Kyra called up. Marissa on her hip. Kendall arriving at the last moment with one more bag. The kiss to Kendall (the brother-kiss, not the lover's). Marissa's scream. Boarding.

## Codex state at chapter close
- Characters present: kyra-zorantis (aboard the Pioneer). Raynard on-page in the gondola. The other expedition mercs assembling around them.
- Location: aboard the Pioneer, leaving the Skylands westward.
- What changed for Kyra: she has left the Skylands. She has handed Marissa off to a small fragile woman and a queer best friend with a goatee. She has armed herself with a weapon no one outside the Special Forces has ever fired and no one anywhere has fired with this magazine system.
- Threads opened in this chapter: the Pioneer's exact return date (one month, with a three-day grace window before they give up and return); the Outside as a literal destination; Kendall promising to look after Marissa.

## Writer notes
- **The rifle and magazine system** is the trilogy's central piece of personal-scale Skylander tech. The writer is to render it precisely once, in scene-02, in Kendall's mouth — naming the magazine, the bolt, the template-stones, the spell-imbued rounds. Subsequent chapters refer to "the rifle" without re-explaining. This is the chapter that does the explaining.
- **Kendall's "I know you'd jump at the opportunity to go Outside" beat** must land — it is the source's clearest indication that Kendall has been thinking about Kyra's needs harder than Kyra has been. Voice-correct his lines; preserve the dry-tongue affection.
- **The kiss at the pier** — explicitly the brother-kiss, not the lover's. The source line "It was not a lovers kiss, but the kiss of a brother who did not truly believe that he would ever see his sister again" is the model. Keep the disambiguation.
- **Marissa's scream of "Kyra!"** is the chapter's closing beat and the trilogy's first true twist of the knife. The source has it land without prologue. The rewrite holds the same restraint.
- **Raynard is on-page in the gondola** at the chapter's close. The reader registers him as a returning problem; the chapter does not stop to comment.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 7–9k words. Scene-05 (the pier) is the chapter's emotional centre and may run longer than the others; scene-04 (the week before) is the shortest, almost a montage.
- **The same square where the dragon died.** Note the recurrence. The colonel chose it deliberately — the Skylands' propaganda needs a victorious symbol. Kyra registers this in passing; the rewrite preserves it as a single beat of irony, not a thesis.
