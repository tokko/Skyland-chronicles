# Book 1, Chapter 07 — The Pioneer / The Forest

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.8 (the voyage — boredom, card games, the murdered merc thrown overboard, the first landfall sighting) and source-book-1 / ch.9 (the first day on the ground with Alissa — the kiss, the stream, the small furry creatures, the hunt and the kill of the horned beast, the first taste of meat) and source-book-1 / ch.10 (the second day — the boat-meat experiment, the water-creature catch, Kyra warding the camp at night, the cooking-fish discovery).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
The voyage of the Pioneer: boredom, the murdered merc no one will own up to killing, the moment the lookout calls land. Kyra and Alissa volunteer (the colonel picks them) for the first drop into the forest. Kyra rappels down before Alissa can even reach the wire. They spend three days walking through the Elweyr forest — water as a fact, soil as a fact, a small bright-winged creature on a flower as a fact, the hunt and kill of a horned grazer, the first taste of meat in Kyra's life. Alissa kisses Kyra after the squirrel — a quick, light moment Kyra returns and lets pass. They learn that cooked meat keeps and raw meat does not. They learn that the wilderness is unbroken and unafraid. External goal: gather knowledge for the Skylands, stay alive, eat real food. Internal goal: stop being the woman who killed a child in the dark long enough to see something growing.

## Beats this chapter advances
- _none directly_ — this is the trilogy's pivot from city to plains. The chapter is the bridge from the Skylands to the Bizhjerai material. No hard beat lands; the chapter's job is to render the forest and the partnership with Alissa as the reader's emotional anchor for the next several chapters.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- S-the-airship-falls — landed in **modified form**. The source's soft beat allows reshaping the mechanism for getting Kyra into Bizhjerai hands; the load-bearing endpoint (Kyra captive among the Bizhjerai, no working return) is what the beat actually requires. The rewrite reshapes it as follows: the Pioneer drops Kyra and Alissa as planned in this chapter; the ship continues east to drop other teams. The reader does not see the Pioneer attacked on-page. By the time Kyra is in Bizhjerai hands (chapter 09), the three-day grace window has expired; the Pioneer is presumed lost (the Skylands later confirm none of the other teams returned either — this lands as backstory in Book 2). The "fall" is off-page; what survives on-page is the *fact* that the airship does not come back. The orchestrator may revisit this during outlining if the user wants the airship's destruction dramatised in-book; the current plan keeps the airship-fall as off-page rumour and uses the Bizhjerai capture (chapter 08) as the active mechanism.

## Opening hook
A hammock on the Pioneer's lower deck, Raynard at the next post over, the corpse of a man no one will admit to killing wrapped in a tarp and dragged toward the cargo door. Kyra not asking which of the mercs did it. The chapter does not give the dead man a name.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra and Alissa under the same blanket, Kyra mostly bare from her armour dissolving for the night, the pine-branch bed under them. The stream's water audible. Tomorrow they will walk further west. The chapter ends on the small physical fact of falling asleep next to a person who is not Kyra's enemy — and the reader knows what kind of book this is now.

## Codex state at chapter close
- Characters present: kyra-zorantis, alissa (asleep under one blanket).
- Location: a forest camp by a stream in the southern Elweyr.
- What changed for Kyra: she has tasted meat. She has touched soil. She has kissed a woman on the corner of the mouth in the woods. She has stopped — for one evening — being the woman who shot the small girl.
- Threads opened in this chapter: the forest as a body of fact, not a body of myth; alissa as the second person who calls her by her name; the wide magic-field of the Outside (which makes the forest feel like home and the Skylands feel like a furnace consuming itself).

## Voice / polish-targets specific to this chapter
- **Anti-anchors to suppress.**
  - `polish-targets §15` — the kiss at the stream is **not** post-battle sex. It is a one-beat moment Alissa initiates after the squirrel. Kyra returns it and lets it pass. The night under the blanket is platonic. No sex scene in this chapter. The chapter is the trilogy's first peaceful sequence and must not be defaulted into a cool-down register.
  - `voice §Kyra anti-anchor 2` — Kyra's voice will want to monologue about how strange the forest is. The rewrite holds her to noticed-specifics, not abstractions about the strangeness.
  - `polish-targets §17`, §18 — "suddenly" and "of course" are at high risk here, especially in the first-meat passages. Cap to one per scene.
- **AI-isms with elevated risk.**
  - `ai-isms §1.7 bustling`, `§5.4 "the land of"`, `§5.10 map-tour establishing shots` — high risk on the forest-establishing sentences. Substitute with what Kyra physically can see at the moment she sees it.
  - `ai-isms §6.13 "sunlight streamed through the window, casting [X] shadows"` — high risk for any morning-in-the-forest opener. Replace with what Kyra hears (the chittering of the small furry animal that means squirrel) or what she smells (the pine needles).

- **Specific Kyra voice anchors this chapter leans on.**
  - `voice §Kyra anchor 6` — the small life details landing alone on their lines. Kyra's first description of meat in her mouth should be one short, specific sentence that doesn't editorialise. "Fat dripped down my chin and I did not care" kind of register.

## Writer notes
- **Alissa idiolect.** Bright, naive register; smiles at small animals; calls Kyra "Dragonbane" in a way that is half-awe, half-joke. Her grief (her parents killed by the Skylands' government) is named once at the conversational level in scene-03 or scene-04, but the chapter does not dramatise it. Sentences shorter than Kyra's; vocabulary plainer. Alissa is the foil that lets Kyra's voice sound diagnostic by contrast.
- **The murdered merc on the Pioneer.** The source establishes the mercenary code by having no one identify the killer. The rewrite preserves this — the dead man is not named, his killer is not found, the body goes overboard, and life continues. The reader registers the moral landscape without comment.
- **The kiss at the stream is one beat.** "Their lips met" or any romance-novel formulation is forbidden. The kiss is closer to a peck — Kyra impulsive, Alissa laughing. The voice acknowledges Alissa's body in the source ("supple body against my back") but the rewrite treats Alissa as a colleague Kyra finds attractive, not as a romantic object. The Anjali relationship is what the trilogy is building toward, not this.
- **No flashback to the dead child in this chapter.** Even though Alissa's hair is dyed green (not red), the chapter holds back the trigger. The flashback is held for chapter 08, when Kyra meets Anjali at spearpoint.
- **The forest must be specific.** The source uses generic phrasing for many of its plants and animals (Kyra does not know the names). The rewrite leans into her not-knowing: she calls the squirrel "a small furry creature with a fluffy tail" and only later does she recover the word from old lore. This is character-voice and it must be preserved.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 8–10k words. The chapter's bulk is in the two days on the forest floor (scenes 3 and 4); scene-01 (the voyage) is compressed.
- **No on-page bear.** The bear attack belongs to chapter 08. This chapter ends before that fight.
