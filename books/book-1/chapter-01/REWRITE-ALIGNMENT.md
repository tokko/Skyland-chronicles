## Scene map (5 scenes — the outline container, repopulated from source)

### scene-01 — The breach
Stakeout in the indigo alley: two fingers, Anton on the far wall (graft warming). The explosion-signal; both swing round. Kyra headshots the right door-guard; Anton lung-shots the left then crowbars him. Lock/hinges melted, door kicked in, thrown-crystal flash; breach. First room: four blinded men killed (sword, Anton's pistol+crowbar, blade to temple), plus a fifth gunman whose shot the gauntlet shield eats — Kyra lung-shots him. Anton succumbs to graft-flood and charges off-script (he was to watch her back); Kyra curses, adapts (he is now a distraction; she picks targets). She follows him deeper toward the cooking smell.
ENDS: Kyra moving after Anton toward the cooking room.

### scene-02 — The cooking floor
The Stardust cooking room: near-naked women at a long table, chemical reek. Anton kills one (throat-grab, head dashed into the crystal wall) and advances on the huddle. Kyra magically destroys an interior locked door (holstering her revolver to do it); gunfire — a woman gut-shot. A club-man attacks Kyra (her hand empty post-holster); the duel; she kills him (hilt-smash, then sword through the mouth/skull). Anton finishes his three (eye-burst, temple). They press deeper — and Anton is shot through the back of the head, killed instantly, brains spraying Kyra's exposed face. She takes in the stair-and-gantry hall revealed beyond.
ENDS: Anton dead; Kyra facing the three musketeers in the hall.

### scene-03 — The gantry & the target room
The large stair-and-gantry hall: three muzzle-loader men face Kyra; a second breach party on the gantry fires down (their smoothbores as likely to hit her); she shields, then shoots the survivors. She salutes the gantry with her sword; the **golden-haired blond merc** smiles. "Follow me. I'll tank, you shoot… fix your bayonets." Joined by the **braided-redhead spearwoman + two-pistol friend**. The double-door target room: men behind a row of near-naked **human-shield** women; the line collapses into chaos; many women die. The squad fights through; the **blond merc bayonets a man behind Kyra and is then headshot dead**; Kyra burns a man's face with the gauntlet and kills him; the **redhead executes the surrendering man** ("No please…"). Cleanup (the wounded killed). Kyra to the window: Ford at the dry fountain with captives below; she pulls her helmet, opens the window, dissolves the combat preset to casual (bra/thong/coat) to cool off. From the window she watches mercs begin to rape captives in the courtyard and notes it without intervening.
ENDS: Kyra at the window, cooling, the courtyard below.

### scene-04 — Raynard at the window
Raynard arrives ("Oh, what a prize I have found." / "There is nothing of value here, Raynard."). The kill-count exchange. The encounter at the window (courtyard mercs cheer); he takes her hard, she taunts his size, he backhands and chokes her, she nears climax, he deliberately pulls out and finishes on her, denying her ("That was for calling my dick small."). She kicks him; the keep-letting-him barb; he pockets bills in her coat (groping), calls her a whore, leaves — his hold abates only as the door closes. She cleans up with a dead woman's underwear, her wrecked bra, the merc-code reflection, sees the braided redhead enthusiastically riding a merc and feels envy; swears off Raynard knowing she'll fail. She collects the second half from Ford (his eyes stay on hers, then dip once; he does not pretend he didn't). As she walks out, Ford orders the captives executed — a string of shots, women included (deniability). At the gate: the cannibal-economy reflection (grinder/fungus, butchered for the rich, **Dad**, **Mom**, the Apocalypse). She does not turn.
ENDS: through the gate, the second shot ringing behind her.

### scene-05 — The ride home
Three blocks to the Skyway. The Guard watching her Powerstone by the mana-stream; she does not Drain. The tram launches from the Skylands' underside — the Barrier (brilliant blue), the ocean far below, the Great Geyser, a satellite Skylands drifting past, the daydream of moving Mom there. Home in a shady ward, hand on the backup the whole way. The apartment, the dead/idle home dispenser, the recharge cradle for spent casings (metered cost tomorrow). The expensive bath; muscles letting go one at a time; the Raynard soreness; the Powerstone reflection (Soul-Bound, can't be stolen, Kendall's 25th-birthday gift). The flat grey melancholy after the rush. One observational pacing-brake. Bed near dawn; the choker stays on.
ENDS: she goes to bed; the choker stays at her throat.
