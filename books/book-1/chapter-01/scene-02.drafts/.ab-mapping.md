## Test parameters

- **Brief**: `books/book-1/chapter-01/scene-02.brief.md` (shared, identical inputs).
- **Output paths**: `draft-1-A.md` and `draft-1-B.md` in this directory.
- **Reviewer**: Opus 4.7 reads both blind, scores both against the same criteria, picks a winner. The losing draft is archived; the winner becomes `draft-1.md` and continues through the standard pipeline.
- **Lock-in**: after scene-02 the winning model is locked as the scene-writer default for all subsequent scenes (per CLAUDE.md Pipeline parameters, "Pending model A/B test").
- **One-shot**: the A/B runs once, on scene-02 only. Not repeated.

## Why blind

To remove the orchestrator's and reviewer's prior beliefs about model identity from the scoring. The reviewer reads two pieces of prose, scores them on the same criteria, picks one. The orchestrator opens this file only after the verdict, applies the lock-in, and notes the decision in `STATUS.md`.
