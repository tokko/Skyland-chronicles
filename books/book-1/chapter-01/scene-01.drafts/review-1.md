## Continuity check
**Fail — one inventing-of-entity violation.**

- **Para 37**: "Ford's contract was the merchandise in the inner room and the ledgers **Micah** kept in there — not the workers." Micah is a canonical character (Guard Captain → Groundlands commander, book 2), and Kyra's history.md lists no Stardust-cartel involvement for him. Inventing him as the cartel ledger-keeper of this manufactory is a forbidden invention per CLAUDE.md guardrails. It also actively creates a continuity contradiction with book 2, where Micah is the Skylander Guard officer who trained Kyra and later commands the Groundlands. Either remove the name (use an unnamed cartel boss, leave it generic), or pre-clear a Micah cartel-tie with the user. **Cleanest fix**: cut the name.
- Brief lists Kyra's kit as "regular revolver and a heavy revolver, sword, the Powerstone on her gorget, Kendall's repeating revolver as well." Draft mentions only "the repeating revolver" and "the sword" and a "shield-breaker" on her belt. Soft miss; not a contradiction, but a kit-tracking gap.

## Voice fidelity
**Drift toward spectral-Kyra register.**

- **Zero profanity in 1,847 words.** `voice.md` §Book-1 addendum: "Profanity is constant and casual; it is voice-correct." A breach-and-clear scene with no "fuck", "shit", or "asshole" anywhere is a clear voice miss. The breach should sound like a working merc on the job, not like the spectral Kyra of books 2-3.
- **No self-deprecating height tic.** Voice.md addendum lists this as a recurring Kyra tic. "I am not a narrow woman" gestures at the inverse but does not fish the joke.
- **No "of course" or "I dunno" tics.** Voice.md addendum lists these as concessive throat-clearings characteristic of living Kyra.
- **"Hope is a more powerful drug than Stardust"** (para 41) — too aphoristic, edges into the philosophical-spectral Kyra register rather than the working-mercenary one.
- **What lands well**:
  - "I was not going to tell him I was using it on a Stardust job. He would have opinions." — voice-correct dry editorialising.
  - "He was twice as wide as I was and I am not a narrow woman." — diagnostic body-fact.
  - "Ford was a good detective and a mediocre intelligence source." — clean Kyra assessment-of-others beat.
  - "Anton stopped when he stopped. Not when the threat stopped." — voice-correct kill-beat cadence.
  - "I didn't stop." — voice-correct grief/realisation fragment.

The cadence and sentence-shape work is mostly right. The lexical register is too clean.
