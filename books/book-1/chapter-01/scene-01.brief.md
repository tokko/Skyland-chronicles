## 1. Identity

- **Book / Chapter / Scene**: Book 1 *Stardust* / Chapter 1 *The Stardust Raid* / Scene 01
- **Working title**: The Breach
- **POV character**: Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close)
- **Setting**: Skylands Undercity, mid-level indigo-crystal manufactory; an unspecified Skylander year, late evening of an unsanctioned drug-raid contract for Detective Ford
- **Target length**: 1,800–2,400 words
- **Mood / tone**: professional, diagnostic, body-facts noticed one beat late; the Skylands seen by someone who has lived in it long enough to stop seeing the crystal as exotic

This is the **canary** scene — the first scene committed on this branch. After it is drafted, reviewed, and committed, the queue pauses for user sample-read. Voice fidelity must land on the first pass.

## 2. Scene goal

- **External**: Kyra detonates the Stardust manufactory entrance, fights through the corridor guards, and reaches the cooking floor with the breach team still functional.
- **Internal**: Kyra keeps the professional accounting intact — read the room, spend the fewest bullets, leave her own feelings nowhere they can slow her down. By the end of the scene the reader knows she succeeds at the tactical and not quite at the rest.

## 3. Resolution rules

**Must resolve in this scene:**
- Kyra's voice, method, and professional register are on the page from the first sentence.
- The breach is broken into rhythm units — by lighting change, by room, by adversary — not delivered as a single unbroken block.
- Anton's enhancement is visible and his bloodlust is foreshadowed without being named as such.
- The Powerstone and the gauntlet are named as Kendall-made; Kendall is referenced off-page only (a gift, an engineering habit, a name Kyra uses internally).
- The cooking-room workers are present; Kyra registers them as a tactical complication before she registers them as people.

**Must NOT resolve (carry forward):**

- Any introduction of Raynard — Raynard is scene-03.

- The "dead child" flashback — held entirely for chapter 02.

## 5. Characters in scene

### Kyra Zorantis (`kyra-zorantis`) — POV

- **Current state**: Approximately late twenties. Petite, short for a Skylander, with curly purple hair (dyed) to her shoulder-blades. Multiple scars from earlier mercenary work. No active injuries. Carrying full kit: materialised-magic armour (corset, gorget, helmet, gauntlet), a regular revolver and a heavy revolver, sword, the Powerstone on her gorget, Kendall's repeating revolver as well. Boots, sound knees. Mind at full working capacity.
- **Goal entering**: complete the contract, get paid, get clear. The professional accounting.
- **Goal exiting**: same — but Anton's behaviour has just become a problem she will have to solve.
- **Knows**: the manufactory layout (Ford's intel); Anton's name; Anton's enhancement chemistry is implied (she has not been told outright); Detective Ford is in the courtyard waiting for the all-clear; her payment is contingent on a clean job. She knows the Stardust trade exists because the Skylands' legal magic supply is priced out of reach of the people doing the cooking.
- **Doesn't know**: that this raid will be different from her ordinary contracts. She does not know it; the reader will not be told it; the body knows.
- **Voice**: see `codex/characters/kyra-zorantis/voice.md` — Book-1 addendum. The living-Kyra voice: rougher, more profane, sexually direct, snide about her own past. Short SVO, declarative, no euphemism. Comma-spliced compound sentences for description; sharp fragments for kills.

### Anton (`anton`) — Enhanced muscle / berserker

- **Current state**: Tall, broad-shouldered, "twice as wide" as Kyra. Bulging muscles beneath a sleeveless vest. Carries a crowbar and a single-shot pistol. Heavily enhanced — chemical or magical, Kyra does not know which.
- **Goal entering**: kill Stardust dealers, collect pay. Controlled at the start.
- **Goal exiting**: still controlled, but the bloodlust is starting to rise. Kyra registers this as a professional problem.
- **Voice**: one line at most. No dialogue tag carousel. He howls more than he speaks.

## 6. Codex slice (verbatim)

### Location — Skylands Undercity (`codex/locations/skylands-undercity.md`)

> The lower levels of the Skylands monolith — caves and tunnels carved or grown beneath the upper-surface city. Dimly lit by sparsely placed glow globes with long dark sections between them. Most of the fungus farms that fed the city were down here; most of the poor lived in residential tunnels above the farm levels.
>
> Distinct sub-levels:
> - **Upper Undercity** — residential; tunnel apartments, fungus markets, tram stations.
> - **Lower Undercity** — fungus farms, illicit markets, brothels (e.g. Mama June's), Stardust manufacturing sites, gang territories.
> - **The Bowels** — deepest tunnels; worst territory; where bodies and the most desperate disappear.
>
> The Skyway tram station connected the Undercity to the Surface and to the wider city; the trams ran underneath and out along the magic stream that supported the city.

**For this scene**: the manufactory is in the **Lower Undercity**. Indigo-crystal walls (the substrate of the entire Skylands monolith). Lit by Powerstone sconces in places, by nothing at all in others. The corridor approach is dark — Kyra holds up two fingers in shadow, Anton on the far wall. The cooking-floor room is bright with whatever the women are using to keep the Stardust process visible.

### Faction — Stardust Cartels (`codex/factions/stardust-cartels.md`)

> The criminal drug-manufacturing and -dealing network of the Skylands. Stardust is a white-powder narcotic with strong addiction profile. Production sites are large mansions converted to manufactories, staffed by underpaid (often forcibly retained) women who work near-naked to prevent theft of the drug.

**For this scene**: the manufactory is one of the production sites. The women on the cooking floor are part of the staffing model — not enslaved, not free. Kyra registers them as a tactical complication before she registers them as people.

### Magic — Materialised Magic + Powerstones (composite)

The Skylander mercenary's combat magic runs through a Powerstone (a charged crystal) seated in their armour. The Powerstone holds magical energy; the armour (corset / gorget / helmet / gauntlet) is materialised-magic construction, grown to the wearer's body. Weapons are produced or enhanced through the gauntlet — a magical jet that pushes a bullet, or a hand-summoned blade. Skylander revolvers are crystal-stocked, repeating where Kendall has bespoke-built them. The Powerstone is finite — Kyra burns through her reserve as she fights. This scene shows the gauntlet's hum-and-spend, the Powerstone's slow drain. Cost: each spell or shot draws from the Powerstone; running out leaves her with her sword and her wits.
