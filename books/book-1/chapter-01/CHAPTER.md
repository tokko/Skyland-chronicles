# Book 1, Chapter 01 — The Stardust Raid

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.1 (the entire breach, plus the post-battle window scene with Raynard and the Skyway ride home).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Kyra leads a breach for Detective Ford against a Stardust manufactory in the indigo-crystal undercity. She works the room with professional economy, kills cleanly, watches her hired muscle (Anton) lose himself in bloodlust, and walks out alive with a wad of bills in her coat pocket. The post-battle encounter with Raynard at the open window — and the long Skyway ride down past the Geyser to her own tub — closes the chapter on the dissonance the trilogy will spend three books trying to resolve: she hates the man, she does not stop the man, she goes home and runs a hot bath alone. External goal: finish the contract, get paid, get clear of the burning house. Internal goal: do not feel anything that will follow her into next week's work — and discover, by the chapter's end, that she has failed at that without knowing it yet.

## Opening hook
Two fingers held up in the dark of an indigo alley. Anton on the far wall, waiting. The detonation comes; the guards turn; Kyra puts the first round through the back of a head and the body is on the ground before the chapter has named her.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra in her own tub, water cooling. The bills counted. The bruise on her throat. The taste in her mouth that she cannot quite place. Sleep — but not yet rest.

## Writer notes
- **Canary scene reminder.** The first scene of this chapter (the breach) is the trilogy's canary. Per CLAUDE.md, after the codex update and the git-haiku commit, the queue pauses for user sample-read. The user signs off by creating `.canary-approved` at the repo root. Until that file exists the orchestrator may not drain further. Voice fidelity and AI-ism suppression are load-bearing on the first pass — there is no margin for a second attempt before user signoff.
- **Detective Ford's executions** at the end of scene-03 must remain on-page. The source has Ford ordering the captives killed because the raid is unsanctioned; the moral register of the city is established here in a single beat. Do not soften, do not summarise.
- **No flashback to the dead child yet.** That beat is held for chapter 02 (its native source position).
- **No naming of Marissa.** She belongs to Greg in chapter 02, not here. Greg himself is not in this chapter — the source introduces him in source-ch.2.
- **Kendall named in reference only.** The Powerstone on her gorget and the repeating revolver are his work; the source establishes both as gifts. Kendall appears on-page in chapter 02.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 7–9k words. The breach is the longest sustained set piece in the book outside the Dragonkin invasion and the cliff-side battle in source-ch.15.
- **Lexicon discipline.** Skyland trade vocabulary: "Powerstone", "gauntlet", "gorget", "corset" (the manifested armour), "Skyway", "Barrier", "Great Geyser", "Stardust", "manufactory", "merc", "boss", "shift", "the cleanup". When the prose reaches for "intricate" or "delve", substitute with one of these or with a verb.
- **Closing rhythm.** Scene-04 should end on one short observation Kyra would not normally allow herself. The source uses "I went to bed" as its closer; the rewrite earns something stricter — a specific physical fact about the apartment, the cold water of the tub, the empty dispenser on the wall. The reader leaves chapter 01 with the magic-shortage already a fact, not a thesis.
