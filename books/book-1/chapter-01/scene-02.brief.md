## What the arch is

The arch is the boundary between the cooking floor (the cartel's public-facing manufactory) and the **inner sanctum** (the cartel's high-security operation). It is sealed by a projector-warded shield — a magical barrier emitted by a Powerstone-fed projector embedded in the arch frame. **The shield-breaker cracks the projector permanently**: it is a one-shot device that destroys the emitter along with the shield. Kendall gave Kyra the device with the warning to save it for projectors worth the cost. The cartel's inner sanctum qualifies.

When scene-02 opens, **the shield-breaker has just discharged** — the projector is dying, the shield is collapsing, and Kyra is about to step through.

## What is beyond the arch

The inner sanctum holds:
- **The bayonet team**: 4–6 men with Skylander Guard-issue smoothbores and fixed bayonets. These are off-duty Guard moonlighting at cartel pay — professional soldiers, not the cheap labour Kyra has dealt with so far. They have positioned themselves with **cartel workers as cover** (human shields, deliberately).
- **More cooking workers**: 4–8 women near-naked at inner rigs, same cartel policy as the cooking floor.
- **The illegal ley-line tap**: a fattened, visibly-jury-rigged ley-line trunk piped into the room, feeding the rigs, the lighting, and the projector that was just cracked. This is the cartel's actual mana source — a clandestine tap on the public ley-line network. (Per `codex/magic/system.md` "Skylands mana economy", the city's mana is rationed; the cartels are one of the principal mana-thieves contributing to the shortage. Ford's "greater good" framing is implicit in the raid's targeting.)
- **A way up**: stairs, ladder, or gantry to an upper level. Some way to reach the upper window.

The scene ends with Kyra at an **upper-level open window** looking down at Ford in the courtyard below. Ford's men have the surviving captives (workers who fled or hid) and the executions are beginning or about to begin.

### Material absences (no nature, no paper, no wood, etc.)
Per `codex/locations/the-skylands.md` and `polish-targets §25`:
- No nature metaphors: no "clever as a fox", no "sharp-eyed as an eagle", no "rooted to the spot", no "blooming", no "branching", no "dog-leg".
- No paper references: no "wallpaper", no "paper-thin", no "newspapers", no "leaf through", no "scrolling".
- No wood: no "wooden door" (crystal), no "wooden expression", no "beams", no "stiff as a board".
- No leather-from-animals: "leather" on the Skylands is human leather — do not euphemise.
- No plant/animal textiles: no wool, cotton, linen, silk, yarn-spinning.
- No birds inside the Barrier: no "bird's-eye view", no "free as a bird", no "ruffled her feathers".
- No unfiltered sun: no "felt the sun on her face" (Barrier-light only).

Substitute with materials Kyra knows: crystal (indigo, violet, dark-crystal), fungus, brass, gauze, gut, ore, fume, glow-globe, Skyway, Geyser, Bowels, scaffolding.
