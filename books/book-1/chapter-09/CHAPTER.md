# Book 1, Chapter 09 — The Cuffs and the Stream

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.11 (Kyra waking in the Bizhjerai tent, the cloth, the manacles, "Red" introduced as the nickname, the first food, the milk, the porridge) and source-book-1 / ch.12 (the bath at the steep-banked canal, the language exchange — leather, wool, buffalo, the Bizhjerai Clan tattoo, the magesense lesson, the dressing for the trial).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Kyra wakes captive in a Bizhjerai tent: collared, cuffed, ribs stitched neatly by a shaman. The redheaded woman from the path is at her side with a damp cloth. Kyra nicknames her "Red" because her real name has not been given. They talk past each other for hours — Kyra trades information about the Skylands for porridge and milk, Red trades information about leather, wool, buffalo, woodsmoke, and Clan custom. The Chief is due in the morning; Red brings Kyra to a steep-banked canal to bathe before the trial. They strip together, they look at each other (the source's body-noticing is preserved as character; the rewrite holds it to one beat per scene). Red dries Kyra's hair with magic Kyra has never seen used that way. Kyra tries the spell herself; her cuffs blaze and drop her with a current of pain. Red helps her into a too-small grey wool dress. The chapter ends with Kyra walking to her own trial, knowing she has no weapons, no magic, and exactly one possible advocate. External goal: survive the interrogation, learn the rules, secure an ally. Internal goal: discover that the spear-bearer of yesterday is a person.

## Opening hook
A damp cloth on Kyra's face. Sunlight through tent canvas. Above her, a face she cannot reconcile with the face she saw yesterday at spearpoint. The cloth pauses. The face hardens. A knife at Kyra's throat in the moment her eyes open. Kyra does not move.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra in the too-small grey wool dress with her boobs straining the laces and her hands bound in shining bronze cuffs. The cuffs glow faintly. Red ahead, walking. Two male guards behind. The Chief's pavilion in view. The reader knows Kyra's tits will not buy her this one.

## Writer notes
- **Anjali idiolect (still "Red" through this chapter).** Twenty-five years old. Bizhjerai-raised. Short sentences. Imperative voice when on duty ("Don't move", "Eat"). Direct curiosity beneath the warrior mask — when she relaxes, she asks questions plainly. Sharp tongue when stressed. Voice anchors come from the source's bath scene: "I bathed earlier this week" is exactly right. She does not soften or charm; the playful moments arrive on her own terms.
- **Kyra calling her "Red".** The nickname is born of mistaken identity (chapter 08) but Kyra never tells Red the origin. The rewrite preserves this — Kyra uses the nickname as a private joke that masks a wound. Red accepts it because the alternative is being called "captive's keeper".
- **The Chief — Anjali's father — is not on-page in this chapter.** Mentioned. His return is the deadline. The rewrite holds him off until chapter 10.
- **The dead Mage / dead child flashback does NOT recur here.** The source has Kyra register the mistaken-identity moment again in the bath; the rewrite has used the flashback's last allowed Book-1 appearance in chapter 08 and does not echo it here. Trust the reader.
- **Alissa is mentioned by Kyra once.** Briefly. The chapter is otherwise about Red and the city Kyra is now in.
- **The information-exchange scenes** (porridge, the bath dialog, the dressing) are the source's most lecture-prone passages. The rewrite breaks the exchange into action: Red feeds, Kyra eats, they speak in fragments rather than paragraphs. No "I'm with you so far" beats.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 8–10k words. Scene-03 (the bath) is the chapter's centre of gravity but must not run long; the dialog should pivot frequently between the two women's surprises.
- **The shaman is name-dropped (he stitched her ribs).** Not on-page yet. The reader registers the existence of a magical Clan-figure who can heal and "speak to the Elements". The rewrite does not commit yet to the shaman's later appearance.
