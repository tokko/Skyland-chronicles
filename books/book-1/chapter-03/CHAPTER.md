# Book 1, Chapter 03 — Mama June's

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.2 (the second source-numbered ch.2 — the Kendall favour and the brothel infiltration) and source-book-1 / ch.3 (the surveillance of the house and the killing of Markus and his Mage lover).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Kendall arrives uninvited the morning after the dead child, asking Kyra to kill a man-whore who has the leverage to ruin him. She accepts because Kendall is her oldest friend and because the job is something she can finish. Over the following days she infiltrates Mama June's, becomes invisible by becoming familiar, kills a violent client to earn the inner circle, finds Markus's ledger page, hunts him to a fashionable house in the upper district and finds him in bed with a man who turns out to be a Mage. Markus dies; the Mage dies; Kyra walks home through the dark city as the Guard sirens approach. Kendall is waiting in her apartment. They cry together — Kendall for the dead Mage, Kyra for the small body with red hair she cannot put down. External goal: find and kill Markus before his deadline expires. Internal goal: stay inside one professional skin for long enough that the small body cannot find her.

## Beats this chapter advances
- H-kyra-merc-life — Kyra's working life under pressure; the underclass-protector framing tightened (Mama June, the working girls, the bouncers); the city's law-enforcement-as-collateral logic.
- H-the-magic-shortages — passing reference: the dispenser is back, the water is off; Kendall as the genius who keeps Kyra's gear alive in the supply crunch.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- _none_ — this is the bridge between the chapter-01 establishment and the chapter-04 invasion. No hard beat lands here; the chapter is the engine that maintains H-kyra-merc-life and earns the Kendall relationship that will outlast the book.

## Opening hook
Kendall stepping into Kyra's apartment in his blue three-piece suit, the morning after the dead child, knowing what shape Kyra is in and asking anyway. The cane-tip under her chin. His blue eyes never on her body.

## Closing hand-off
Kendall and Kyra falling asleep on her bed, Kendall's face in her bosom. Kyra's tears land on the back of his head — not for the Mage. She does not tell him that. The reader knows.

## Voice / polish-targets specific to this chapter

## Writer notes
- **Kendall idiolect.** Tall, blond, gay, dapper, blue-tinted spectacles, an indigo crystal-headed cane. Voiced as the city's least-likely-to-survive-the-undercity figure who has stayed alive by being smarter than anyone except Kyra. Cleaner grammar than Kyra. Self-mockery rather than sarcasm. Diminishes Kyra ("you look like shit") only as a form of affection. Never lecherous — Kyra's body is invisible to him and the voice notes this with quiet relief.
- **Mama June idiolect.** Brisk, transactional, kindness as policy. Sentences clipped at the practical end. Speaks to working girls as employees, not as victims; the working girls' code of post-vase armament is her doing.
- **Markus.** Voiced briefly. The source has him scream, plead, and die in three lines. Keep the death short. Do not over-elaborate his beauty; the source emphasises it via Kendall, not via Kyra. Kyra's eye notes professional things — the scar on the shoulder confirming identity — not the aesthetic.
- **The Mage.** Voiceless in the source. Holds shape that way in the rewrite. He is the man who made Kyra a Mage-killer; he should not be a character.
- **Markus's lover is a Mage who tried to defend his bed.** Kyra knows this in the moment and the chapter does not duck the cost. "It pained me to kill an innocent, possibly another victim of Markus' but I had no choice" — the source line carries the moral weight; the rewrite keeps the cost on the page without inflating it.
- **The Kyra-Raynard sex beat in the alley** is the second of the book's Raynard encounters and the source's worst case of post-battle sex as default break. The rewrite breaks the pattern by treating it as **transactional rather than appetitive** — Kyra makes him come to shut him up and end his line of leverage, then spits it back into his hand. The chapter avoids any narration that frames this as desire fulfilled.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 8–10k words. Scenes 2 and 3 are roughly equal in length.
- **No flashback to the dead child** in narration. The closing scene's tears do the work without naming.
