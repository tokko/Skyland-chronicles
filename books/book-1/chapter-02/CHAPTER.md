# Book 1, Chapter 02 — The Small Body in the Dark

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.2 (the first chapter-2 in the source numbering — the Greg-and-Ghost escort job and the dead child). The second source-numbered ch.2 (Mama June's, the Markus assignment, the brothel infiltration) belongs to chapter 03 of the rewrite.

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Kyra wakes the morning after the Stardust raid into the small daylight reality of a failing apartment: the dispenser dead, the cool water cut, the bills on the floor. She meets Greg at a bar in the undercity for a contract from a clueless rich man — "Ghost" — escorting a Stardust deal. The deal completes. The robbery on the way back does not. The runner who snatches the bag is a thirteen-year-old girl with red hair, and the explosive round Kyra fires into the dark dismembers her. Kyra stomps her dead to end it. External goal: complete the contract, get paid, get home. Internal goal: discover the floor of her own profession, and refuse to admit she has fallen through it.

## Beats this chapter advances
- H-the-magic-shortages — apartment dispenser failure; the "Robin Hoods" drainage rumours; the Justiciary's tacit corruption; the rates rising as the supply falls.
- S-kyra-and-greg — Greg named, voiced, located. His daughter Marissa named (Greg's "Marissa would like to see you"). The friendship is on the page.

## Beats this chapter LANDS
- H-kyra-kills-a-child — full land, with the chapter's title-beat. The flashback to the small body is held for two later, deliberate placements (chapter 05's hospital room and chapter 08's mistaken-identity at spearpoint); no further on-page recurrence within this chapter.
- S-kyra-and-greg — full land. Greg voiced on-page; Marissa named in his mouth; the relationship established as load-bearing for Greg's death in chapter 04 to matter. The "she admires you, you know" beat lands here; Greg's love for his daughter is established by his mouth, not by Kyra's narration.

## Opening hook
The dispenser in the corner of Kyra's apartment, dark. Her revolvers laid out on the rug. Her hands shaking very slightly as she reloads — not from cold; she has paid her bill — and the rumours about the Robin Hoods running in the back of her head.

## Closing hand-off
Kyra at the Skyway platform, Greg squeezing her hand as the doors close. The tram pulls away. She collapses on the floor of the carriage. The reader does not need to be told that the next morning will not fix what just happened.

## Scene count (preliminary)
4 scenes.
- scene-01 — *The dead dispenser.* Kyra reloading on the rug, the magic outage, the Robin Hood rumours, the fungus sandwich. Half a flashback to her father's execution, named once, not dramatised.
- scene-02 — *Greg at the bar.* The meet in the undercity tavern; Greg's voice; the Ghost contract.
- scene-03 — *The deal.* The Stardust transaction in the cave-junction; the ambush on the way back; Greg holding two attackers; Kyra running down the corridor after the runner with the bag.
- scene-04 — *What Kyra finds.* The detonation in the dark, the trail to the small body, the stomp, the Skyway ride home.

## Voice / polish-targets specific to this chapter
- **Anti-anchors to suppress.**

  - `polish-targets §15` — post-battle sex as default. No partner appears in this chapter. The cool-down beat (the Skyway carriage) replaces the source's tendency to use sex as the post-action register-shift.
  - `voice §Kyra anti-anchor 2` — Kyra over-explaining grief immediately after the act. The voice goes physical: dripping fingers, the wet sucking sound of pulling the boot back, the bruise of crystal floor against her knees. Not interpretation.

  - `voice §Kyra anchor 2` — the source's strongest passage. The unselfconscious noticing of her own tears; turning from Greg in shame once she noticed; Greg wise enough not to say anything. **Preserve the rhythm of late-arriving noticing.** It is the chapter's load-bearing voice anchor.

## Writer notes
- **The stomp must remain on-page.** Not summarised, not off-screened. The chapter only works if the act is rendered specifically. The boot. The crunch. The wet sucking sound of pulling it free. The red hair. The dead one-intact eye.
- **The flashback to the dead child does not begin in this chapter.** The first flashback appearance in the trilogy is held for chapter 05 (the hospital room, when Kyra learns Greg is dead) and the second for chapter 08 (the spearpoint encounter with Anjali). Source has eight; the rewrite has two.
- **Greg is voiced from his first line.** His blue eyes, the curved short sword, the impeccable manners for a mercenary, the genuine fondness for his daughter. He must sound different from Kyra in the dialog. Idiolect targets: shorter sentences, fewer hedges, the courteous-mercenary register.
- **Marissa is named once, in Greg's mouth, in passing.** She is not on-page. The reader notes the name; the chapter does not gloss it.
- **The Robin Hoods are rumour, not fact.** Treated as a working hypothesis Kyra holds with appropriate caution. The bodies-drained-of-magic detail belongs here, in passing.
- **"Ghost" is a comic figure, not a noir antagonist.** His ostentation is the joke. The brutal collision between his upper-city self-image and the actual transactional rules of the undercity is part of the city-mechanic the chapter establishes.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 7–9k words. Scene-04 (the dead child and the ride home) is the chapter's emotional centre and should be the longest single sustained passage, but not the longest paragraph.
- **The Skyway closer.** Greg's "Don't let this bring you down. Remember, you didn't know." is the source's line. The rewrite earns or replaces it. If kept, the placement is at the doors closing — not in narration after.
