# Book 1 — Stardust

## Spine

Kyra Zorantis is a Skylander mercenary working off-the-books raids for an honest cop in a city that no longer has enough magic to run itself. She kills a child by accident on a Stardust job and finds out that grief does not stop her from taking the next contract. When the Skylands are invaded across the Barrier for the first time in living memory, her best friend Greg dies saving her and she becomes mother to a girl she did not bear. The Skylands offer money to anyone willing to cross the Barrier and see what is on the other side; Kyra volunteers — partly to deserve the orphan she is now raising, partly because she cannot stay in the rooms Greg used to fill. She is captured by a Bizhjerai war-band led by a red-haired woman whose face triggers the same wound she has been pretending to recover from. By book's end Kyra is fighting alongside her former captors against another Clan, and she has begun to suspect she is going to live here a long time.

## Opening image

Kyra and Anton pressed to opposite walls of a dark indigo-crystal alley, two fingers held up, waiting on the signal. The detonation comes; she puts a revolver round through the back of a guard's skull before either guard has turned. The first sentence is action; the first paragraph is professional. The reader knows what this woman does for a living before they learn her name.

## Closing image

Kyra walking out of Bizhjerai pavilion in the dark with the smell of horse and wood-smoke on her, the Bizhjerai Mark not yet on her shoulder but already a name she has earned from Anjali — a pet name for the murdered child she keeps mistaking for her captor, kept now for the captor who is no longer holding her at spear-point. The Outcast raid is behind her; the dragons and the gem and the war are ahead. She does not yet know she has been adopted; she suspects she is about to be.

## Theme

What a mercenary owes the next stranger when the city that pays her has stopped paying for itself.

## Target word count

105–110k. The book is the trilogy's canary and carries the full weight of establishing voice, city, magic system, and the orphan-Marissa connection that anchors arc II. Pulled toward the upper end because the Skylands must be a place the reader can lose, not a setting they tour. The opening fight, the dragon invasion, the Bizhjerai capture, and the Outcast raid are each long enough to demand breath; the book is not asked to push past 110k.

## What's not in this book (leapt)

- Kyra's father's execution (the political reason her mother lives in the Undercity) — referenced in dialogue / interior monologue only, never dramatised on-page.
- Kyra's time in the Skylander Guard under Micah — referenced only when relevant to current judgements; the burning of Micah is decades away.
- The full politics of the Skylands' Council — the Skylands is a city, not a polity, in this book. The reader meets it from below.
- The Bizhjerai's interior life as a Clan — the second half of the book is from inside the camp but Kyra is captive; the deep cultural reveal is held for Book 2 when she belongs.
