# Book 1, Chapter 08 — The Bear and the Spear

## Source coverage
source-book-1 / ch.10 (the bear attack — cute cub in the bushes, the charge of the mother bear, Kyra thrown against the tree, the wound, Alissa fleeing) and source-book-1 / ch.11 (Kyra alone with the carcass, the cub she has to put down, the days following alone, the meeting with the foraging woman, the chase, the spear-handed redhead at the end of the path).

## POV
Kyra Zorantis (first person, retrospective past tense, close — see voice §Kyra)

## Spine
Days into the forest. A bear cub in the underbrush. Alissa's "we can't kill it, it's a baby". The mother bear's roar. Kyra's single explosive round shot blindly. The bear's charge bowls her through the air; her ribs are opened to bone by claws. She wakes alone, follows the trail of blood, finds the dead bear and the orphaned cub, and shoots it because she cannot afford to leave it at her back. She walks for two days, concussed, eating raw bear flesh because she cannot use magic, looking for Alissa, finding nothing. On the third day she follows the sound of a woman's singing through the trees. A young woman in a grey dress runs from her. Kyra chases. At the end of the path a second woman is in a combat stance with a spear — red hair lit from above, green eyes, copper freckles. The dead child's face overlays the spear-woman's for half a second. Kyra cannot get her rifle up in time. The butt of the spear hits her temple. External goal: find Alissa, get to the rendezvous, do not die alone in the woods. Internal goal: discover that the small body she put down in the dark has been waiting for her in every wood she has ever walked.

## Closing hand-off

## Writer notes
- **The bear-and-cub structure mirrors the dead-girl structure.** The cub is at the wrong end of a child Kyra cannot save; her killing it is professional and grief-flavoured. The rewrite preserves the parallel as a felt shape, not a stated one. Kyra does not narrate the parallel; the reader sees it.
- **Alissa's fate.** Open in this chapter, as in the source. The reader does not know whether she lived. The codex INDEX shows Alissa alive — she returns in book-2 as the Bizhjerai shadow operative "Alana Kelvari". The rewrite preserves the ambiguity in Book 1: Kyra's last sight of her was the bear closing.
- **Anjali's name is not in dialog yet.** Kyra calls her "Red" privately for the first time in chapter 09. In this chapter the spear-bearer is voiceless — she strikes Kyra unconscious on sight and the introduction proper waits.
- **The foraging woman's name (Jalani)** is heard once, in passing, in chapter 09. Not in this chapter.
- **The wordless singing** must be specifically musical, not "ethereal". Anjali was singing because she was alone with Jalani at the foraging site; the song is a Bizhjerai pastoral tune. The rewrite does not over-render it but treats it as the moment Kyra has heard something unrehearsed for the first time in years.
- **Two-days-alone compression.** Scene-03 is a single scene by length but covers two days of stumbling. Compress aggressively. The reader needs to feel the duration of Kyra's solitude; the chapter doesn't need to inhabit each meal.
- **The mistaken-identity flashback is the chapter's load-bearing voice beat.** The girl from chapter 02 should arrive in the same physical specifics: red hair, one intact green eye, the heel print. The flashback closes the loop on H-kyra-kills-a-child and opens H-meet-red in the same instant.
- **Pacing target.** Roughly 7–9k words. Scene-04 (the meeting with Anjali) is brief but the chapter's emotional centre.
