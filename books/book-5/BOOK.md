# Book 5 — Crucible

## Spine

The book opens with Marissa back from her confrontation with Kendall and the team noticing. Within a few chapters Marissa and Valia have kissed, Zandor has walked in, and the three of them have tipped into a polyamorous configuration that is both real love and operational protection. Alissa arrives in alias as Alana Kelvari and Marissa now has two hands — one visible, one not. Marissa works out that the shade is Kyra-in-the-gem and that the dagger on her hip has been her mother all along. Mother tells her the parable about the warlord; Marissa accepts it. Marissa rides south with Zandor and fails to trade with the farmers; Micah orders the farms taken; the farmers are murdered and Marissa's last hesitation breaks. Valia floats a question at the dam that will, decades later, become Epitome. Then the Prowler descends. Mathias Varl steps out and declares he has never seen Marissa before in his life. Marissa is arrested, tried, ordered to shoot her own surrogate aunt to prove her loyalty, and dropped in a box that drains the magic out of her until she breaks her nose against its wall. Zandor and Valia carry her home.

## Opening image

A workshop at Groundlands camp. Marissa is bent over a schematic, half-asleep. A blob of cold tea against her elbow. Valia in the doorway, not entering, watching her. The summoning-crystal in Marissa's coat pocket. The first line is Valia's — soft, sideways, voice-distinctive, a question that does not ask the question.

## Closing image

A wooden cot in Zandor's quarters. Marissa's nose splinted. The pads of her fingers bandaged where the nails were. Zandor in a chair he has not slept in. Valia at the door bringing soup that no one has touched. Marissa's eyes open, finding the lamp first, then the dagger laid on the table by the cot. The shade is not on-page. The dagger is gem-side-up. The reader feels the absence; Marissa feels the dagger.

## Theme

What loyalty looks like when the people who love you have been allowed to know who you are, and the people who would kill you have been allowed to find out.

## Voice

Marissa Zorantis, book-2 register. The voice runs longer here than in Book 4 — domestic chapters; engineering interludes; the shade-conversations are quieter and more thought-shaped than action-shaped. The bawdy register has its peak across the threesome chapters. The cadence under stress (the box) fragments hardest in this book. See `codex/voice/voice-profile.md` §Marissa cadence and §Marissa anchors 3 (the box) and 4 (Valia's clean run of sentences).

## Target word count

100–110k. Sits at the upper end of the band. The book carries the middle act's full weight — the relationships that pay off in Book 6, the magic-system distribution that has to land *here* rather than concentrated, and the trial-box sequence that breaks Marissa for the climax. The expansion budget is real; the source over-stuffs the middle and the rewrite must do the same load at lower density per page.

## What's not in this book (leapt)

- The full backstory of Alissa with Kyra — referenced only in dialogue between Marissa and Alissa; the reader does not get a flashback. The relationship is voice-rendered.
- The savages' intelligence — the egg chambers, the wooden ramps, the coordinated assault. Held for Book 6.
- The fleet's construction — Kendall's bombardment cannon, the Bizhjerai ships. Marissa knows what the summoning-crystal does but the reader does not see the fleet until Book 6.
- Most of Marissa's plan — the four-part sabotage. She thinks in fragments here; the plan crystallises in Book 6.
- The full mother-shade absolution after the Alissa-killing — held for Book 6's opening beat (H-mother-on-killing-alissa) so that the chapter break is the grief and the next book opens with restoration.

## Hooks into the next book

- Mother absolving Marissa — the first scene of Book 6 (H-mother-on-killing-alissa is by source-chapter Book 5 territory at ch.21 — held over to Book 6 because the absolution restores what the box destroyed and Book 6's spine is *the plan executed*).
- The poison Alissa hinted at — Marissa knows it is there; she does not yet know where. Found in Book 6.
- The Prowler — looming over the camp at book's close. Brought down by Kendall in Book 6.
- Mathias Varl — alive, knowing Marissa's claim was false. The reader and Marissa both think he must be killed. He survives the book — and survives the trilogy off-page.
- Zandor's not-knowing. The reader (post-Book 6) will know that Zandor knew. Book 6 reveals; Book 5 sets up by having Zandor be visibly *fine* with Marissa in a way that should be reread as a tell.
- Valia's not-knowing. Same. The cliff in Book 6 reveals.
- Meta-magic — the seed of Epitome. Marissa is already prototyping by book's close.
