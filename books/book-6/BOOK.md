# Book 6 — Genocide

## Spine

Marissa wakes in Zandor's quarters with her nose splinted and her mother's voice in the dagger telling her she did the right thing. A red flare rises over the northern tower and the team rides out — into a trap. Felaan dies covering Wylah; the savages drive the rest into the caves where the wooden ramps and the rows of nested eggs prove what kind of enemy the camp has been fighting. Valia falls from a cliff with Marissa in her arms, breaks free of her grip on the way down so that one of them lives, and dies revealing that she has known who Marissa is for months. Marissa walks home and executes the four-part sabotage she has been carrying behind her eyes since the box. Poison in the water. Bombs in the magazines. A signal to Kendall's fleet. The shield tower destroyed from inside. Zandor at the tower door — having known too — shot through the thigh; not killed. Micah charging her with a sword; killed. Kendall's flagship destroys the Prowler. The Bizhjerai sweep the ruins. Ten thousand dead. Marissa, walking among them, names herself: I am the last Skylander. By the epilogue she is at Dun Traelak. The dagger sits over the fount where Malkareth hatched. Kendall presses a tome into her hand and tells her the savages were once people. She rides south alone to study them.

## Opening image

Marissa's eyes opening on the cot in Zandor's quarters. The lamp first, then the dagger, then — finally — her mother in the corner of the room. The shade is not quite there; the magic field is too thin. But she is there enough. Marissa does not speak. Mother does. The first line of the book is the absolution.

## Closing image

A fount at Dun Traelak. Steam rising. The dagger laid on the lip of the basin, gem-side down. Marissa's hand on the stone, not picking it up. Kendall behind her in the dawn, the tome under his arm. The Bizhjerai-Rune disguise has been retired; the Mark on her shoulder is visible in the cold. A horse waits at the gate. The shade is silent because the dagger is on the stone. The next book opens in the ruins.

## Theme

What it costs to be right.

## Target word count

105–115k. The book is dense by design (16 hard beats), and the trim pass has to deliver each beat without the source's heaviest decoration. The expansion budget goes to Valia's revelation scene, to the genocide sweep (which must land at scale without becoming a list of named deaths), and to the epilogue at Dun Traelak (which carries Arc II's emotional close and sets up Arc III's project).

## What's not in this book (leapt)

- Most of the Skylander camp's internal politics during the assault. The fleet arrives; the shield falls; the city dies. The chain of command between Micah's death and Kendall's sweep is not staged.
- The exact mechanism of Zandor's escape — the missing bike is the beat; the route south is not.
- Mathias Varl's escape from the Prowler. The reader will learn in Book 8 that he survived; this book leaves it ambiguous (Marissa thinks he is dead; Kendall is not certain).
- The first months of Marissa's southern study — held for Book 7's open.
- The Collegium's founding — the school exists by Book 7; the years of building it are leapt at the open of Book 7.
- The full content of the precursor-tome. Kendall hands it over; Marissa accepts it; the reader sees the cover. Book 7 reveals what it contains.

## Hooks into the next book

- The precursor-tome — handed off in the epilogue, opened in Book 7.
- Zandor's missing bike — the seed for Book 8's biggest reveal. The reader has now seen the bike-rack and knows it is gone.
- Marissa heading south alone — the geography of Arc III is the valley above the ruins.
- The dagger on the fount — Marissa nearly releases the shade. She does not. The relationship continues.
- "I'm the last Skylander" — the line the trilogy's third arc will spend nine more books contesting and confirming. Marissa names it; Arc III tests it.
