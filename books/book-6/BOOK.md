# Book 6 — The Sacrifice

## Spine

Marissa wakes in Zandor's quarters with her nose splinted and her mother's voice in the dagger telling her she did the right thing — Alissa was already dead the moment she was caught. A red flare rises over the northern tower and the team rides out into a trap. Felaan dies covering Wylah; the savages drive the rest into the caves where the wooden ramps and the rows of nested eggs prove what kind of enemy the camp has been fighting. Cornered on a high overlook, Valia kisses Marissa, reveals she has known her identity for months and loved her anyway, falls with her from the cliff, pours all her magical power into Marissa on the way down, and breaks out of Marissa's grip so that one of them lives. Back at the Groundlands Marissa finds the poison Alissa hid behind her workshop wall, kills the man tailing her, and plumbs the poison into the camp's water main. She rigs explosive power cores into the central munitions caches. Days later thirty thousand savages assault the wall in an ordered horde. The wall holds. Marissa fights at it. By the close of the book the camp is bedridden from the poisoned water, the magazines are wired, the fleet-summoning crystal is hot, and Marissa is dressed and watching the eastern horizon. The plan is complete. The trigger is not yet pulled.

## Opening image

Marissa's eyes opening on the cot in Zandor's quarters. The lamp first, then the dagger on the bedside table, then — finally — her mother in the corner of the room. The shade is not quite there; the magic field is too thin and Marissa is half-broken. But she is there enough. Marissa does not speak. Mother does. The first line of the book is the absolution.

## Closing image

The eastern horizon at dusk. Pyres of thirty thousand savages still burning outside the wall, grease-smoke hanging low over the camp. The Groundlands quiet from the poisoned water. Marissa standing at her workshop window in her Angel armour, the magazines wired beneath the central towers, the summoning-crystal warm in her palm. She is waiting. The next book opens on the night sky lighting up.

## Theme

What it costs to plan the killing of a city, with the people you have loved still living in it.

## What's not in this book (leapt)

- The full anatomy of the savage cave system beyond the egg chamber. The chamber is shown; the rest is implied.
- Most of Zandor's interior in this book — his confrontation at the tower door is held for Book 7. The reader has known something is wrong with how steady he has been; the reveal waits.
- The Bizhjerai fleet's approach. The summoning-crystal is hot at this book's close; the fleet appears in Book 7's open.
- Wylah's grief at Felaan's death beyond the immediate scene. She survives the cave; her later mourning is held for Book 7's quieter beats.
- The exact contents of Marissa's four-part sabotage plan as a list. The reader watches her execute parts as they happen; the catalogue never lands as a monologue.

## Hooks into the next book

- The summoning-crystal — hot and unused at the book's close. Book 7 opens on the fleet's arrival.
- The shield tower — wired and waiting. Book 7 triggers the blast.

- Valia's gift — Marissa carries all of Valia's magical power forward into Book 7. The book that triggers the genocide is fuelled by the woman who loved her anyway.
